    Incident satisfaction survey - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-19Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)User guide: Tell IT how it went

    The applications NEW

    ScopeWhat it gives
    global (Assessments and Surveys)the trigger condition, its business rule, the survey and its questions, the Incident Survey email E01 E02 E04 E08
    sn_assessment_coreAssessmentCreation, which creates the survey instance; not in the mirror E02

    The census lists only the business rule (Auto assessment business rule, global) for this feature: the trigger condition, the survey and its email are on tables outside the process.

    1. The trigger NEW

    1. Auto assessment business rule runs async after an insert or update of an incident whose state changes to 7 (Closed). E02 E03
    2. It passes the trigger condition ITSM Demo Survey to AssessmentCreation.conditionTrigger. E02 Q03
    3. The trigger's full condition adds that the incident has no universal request. The survey goes to caller_id, always (probability 100%), at most once per caller per 30 days (repeat interval). E01

    Steps 2 and 3 are done by sn_assessment_core, which isn't in the mirror: the repeat interval's exact effect is read from the record, not from code. Q67

    2. The survey NEW

    Short Customer Satisfaction Survey using Smiley Face: published, on demand, 14 days to answer, no retake, not anonymous, not public. E04

    OrderQuestionTypeMandatory
    100How satisfied are you with the service provided by IT?smiley scale 1-5 E05 E06no
    100Tell us what you think about IT and what you want or need.multi-line text E07no

    Neither question is scored. E05 E07

    3. The invitation NEW

    Incident Survey listens to record.send_survey on asmt_assessment_instance when the instance's task is an incident; it sends to parm1 with the subject "Feedback makes us better". E08 Its link is the survey URL passed in parm2, labelled Take the survey. E09 The email says "your recent request", though it is about an incident. Q14 Requests have their own Request survey on the same event. E10

    The reminder and overdue notifications filter on the evaluation methods assessment, quiz, attestation or test plan, so none reminds a caller about this survey. E11 E04 Q33

    4. Where it is answered NEW

    Email redirection is on and Employee Center is the default portal, so the link opens esc?id=take_survey&instance_id=…. E03 E12 E13 E14 A caller can also find open surveys on the portal page My Assessments and Surveys E15, or in the platform under Self-Service › My Assessments & Surveys E16.

    5. Where results are seen NEW

    ModuleShowsRoles

    Survey › Survey Responseseach answer (asmt_metric_result) E18survey_admin, survey_reader, survey_creator
    Survey › Trigger Conditionsthe trigger E19survey_admin, survey_creator

    The survey's own roles are survey_admin and survey_reader. E04 The incident has no survey related list in Service Operations Workspace, so agents don't see the answers on the incident. E20 Q34

    Open questions OPEN QUESTION

    • Q03 Should the satisfaction survey, whose trigger is named ITSM Demo Survey, keep running on this instance, and who reads its results? Decision, for the process owner
    • Q67 Does the survey engine send the survey only once per 30 days per caller, as the repeat interval suggests? Value not in the mirror, for the platform team
    • Q14 Should the survey email speak of the incident instead of "your recent request"? Decision, for the process owner
    • Q33 Should callers get a reminder when they haven't answered the survey? Decision, for the process owner
    • Q34 Should resolvers or team leads see the survey answers for their incidents? Decision, for the process owner
