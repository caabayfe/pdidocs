    1. Record templates NEW

    Where they appear. The SOW side panel route Templates opens Template SNC on almost every table, with no role audience. E01 E02 The census claim sn_itsm_workspace:Templates (and its global twin) is the Agent Workspace side panel action, shown while glide.ui.show_template_bar.<table> is not false; pdi2 sets no such property for incident. E04 E05

    Who sees which template (in execution order):

    1. SNC Template Query narrows every interactive query to global, own and my groups' templates, unless the user has template_editor_global. E06
    2. Read ACLs: global or own (and the template's roles or groups) E07; all for template_editor_global E08; group templates for template_editor_group E09.
    3. Create: template_editor, template_editor_global or template_editor_group. E10 itil and sn_incident_write contain template_editor. E11 E12

    The SOW panel fetches up to 100 Form-channel templates with their evaluated body E25 through ResponseTemplateSOW E27; no extension overrides the platform implementation. E28 The panel copies the message as plain text; it doesn't write to the incident. E29

    Who can read and write

    • Read: role template_snippet_reader, then template_snippet_writer or the template's visibility groups (empty means everyone). E32
    • Configure: Response Template Configuration, role template_snippet_writer. E34
    • Roles: reader ⊂ writer ⊂ template_snippet_admin ⊂ admin. No ITSM role contains the reader, so on pdi2 only administrators see response templates. E33 shared/Q02

    Variables. ${field} (dot-walking allowed, such as ${caller_id.name}), ${Date} and ${current_user}; a field the agent can't read, or an unknown name, shows in red. E36 A template with unknown or unreadable variables can't be saved. E35

    3. Compose Email and email templates NEW

    1. Action: Compose Email is in the incident's overflow menu (sn_sow_inc) and in the global layout, both pointing at the UI action compose_email_workspace. E42
    2. Condition: saved record, write access, the table's email_client attribute, and rights to the email client processor. E43 Incident has email_client=true. E44
    3. Roles: itil and sn_incident_write contain email_composer, which contains email_client_template_read. E45
    4. Configuration: no incident-specific email client configuration; the Default one applies. E46
    5. Templates: the Email template panel appears where the email client is enabled and searches sys_email_client_template. E47 E48 There are none for incident: the existing ones serve incident communication tasks, communication tasks, change requests, email replies and surveys. E48 Q36
    6. Quick actions in the email body: r response template, e email template, d draft. E49
