    Communication plans - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-18Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)Operator guide: Communicate about an incident

    This page covers communication plans on any incident. The major incident pages (Communicate) cover the same records from the major incident side and list the email templates.

    1. Plans by definition NEW

    The async rule Attach Communication plans runs on every task save but acts only when an active, condition-based plan definition exists for the table. E01 E02 There is none for incidents E03, so no plan attaches by itself, on an ordinary or a major incident.

    2. Who is offered the action NEW

    Open new communication modal is assigned to the incident table in the workspace, for any active incident: it has no major incident condition. E04 It shows as Create new communication in the header's overflow menu E05 E06, for users with itil (or sn_incident_read) and ia_admin. E04 Q05

    ia_admin is the communication plan admin role E07; it contains comm_plan_manager E12, which the create ACLs on plans and tasks require. E13 E14 Reading plans needs comm_plan_viewer or above. E15

    3. Creating the plan and task NEW

    The dialog posts to the adhoc_comm_plan operation, which needs a short description and a frequency and passes the incident to the handler for its table E17: for incident, plans go to incident_alert and tasks to incident_alert_task. E18 The handler refuses users without create rights, then sets the plan's short description, type and source, and the task's frequency and due time. E19 The plan's assignee defaults to its creator. E20

    On an accepted major incident only, plans take the incident's group and assignee E21 and tasks take the plan's E22; on an ordinary incident, the task has no assignee unless someone sets one. Q06

    4. Channels NEW

    The dialog lists only available channels, never a conference bridge. E23 Notify is off E24, and SMS needs Notify or a Notify number E25, so email is the only channel. Email recipients go to blind copy. E26

    5. Due dates and reminders NEW

    A recurring task's next due date is its last communication plus Due in. E19 The events incident_alert_task.complete.75 and incident_alert_task.breached are raised on task_sla by the Incident communication task SLA E27 Q55, and send Communication Due Warning and Communication Overdue to the task's assignee only. E28 E29

    6. On the incident form NEW

    The workspace incident view lists the plans as Incident Communication Plans (related list incident_alert.source) under Related records. E30 E31 The list has a New action with no role of its own, so the create ACLs decide who can use it. E32 E13

    7. Closing NEW

    Cascade Closure of ICPs would close or cancel an incident's plans when the incident is closed or canceled, but only when com.snc.incident.incident_alert.closure is true; it is false. E33 E34 Q27 Closing a plan doesn't close its open tasks either. E35

    Open questions OPEN QUESTION

    • Q05 Should service desk agents and team leads (itil, itil_admin) be able to create communications on ordinary incidents, or only incident, major incident and communication managers? Decision, for the process owner
    • Q06 Should a communication task on an ordinary incident get an assignee automatically, so that its due and overdue reminders reach someone? Decision, for the process owner
    • Q55 Is the Incident communication task SLA, which raises the communication due and overdue events, defined and active? Value not in the mirror, for the platform team
    • Q27 Should closing an incident close its communication plans? Decision, for the process owner
