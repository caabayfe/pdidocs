    4. Work on an incident

    For: ResolverWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)

    Talk to the right audience

    Write inWho sees itUse it for
    Additional commentsthe caller (emailed) and the team E03questions to the caller, progress they should know
    Work notessupport teams only E04findings, what you tried, hand-over notes

    In the workspace both are in the form's Notes section, above the activity stream. E07 Keep the caller informed in plain words. Keep the detail for the work notes.

    When you have to wait

    Set State to On Hold and choose the On hold reason. E01

    ReasonUse it whenWhat happens
    Awaiting Calleryou need information or a test from the callerwrite your question in Additional comments (required) E02; the fix deadline stops E05; the caller's reply puts it back to In Progress E10
    Awaiting Changethe fix needs a planned changelink the change in Related Records (see Raise a change); nothing takes it off hold for you when the change is done
    Awaiting Problemthe cause is being worked on as a problemlink the problem (see Link incidents to a problem); when the problem is closed as Fix applied, the incident is resolved for you; if the problem team accepts the risk instead, you only get a work note and the incident stays on hold E06
    Awaiting Vendora supplier must actnote the vendor's reference in the work notes; nothing takes it off hold for you

    Only Awaiting Problem ends by itself, and only for a fixed problem. E06 For the other reasons, you take the incident off hold.

    When you take it off hold, set the state back to In Progress; the reason clears. E09

    Deadlines

    The incident shows its SLAs (the deadlines) in the Task SLAs list at the bottom of the form. Watch the response deadline (someone has taken it) and the resolution deadline. The resolution deadline pauses while you wait for the caller and from the moment you resolve the incident, and it completes when the incident closes. So it is met when you resolve the incident in time, as long as it isn't reopened. Waiting for a change, a problem or a vendor does not pause it. E05

    Which timers the workspace shows, what starts, pauses and stops them, and who is warned: Meet an incident's deadlines.

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. New. An incident starts as new. It is logged and gets its number, but nobody is working on it yet.
    2. In Progress. As soon as someone is assigned, it moves to in progress, and the deadlines run.
    3. On Hold. When the team has to wait, for the caller, a change, a problem fix or a vendor, the incident goes on hold with a reason. A reply from the caller puts it back to in progress.
    4. Resolved. When the fix is in, the incident is resolved, with a resolution code and notes, and the caller is told.
    5. Reopen. If it is not fixed, the caller or the team can reopen it, with a comment saying what is still wrong.
    6. Closed. Once the fix is confirmed, the incident is closed, by the caller, a team lead, or automatically after a few days. A closed incident cannot be reopened.

    Changing the priority while you work

    If the impact or urgency changes, update them. You must add a work note explaining the change. E08 See Set the priority.

    Add and find attachments NEW

    Screenshots, logs and error files belong on the incident, not in someone's mailbox. In the workspace, open the Attachments icon (the paperclip) in the record's side panel. E11 No walk has recorded the panel yet; it is read from the mirror. Q89

    • Add: choose Add file, or drag files onto the panel, then Upload. E12
    • Find: Search attachments, and sort by newest or oldest. E12
    • Open: Preview a file, or Download it; Download all takes every file. E12
    • Tidy: Rename a file, or Delete it. Deleting is permanent. E12

    Each file also shows in the activity stream, with a preview. E21

    WhoCan
    anyone who can open the incidentsee and download its attachments E13

    Limits on pdi2: a file can be up to 1024 MB E16, and a file whose content doesn't match its type is refused E17. The panel shows the maximum size and the allowed formats when they are set. E12 Q61

    Once the incident is Closed, the form switches attachments off: add files before it closes. E22 Q89

    The caller sees them too. The portal's ticket page has an Attachments panel: the caller sees every attachment on their incident, including the ones you add, and can add their own while they may update it. E18 E19 When the caller deletes a file there, the comment "deleted an attachment" is added to the incident. E19 Q13 Don't attach anything the caller shouldn't read: unlike work notes, an attachment can't be kept to the support teams. E13 Emails about comments don't carry the attachments. E20

    Tips

    • Update the incident when something changes, not only when you finish. A silent incident looks forgotten to the caller.
    • Before you put it On Hold - Awaiting Caller, ask a question the caller can actually answer.
