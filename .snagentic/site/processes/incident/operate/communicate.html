    8. Communicate about an incident

    For: Resolver, Major incident managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    What a communication plan is NEW

    A communication plan collects the messages sent about one incident to people outside the team: business users, managers, other teams. Each message is a communication task: one email (or a series of updates) to a set of recipients. Nothing on pdi2 attaches a plan by itself E20, so a plan exists only when someone creates a communication.

    It works on any active incident, not only on major incidents. E01 For a major incident, see Communicate, which also lists the email templates.

    WhoCan
    Incident managers, major incident managers, communication managerscreate communications E01 E04 E05 E06
    Every support agentsee the incident's plans and tasks E07

    Use it when an incident affects people who aren't the caller and should be told on a schedule. To tell the caller, write in Additional comments instead (see Work on an incident).

    Create a communication NEW

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Start. On any active incident, open more actions and choose create new communication. Incident managers, major incident managers and communication managers see it.
    2. When. Write a short description, choose now or later, one time or recurring, and when it is due.
    3. Who. Pick email as the channel, and add people or groups as recipients. Text messages are switched off on pdi2.
    4. Send. Compose communication opens the email for you to write and send. Save keeps it for later, in the upcoming section.
    5. See it. The plan appears in the related records tab, under incident communication plans. Every support agent can see it.
    6. Reminders. The task's assignee is emailed when the next update is due, and when it is overdue. On an ordinary incident, set the assignee yourself.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. In the Service Operations Workspace, open the incident, open More actions (⋯) in its header and choose Create new communication. E02 E03 The dialog "Select when you would like to send your communication, add channels, and add recipients." opens. E11
    2. Fill Short description: the subject of this communication. E11
    3. Choose Now to write it straight away, or Later to schedule it. Choose the Frequency: One time, or Recurring for regular updates, and Due in (minutes) for when it is due. E11
    4. Under Channels, pick email. SMS is switched off on pdi2 E15, and a conference bridge is never offered here. E16
    5. Under Recipients, search and add people or groups; Clear all empties the list. E11
    6. Press Compose communication (with Now) or Save (with Later). The button stays disabled until the short description, a channel, a recipient and the frequency are set; Later also needs Due in. E12 E13

    With Now, the email opens for you to write and send. With Later, you see "New communication successfully created and added to the Upcoming section." E14

    See the plans of an incident NEW

    Open the incident's Related records tab and choose Incident Communication Plans. E08 E09 Each plan shows its number, its type and definition, its state, its group and assignee, and its short description. E10 Every support agent can open it; only the managers above can create one. E07

    Reminders NEW

    When a communication task is coming due, its assignee gets " due for Incident "; once it is late, " is overdue for Incident ". E17 E18 On an ordinary incident the task doesn't take an assignee from the plan (that happens only on a major incident) E19, so set Assigned to on the task if you want the reminders.

    When the incident is closed NEW

    Closing or canceling the incident leaves its plans open on pdi2. E21 Close the plan yourself when no more updates are needed.

    Open questions OPEN QUESTION

    • Should service desk agents or team leads (not only incident managers) be able to create communications on ordinary incidents?
    • Do the due and overdue reminders reach anyone on an ordinary incident, that is, does someone set the communication task's assignee?
