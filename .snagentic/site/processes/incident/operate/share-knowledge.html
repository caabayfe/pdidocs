    10. Create and link knowledge from an incident

    For: Service desk agent, ResolverWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    Three ways knowledge meets an incident NEW

    You want toUseWhen
    Point to an article that already helpsAttach itany time you find one E23 E26
    Write down how you fixed this, for next timeCreate Knowledgeonce the incident is Resolved or Closed E05
    Say an article is missing, without writing itReport Knowledge Gapwhile the incident is not Closed E12

    Everything you attach or create is listed on the incident's Related records tab. E01 E20

    Attach an article NEW

    When the knowledge search in the side panel shows an article that fits, attach it to the incident:

    • In the Agent Assist results, choose Attach on the article. It is marked Attached, and Unattach undoes it. E23
    • In Recommendations, a suggested article offers Attach, Attach and add link in comment and Add link in worknote. E24 E25 E26 E27 E28

    What it does:

    1. The article appears in the incident's Attached Knowledge list, with its knowledge base and publish date. E01 E21
    2. Attach in Agent Assist also puts a link to the article in Additional comments, which the caller can read. E23 Q12 In Recommendations, choose Add link in worknote when the link is only for colleagues. E28
    3. If the article is a known error written from a problem, and the incident has no problem yet, the incident's Problem is set to that problem. E29

    Create an article from the incident NEW

    Transcript (7 steps)
    1. Resolve. Resolve the incident first. Create knowledge is offered only on resolved or closed incidents, to resolvers who may contribute to the knowledge base.
    2. Create. Open the create menu in the header and choose create knowledge.
    3. Choose. A create new article tab opens. Choose the knowledge base and, if offered, a template.
    4. Write. Choose next. The tab closes and a new article form opens. Write the issue and the fix, and save.
    5. Draft. The article starts as a draft, and is published through its knowledge base. Afterwards, the create menu offers edit knowledge instead.
    6. Attach. If an article already helps, attach it from agent assist. It joins the attached knowledge list, and its link goes to the additional comments, which the caller can read.
    7. Gap. If no article exists and you won't write one, use report knowledge gap in more actions. It creates a knowledge feedback task for the knowledge team. Nobody is emailed, so tell them if it's urgent.

    1. Resolve the incident first. Create Knowledge is offered only when the incident is Resolved or Closed, and only once: when an article from this incident exists, it is no longer offered. E05
    2. Open the header's Create menu and choose Create Knowledge. E02 E03 E04 Q83
    3. A Create new article tab opens. Choose the knowledge base and, if you can use them, an article template. E06
    4. Choose Next. The tab closes and a new article form opens. E07 Q84
    5. Write the article: the issue as people describe it, and the fix. Save it.

    The new article starts as a draft. E08 It is published through its knowledge base, not through the incident.

    You see Create Knowledge only if you have the itil or sn_incident_write role and may contribute to the knowledge base used for articles from tasks. E04 E05 Q10

    Edit the article later NEW

    When the incident is Resolved or Closed and has exactly one article that you can edit, the Create menu offers Edit knowledge instead. It opens that article; when you finish, you go back to the incident. E05 E09

    The incident's Related records tab lists the articles created from it, with their number, knowledge base, workflow state and author. E20 E22

    Report a knowledge gap NEW

    Use it when you needed an article and none existed, and you aren't the one to write it.

    1. In the header, open More actions and choose Report Knowledge Gap. E10
    2. In Report a knowledge gap, keep or change the Topic (it starts as the short description) and add a Description of what the article should cover. E11
    3. Choose Submit.

    You see "Knowledge gap reported" with a link to the new Knowledge Feedback Task (KFT…). The task records the topic, the description, the incident and you. E15 E16 It is a task for the knowledge team, not a demand or an article.

    Who can do it: people with itil or sn_incident_write, on any incident that isn't Closed. E12 E13 E14

    What happens to a reported gap NEW

    WhoWhat
    Youfind the task under the incident's related lists and add comments to it E15 E20
    Knowledge managers and domain expertscan work and assign it E18
    Anyone who can read the incidentcan read the task E17

    Nobody is emailed when the gap is reported: the "Feedback Task assigned to you" email is only for feedback on an existing article. E19 Q11 Tell the knowledge team if the gap is urgent.

    Tips

    • Attach before you resolve: an attached article shows the next resolver what worked. E21
    • Attach in Agent Assist writes to Additional comments, so the caller sees the link. E23 Attach only articles the caller is allowed to open, or use Add link in worknote. E28
    • Have the resolution notes open while you write the article: say what was wrong and what fixed it, in the words a caller would search for.
    • One incident, one article: Create Knowledge disappears once an article from the incident exists. E05
