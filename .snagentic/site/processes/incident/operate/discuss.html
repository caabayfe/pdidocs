    9. Discuss an incident with colleagues

    For: Service desk agent, Resolver, Team lead, Major incident managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    When to use a discussion NEW

    A Sidebar discussion is a chat about the incident with the people who can help: other resolvers, a specialist, the on-call person of a team, or the caller. Use it when you need a quick back-and-forth that would clutter the work notes.

    UseFor
    Work noteswhat was found and done, for the record and the next person
    Additional commentswhat the caller must know
    A discussionworking it out together, in real time

    Messages in a discussion are kept with the discussion, not in the incident's work notes. E15 When the discussion settles something, write the outcome in the work notes.

    Start a discussion from an incident NEW

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Open the panel. On the incident, choose the sidebar discussions icon in the header. This record shows the incident's discussions.
    2. New. Choose new discussion. The subject starts as the incident's short description; change it if you like.
    3. Participants. Add people or a group. You are offered the caller, the assignee and who is on call. Only people who can open the incident can read the discussion.
    4. Start. Write a short message and start the discussion. Make it private only if you must: that can't be undone.
    5. Received. Colleagues see it under unread in their sidebar. A mention reaches their phone. The caller, if added, gets an email.
    6. Collaborate. The side panel's collaborate chat tab lists the same discussions, and its plus button starts one.

    Who you can invite NEW

    • People who can open the incident. The dialog says "You can add users that have access to this record". E03
    • The caller, even though callers have no support roles: this is switched on for pdi2. E08
    • A group, with its child groups, or its on-call people. E03 E07

    Someone who can't open the incident can't read the discussion either, even if added to it. E14 E15

    The Collaborate panel's Chat tab NEW

    The record's side panel has a Collaborate icon with a Chat tab. E02 It isn't a separate chat: it lists the same Sidebar discussions linked to this incident, newest first, and its + starts one. E04 E05 Use whichever you have open:

    • Collaborate › Chat when you are on the incident and want its discussions;
    • the header's Sidebar discussions to see all yours, across records, and the unread ones.

    Private discussions appear in either only for their members. E04

    Tips

    • Give the discussion a subject others will recognise in their list; the short description is only a start.
    • Add the caller only when you need them in the conversation: they are emailed, and the email repeats the incident's short description and urgency. E10
    • When the discussion reaches a decision, put it in the work notes, where the next person will look.
