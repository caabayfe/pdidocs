    11. Find answers with Agent Assist and recommendations

    For: Service desk agent, Resolver, Team leadWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)

    Agent Assist or Recommendations NEW

    The incident's side panel has two icons that look for help with the incident for you E01:

    IconWhat it doesOn pdi2
    Agent Assist (graduation cap)searches knowledge, the catalog, other incidents and outages with the incident's short description, and lets you act on a result E02 E06works, with a keyword search E05
    Recommendations (bell)suggests actions found by AI Search E03 E27finds nothing: AI Search is switched off E29

    Neither uses Predictive Intelligence (machine learning) on pdi2: no trained model ranks or suggests anything in either panel. The configuration page explains why.

    The panels themselves weren't opened on screen; only their icons were seen. E01 Q69

    Search with Agent Assist NEW

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Open. On an active incident, open the Agent Assist icon in the side panel.
    2. Search. Agent Assist searches with the incident's short description. Change the words if they don't find what you need.
    3. Source. Pick where to look: knowledge and catalog, outages, recent incidents, open incidents or resolved incidents.
    4. Act. On an article, attach it. On an incident, link to it as the parent, or copy its resolution. On a catalog item, order it.
    5. Save. Copy resolution fills the resolution code and notes on the form. Check them, then save the incident.
    6. Recommendations. The recommendations icon is there too, but on pdi2 it finds nothing. Its only active source is AI search, which is switched off.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. Open an active incident in the workspace and choose the Agent Assist icon in the side panel. E04 Agent Assist isn't offered on resolved or closed incidents. E05

    2. Agent Assist searches with the incident's Short description. E06 If that finds nothing useful, change the words in the search box; a search needs at least three characters. E24

    3. Choose a source in the selector E05 E07 Q69:

      SourceSearches
      Knowledge & Catalog (the default)knowledge articles, pinned articles and catalog items together E08
      Outagesoutages created in the last 6 months E12
      Incidentsincidents created in the last 6 months, open or not E10
      Open Incidentsactive incidents from the last 3 months, without children of an accepted major incident E09
      Resolved Incidentsincidents resolved in the last 3 months with a useful resolution code, such as Solution provided or Workaround provided E11

    4. Results come ten at a time. E05 Open one to read it, or act on it straight from the list (see below).

    When nothing matches you see No Matches Found and "Try modifying your search text or filter to find what you’re looking for." E13

    Link to Incident appears only while your incident has no parent, and never between major incidents. E18 Linking an outage needs itil or sn_incident_write, and the action disappears once the outage is linked. E21 E22 Linking needs the right to change Parent incident. E19

    Attaching an article is the quickest way to give the caller an answer. For the other ways to share knowledge, see Share knowledge with the caller.

    Recommendations NEW

    The Recommendations icon opens a list of Suggested actions and an Actions history. E30 For incidents it is shown to itil and sn_incident_write. E25 E26

    On pdi2 the list stays empty. The only active recommendation for incidents is AI Search Recommendation E27, which needs AI Search E28, and AI Search is switched off. E29 Expect "No active recommendations" or "No recommendations to show". E30 Use Agent Assist instead. Q04

    Tips

    • Write a clear short description first: it is what Agent Assist searches with. E06
    • Try Resolved Incidents before Incidents: it keeps only incidents closed with a real fix. E11
    • Check what Copy Resolution put in the resolution fields before you resolve: it copies another incident's words. E13 Then save the incident.
    • Attach writes to Additional comments, which the caller reads. Use it only for articles the caller can follow. E15 Q66
