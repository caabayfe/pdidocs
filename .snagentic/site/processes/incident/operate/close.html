    6. Close, reopen or cancel an incident

    For: Resolver, Team leadWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)

    Closing

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Resolved. A resolved incident waits for the caller.
    2. Who closes. The caller closes it in Employee Center, a team lead closes it, or it closes by itself after seven days.
    3. Closed. Once closed, the caller is emailed and may get a survey. A closed incident can't be changed or reopened.
    4. It comes back. If the issue comes back, log a new incident and mention the old number.
    5. Reopen. Before it is closed, reopen asks what is still wrong. The resolution is cleared, and it goes back to in progress for the same team.

    Download the video (MP4)

    In the workspace, Close and Reopen are buttons in the incident's header, shown when they apply. E04

    An incident is Closed when everyone agrees it's done. After that it can't be changed or reopened.

    • Caller: presses Close on a resolved incident in Employee Center when the fix works. E01 E11
    • Team lead (role itil_admin): you can close any resolved incident, and you're the only ones who see Closed in the state list. E11 E12
    • Resolvers: you can't close incidents, unless you're the incident's caller. Resolve it and let the caller confirm, or let it close by itself. E11 Q18
    • Major incidents: once accepted, only a major incident manager, or the caller if they have a role, can close it. E11
    • Automatically: a resolved incident nobody touches is closed after 7 days. E03 E06

    When it is closed, the caller gets an email "Incident … was closed" E10 and a short survey. E08

    Reopening

    If the fix didn't work, a resolved incident can be reopened (not a major incident E11):

    1. Open the incident and press Reopen in its header. E04 E02
    2. In the Reopen dialog, write in Additional comments (Customer visible) what is still wrong (required). E05
    3. It goes back to In Progress for the same team. The resolution code and notes, and who resolved it and when, are cleared. E07

    The caller can also reopen from Employee Center E14, or by replying to the resolution email with please reopen in the subject. E09

    A closed incident can't be reopened. If the issue comes back, log a new incident and mention the old number. (Replying please reopen to a closed incident's email does exactly that: it creates a new incident. E13)

    Canceling

    If an incident was logged by mistake (a duplicate, a test), resolve it with the code Duplicate and the parent incident (see Resolve an incident).

    The state list also has Canceled, and nothing limits who may choose it: the write rules on the state only stop changes once an incident is Closed or Canceled. Who may cancel isn't decided yet. Q19

    Open questions OPEN QUESTION

    • Q18 Is it right that resolvers with itil can resolve and reopen another caller's incident but not close it? Decision, for the process owner
    • Q19 Should operators be able to cancel incidents, and if so who, since nothing limits who may choose Canceled? Decision, for the process owner
