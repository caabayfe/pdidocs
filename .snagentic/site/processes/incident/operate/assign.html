    3. Assign an incident

    For: Service desk agent, ResolverWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    Who gets the incident

    Every incident needs a team (Assignment group) and, once someone takes it, a person (Assigned to) from that team. In the workspace both are in the form's Assignment section. E09

    Transcript (4 steps)
    1. Saved. An incident is saved, with its assignment group, its configuration item, and maybe a person assigned.
    2. Group from the CI. If the group is empty, the configuration item or service offering fills it in.
    3. Member check. The person assigned must be a member of the group. If not, the save is refused.
    4. In Progress. A new incident with someone assigned moves to in progress by itself.

    Download the video (MP4)

    • The team can fill in by itself, but here it rarely does. If the group and Assigned to are empty when you first choose a configuration item or a service offering, the incident goes to the team that supports it. E01 Only 37 configuration items have a support team, and no server has one, so expect to choose the team yourself. E10
    • Otherwise, choose the Assignment group yourself. The team is notified.

    Take an incident

    In the workspace, open Incidents › Unassigned E05, or Tasks › Assigned to your group for your team's queue E08. Select the incident and choose Assign to me in the list E07, or open it and press Assign to me in its header E06. You must be a member of the incident's team.

    Classic form: Incident › Open - Unassigned, then right-click and Assign to me. If the incident has no team and you belong to only one, your team is filled in. E04

    As soon as a New incident has someone assigned, it moves to In Progress. E03 You get an email that it was assigned to you.

    Pass it to another team

    Change the Assignment group. On the form, the person in Assigned to is cleared if they aren't in the new team. E11 Write a work note explaining why, so the next team doesn't start from zero.

    If the save is refused

    "Assigned to user … must be member of Assignment group …" appears when you save a change of Assignment group while Assigned to holds someone who isn't in the new team. E02 Pick someone from the team, or clear Assigned to and let the team take it.

    The check runs only when the group changes. Changing only Assigned to isn't checked when you save, so make sure the person belongs to the incident's team. E02

    Tips

    • Assign to a team, not straight to a person, unless you agreed it with them: the team sees the queue, a person can be away.
    • Only people with the service desk role can be assigned.
