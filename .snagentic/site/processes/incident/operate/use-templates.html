    12. Fill in and reply with templates

    For: Service desk agent, ResolverWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    Which template for what NEW

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Start. On an incident, the side panel has three template icons: templates, response templates and email template. Compose email is in the more actions menu.
    2. Fill in. Templates fill in fields of the incident. Preview the changes, apply, then save. You only see global templates, your own and your groups' templates, and on pdi2 none is shared yet.
    3. Reply. Response templates give you a prepared message, with the caller's name and the short description filled in. Copy it to the clipboard. On pdi2 only administrators have the role to see them.
    4. Post. Paste it into the additional comments or the work notes, or use the response template quick action while you type.
    5. Email. Compose email writes an email from the incident. In the body, the r quick action inserts a response template. There is no email template for incidents on pdi2.
    6. Undo. If you applied the wrong template, undo template puts the previous values back.

    Fill in an incident with a template NEW

    1. Open the incident and choose Templates in the side panel. E01
    2. Find the template: Search Templates, or the tabs Favorites, My Templates and All. E03
    3. Preview it: the panel says "This template will update the record as follows" and lists the fields it sets. E03
    4. Choose Apply. The fields are filled in on the form, and the panel reports "Template Applied", or the number of conflicts, or fields that are not on this form. E03
    5. Check the values, then save the incident as usual. If it was the wrong template, choose Undo template to put the last values back. E03

    For a text field a template can replace the existing text, or insert its text before, after or at the cursor. E03

    Create template saves your own template. Every agent may do this: the itil role includes the template editor role. E10

E04 E05 E06 E07 E08 The workspace shows you only global templates, your own and your groups'. E09 On pdi2, an agent's Templates panel therefore starts empty, apart from the templates they create.

    Don't use the major incident templates to make an incident major: propose it instead, so that the major incident manager decides. See Propose a major incident.

    Reply with a response template NEW

    A response template is a prepared message with gaps that are filled in from the incident. E11

    From the side panel

    1. Choose Response Templates in the side panel. E01
    2. Pick a template in Select response template. The message appears with the incident's values filled in. E16
    3. Choose Copy to clipboard. The message is copied as plain text. E17
    4. Paste it into Additional comments (what the caller sees) or Work notes, adjust it, and post. See Work an incident.

    While you type

    In Work notes or Additional comments, use the Response Template quick action (command r) with a keyword: it lists up to nine matching templates and inserts the one you choose as text. E18

    The response templates on pdi2 for incident

    TemplateShort nameFills in
    Need more informationmore infothe caller's name, the short description, your name; asks for details E11
    Schedule a meetingmeeting requestthe caller's name, the short description, your name; asks when they can talk E12

    Both can be used on the form, in an email and in chat. E13

    Who sees them: the Response Templates panel and the quick action need the role sn_templated_snip.template_snippet_reader. E14 E18 On pdi2 only administrators have it: neither itil nor sn_incident_write includes it. E15 The walk that saw the icon was done as admin. E01

    Write an email with Compose Email NEW

    1. Open the incident and choose ⋯ › Compose Email in the header. E02 It is there on saved incidents you can update, because the incident table has the email client switched on. E19 E20 Agents have the email role through itil. E21
    2. Write the email. In its body:
      • the quick action r inserts a response template, E22 E13
      • e replaces the draft with an email template, and d with a saved draft. E22
    3. The Email template panel lists email templates for the record. E24 E23

    On pdi2 there is no email template for incident: the existing ones are for communication tasks, change requests and email replies. E23 Start from a blank email or a response template. Emails for a communication plan are written from the communication task instead; see Communicate about an incident.

    Tips

    • Preview before you apply: a template can overwrite what you or the caller already wrote. E03
    • Read the message after a response template fills it in. A value you can't read stays as a red placeholder; replace it before you post. E25
    • A response template copied to the clipboard loses its formatting. E17 Fix line breaks before posting.
