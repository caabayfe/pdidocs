    Confirm the fix, or reopen

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    When the service desk has fixed your incident, say whether it works: close it, or reopen it.

    When it is resolved

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Email. When your incident is resolved, you get an email saying what was done.
    2. Try it. Try it. Does it work again?
    3. It works. If it works, press close in My Requests. If you do nothing, it closes by itself after seven days.
    4. Closed. You get an email and a short survey. A closed incident can't be reopened.
    5. It doesn't work. If it doesn't work, press reopen, or reply to the email with please reopen in the subject. Then say what is still wrong.

    Download the video (MP4)

    You get an email "… was resolved" saying what was done. Try it: does it work again?

    It works

    Open the incident in Employee Center (My Requests) and press Close. If you do nothing, the incident closes by itself after 7 days.

    It doesn't work

    Open the incident and press Reopen. It goes back to the support team, In Progress, and you see "Request reopened". Then say what is still wrong in the Activity tab, so the team doesn't start from zero.

    You can also reply to the resolution email with please reopen in the subject, and say what is still wrong in the message.

    Once it is closed

    You get an email "Incident … was closed". A closed incident can't be reopened. If the problem comes back, report a new incident and mention the old number. (Replying please reopen to a closed incident's email does that for you: it creates a new incident.)

    The short survey

    Every closed incident sends you a short survey by email, "Feedback makes us better": how satisfied you are with the service, from Very Dissatisfied to Very Satisfied, and anything you'd like to tell IT. It takes a minute.

    What the survey asks and how to answer it: Tell IT how it went.

    Tips

    • Test the fix the way you normally work before you press Close.
    • Reopen rather than reporting a new incident while it is still resolved: the team keeps everything it already knows.
