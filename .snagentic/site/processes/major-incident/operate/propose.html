    1. Propose a major incident

    For: Service desk agent or resolverWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    When to propose

    Propose when one failure is hurting many people or a critical service: several reports about the same thing, a site or an application everyone uses down, or a security event. Don't wait to be sure; the major incident manager decides.

    Propose it

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Classic form. Open the incident on the classic form. The workspace doesn't offer propose.
    2. Propose. In the form's menu, choose propose major incident.
    3. Why. In the work notes, write who is affected, what doesn't work, and since when. Add the business impact if you know it.
    4. Candidate. The incident becomes a candidate. Its group is emailed, and the major incident managers see it in the candidates list.
    5. Not for every incident. Child incidents, resolved or closed ones, and existing candidates can't be proposed. Link a child to the major incident instead.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. Open the incident on the classic form. The Service Operations Workspace doesn't offer Propose. E03
    2. Open the form's menu (☰ next to the title) and choose Propose Major Incident. E01
    3. In the dialog, write in Work notes why: who is affected, what doesn't work, since when. Add the Business Impact if you know it. Press Propose. E02

    The incident becomes a candidate: its work notes start with "Proposed as major incident candidate", and its assignment group is emailed. E02 E04 It appears under Major Incidents › Candidates for the major incident managers. E05

    To start from scratch, Major Incidents › Create Major Incident Candidate opens a new incident already marked as a candidate. E06

    What you can't propose

    A child incident (it's already part of something bigger), a resolved or closed incident, or one that is already a candidate or a major incident. E01 Link a child to the major incident instead: Link the other reports.
