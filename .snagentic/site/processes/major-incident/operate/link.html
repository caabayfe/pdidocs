    3. Link the other reports

    For: Service desk agent or resolver, Major incident managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    Link from a list (classic)

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Select. Open a list of the reports of the same failure, and select them.
    2. Link. Choose link to major incident, pick the major incident, and press OK.
    3. State. Each linked incident takes the major incident's state, and follows it from then on.
    4. Comments. Comments on the major incident are copied to every linked incident and emailed to its caller. So write to affected users once, on the major incident.
    5. Resolved. When the major incident is resolved, every linked incident is resolved with it.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. In the classic interface, open a list of the reports, for example Incident › Open, and select them.
    2. Choose Link to Major Incident in the list's actions. The dialog Choose a Major Incident opens and lists the active major incidents. E01
    3. Pick the major incident and press OK. Each selected incident gets it as its parent. E01

    Link from the workspace

    In the Service Operations Workspace, open the major incident and go to the list of its child incidents: Add links existing incidents, New creates one already linked, and Remove unlinks the ones you select. E06 E09 E10

    Link one incident

    On a single incident, set Parent Incident in its Related Records section, on the classic form or in the workspace. E07 E08

    What a linked incident does

    • It takes the major incident's state, and follows it from then on. E02 E03
    • Comments you write on the major incident are copied to it, and emailed to its caller; work notes are copied too, for the team. E04
    • When the major incident is resolved, it is resolved with it. E04

    So write to affected users once, on the major incident.

    A major incident can't be linked under another incident, and a candidate that you link stops being a candidate. E05

    What happens next

    Follow the response from Run the response; callers see the updates as described in Follow an outage.
