    6. Resolve, demote and review

    For: Major incident managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    Resolve it

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Confirm the fix. A major incident can't be reopened, so resolve it only when the fix is confirmed.
    2. Resolve. Resolve it as any incident, with a code and notes written for everyone affected.
    3. Children. Every linked incident that is still open is resolved with it, with the same code and notes.
    4. Report. The post incident report section appears, with an overview, findings, and a timeline filled from the activity.
    5. Review. In the workspace, a draft review is created, to be published within twenty four hours.
    6. Demote. If it turns out not to be a major incident, demote it and give the reason.

    Download the video (MP4)

    Resolve the major incident as any incident (see Resolve an incident), with a resolution code and notes written for everyone affected. Every linked incident that is still open is resolved with it, with the same code and your notes. E01 A major incident can't be reopened afterwards, so resolve it when the fix is confirmed. E10

    Demote it

    If it turns out not to be a major incident:

    1. On the classic form, open the form's menu (☰ next to the title) and choose Demote Major Incident. E02
    2. In the dialog Demote Major Incident, give the reason. You see "… is no longer a major incident". E02

    It becomes an ordinary incident again, and is worked as usual.

    The post-incident review (classic form)

    1. Once the major incident is resolved, the classic form shows the Post Incident Report section: Overview, Findings (what you learned) and Timeline. E03 E04
    2. The timeline is filled from the activity when it is resolved. E05 If it is incomplete, choose the related link Regenerate PIR Timeline: you see "Timeline has been regenerated". E06
    3. Choose the related link Preview PIR to open the report; Download Report PDF saves it. E07

    The review in the workspace

    In the Service Operations Workspace a draft review is created when the major incident is resolved E08, to be published within 24 hours. E09

    The Post incident report tab NEW

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Resolve. When an accepted major incident is resolved, a draft post-incident review is created for it.
    2. Open the tab. Open the post incident report tab. Major incident managers, the assignee and the co-authors can edit it. Add co-authors with edit co-contributors.
    3. Write. Choose edit report. The draft starts with a summary, the impact and the resolution, taken from the incident. Complete them and save the report.
    4. Timeline. The timeline holds the milestones, the incident tasks and any activity entries someone flagged. Hide what doesn't help, and add events the records miss.
    5. Publish. Preview the report, download the PDF, then mark it published. It can't be edited while published; unpublish it to correct it.
    6. Deadline. A banner shows the deadline: twenty-four hours after resolution on pdi2. Only the banner tells you; nobody is emailed.

    Download the video (MP4)

    Use the Post incident report tab of the major incident to write the review, build its timeline, preview and download it, and publish it. E12 The tab appears only when the major incident is Resolved or Closed and its draft review exists. E11 On pdi2 there is no major incident yet, so nobody has seen this tab here.

    The tab has four parts: Incident summary, Incident response timings, Related records with Contributors, and the Timeline. E13

    Who can do what NEW

    WhoCan
    Major incident managers, the major incident's assignee and its co-authorsedit the summary and the related records, add events, change the timeline, preview and download, publish; they also see the deadline banner E14 E18
    Others who can edit the incidentchange the timeline settings, preview and download E14
    Others who can only read the incidentchange the timeline settings E14

    Adding co-authors also needs the right to read users' roles. E14 Ask a major incident manager if Edit Co-contributors is greyed out.

    Write the report NEW

    1. Open the major incident in the workspace and choose the Post incident report tab. E11
    2. Read the banner: "Publish post incident report by …". When 70 % of the time has gone it becomes "PIR SLA 24 hours is approaching …", and after the deadline "Publish post incident report as soon as possible." E16 E17
    3. Under Incident summary, choose Edit Report. The draft already holds three headings taken from the incident: Summary (short description and description), Impact (business impact) and Resolution (resolution notes). E15 Complete them with what happened, why, and what is being done so it doesn't happen again, and choose Save Report. E13
    4. Check Incident response timings: how long it took to recognise the major incident, to accept it, and to resolve it. E16
    5. Check Related records: the problem, the change that fixed it and the change that caused it, as set on the incident. E16
    6. Under Contributors, choose Edit Co-contributors to add the people who help write the report. You choose from support agents; anyone who was a contact on the communication plans is shown with their responsibility. E13 E22 Co-authors get the same rights as you. E14

    The summary copies the incident only when the draft is created: later changes to the incident's resolution notes are not copied into it. E15

    Build the timeline NEW

    The timeline is built for you from E19:

    • the milestones: incident created, proposed, accepted as a major incident, resolved, closed;
    • incident tasks created and closed;
    • entries of the activity stream that someone flagged: flag a work note while you work the major incident and it appears here as "Work notes: … flagged by …". E20

    Then, with Edit Timeline: E13

    1. Hide events that don't help the reader, or Show them again.
    2. Add Timeline Event for something the records don't show (a call, a decision): choose the date and time and write a short description. Only these added events can be edited or deleted, and only while the report is a draft. E21
    3. Refresh Timeline after late changes; your hidden events stay hidden and your added events stay. E19 The timeline is also refreshed when the incident or its tasks change. E31
    4. Settings: order of events, Group events within a few minutes of each other, whether hidden or added events show while editing, Show timeline events in final report, and the date and time format. Reset settings goes back to the defaults. E13 On pdi2 the defaults are newest first, grouped within 5 minutes. E29

    Preview, download and publish NEW

    1. Choose Preview: the report opens as it will be shared, with its status, the incident's number, priority, opened and resolved times, the responder, the contributors, the summary, the timings, the related records and the timeline. E13 E26
    2. Choose Download to save it as a PDF (A4). E27 Support agents can open the preview. E28
    3. When it is complete, choose Mark published. You see "Post incident report has been marked published." E23 E24 The report can't be edited while it is published. E25
    4. To correct it, choose Unpublish ("… marked unpublished."), edit, and publish again. E23 E24

    Nobody is reminded by email: the deadline shows only in the tab's banner. E30 E18

    What happens next

    Share the report with the people who took part, and raise a problem if the cause still needs removing (see Raise a problem).
