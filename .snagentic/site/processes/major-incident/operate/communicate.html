    5. Communicate

    For: Communication manager, Major incident managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    Communications

    A communication on a major incident is a communication task: one message (or a series of updates) to a set of recipients through a channel. Plans can attach tasks automatically from their definitions (Major Incidents › Communication Plan Definitions) E08, but there is no definition for incidents E09, so you start each communication yourself.

    Only communication managers and major incident managers can do it: the action needs the role ia_admin, which those roles contain. E01 E02

    Send a communication

    Transcript (4 steps)
    1. No plan. pdi2 has no communication plan for incidents, so you start each communication yourself.
    2. Create. In the workspace, choose create new communication in the incident's menu. Only communication managers and major incident managers see it.
    3. Templates. Emails start from templates: the initial notice, updates, and the resolution notice, for business or technical readers.
    4. Sent. Recipients are put in blind copy. Text messages and conference calls are switched off on pdi2.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. In the Service Operations Workspace, open the major incident, open the ⋯ menu in its header and choose Create new communication. E01 E10 The dialog Create new communication opens: "Select when you would like to send your communication, add channels, and add recipients." E11
    2. Fill Short description: the subject of this communication. E11
    3. Choose when: Now to write it straight away, or Later to schedule it. Choose the Frequency: One time or Recurring, and for a reminder, Due in (minutes). E11
    4. Under Channels, pick how it goes out, usually email. SMS appears only when Notify is set up, and Notify is switched off here E07 E16; a conference bridge is never offered in this dialog. E15
    5. Under Recipients, search and add people or groups; Clear all empties the list. E11
    6. Press Compose communication (with Now) or Save (with Later). The button stays disabled until the short description, a channel, a recipient and the frequency are set; Later also needs Due in. E12 E13

    With Now, the communication opens for you to write and send. With Later, you see "New communication successfully created and added to the Upcoming section." E14

    On the classic interface, open the communication task and choose the related link Compose to write and send it. E17

    What to write

    Emails start from templates:

    TemplateSubject
    Initial communication (Business)Service Interruption - … E03
    Business UpdateUpdate: Service Interruption - … E04
    Resolution Communication (Business)Recovered Service - … E05

    The technical templates add the business impact, the groups involved, the conference details and the actions taken. Recipients are put in blind copy, so they don't see each other. E06

    What happens next

    Send updates as the response progresses (a Recurring communication reminds you when the next one is due E11) and the resolution notice when you resolve the major incident. Callers of linked incidents also get every Additional comment you write on the major incident (see Link the other reports).
