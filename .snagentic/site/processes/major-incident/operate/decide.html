    2. Promote or reject a candidate

    For: Major incident managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)

    Review the candidates

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Review. Open the candidate from the candidates list, read the work notes, and check for other reports of the same failure.
    2. Promote. To promote it, choose promote to major incident and explain why, and what happens next.
    3. Same record. On pdi2 the candidate itself becomes the major incident.
    4. Assign. If you belong to the incident's group, you become its assignee. Otherwise, set the assignment group and the person yourself.
    5. Reject. Or reject it with a reason. It becomes an ordinary incident again, and its assignee and the person who proposed it are emailed.

    Download the video (MP4)

    These actions are on the classic list and form; the Service Operations Workspace header has no item to promote. E10

    1. Open Major Incidents › Candidates.
    2. Open a candidate. Read its work notes: who is affected, what fails, since when.
    3. Check for other reports of the same failure before you decide.

    Promote it

    1. On the classic form, open the form's menu (☰ next to the title) and choose Promote to Major Incident. E01 The dialog Promote to Major Incident opens.
    2. In Work notes, write why it is a major incident and what happens next (required). Add or correct the Business Impact. E02
    3. Press Promote. The form reloads; its work notes start with "Promoted to a major incident" followed by your text. E02

    The candidate itself becomes the major incident: the creation mode is promote, so Create Major Incident never shows. E07

    No major incident group is set E08, so promotion keeps the Assignment group. If you belong to that group, you become Assigned to, and if someone else was assigned you see "Incident has been reassigned to … from … group". E06 Otherwise set the group and the assignee yourself. Nobody is emailed about the hand-over. E11

    Reject it

    1. On the classic form's menu, choose Reject Major Incident Candidate. E03
    2. In the dialog Reject Major Incident Candidate, write the reason in Work notes and press Reject. E12 You see "Major incident candidate … has been rejected.", and the work notes start with "Major incident candidate rejected" followed by your reason. E03 E12

    It goes back to being an ordinary incident; its assignee and the person who proposed it get the email "Major Incident Candidate … has been rejected". E05

    To reject several at once, select them in Major Incidents › Candidates and choose Reject Major Incident Candidate in the list's actions. The Rejection Reason dialog takes one reason for all of them, and you see "Major Incident candidate … has been rejected" for each. E04

    What happens next

    • Promoted: link the other reports (Link the other reports) and start the response (Run the response).
    • Rejected: the incident is worked as usual (see Work on an incident). If the situation changes, it can be proposed again. E09
