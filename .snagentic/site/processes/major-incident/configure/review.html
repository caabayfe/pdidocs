    Resolution and review - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-19Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)Operator guide: Resolve, demote and review

    Resolving and closing

    IncidentUtilsSNC keeps a caller from resolving their own major incident, limits closing to the caller with roles or a major incident manager, and never offers Reopen. E01 Demote Major Incident sets the state to canceled with the reason in the work notes. E02

    Post-incident review

    The classic section shows for resolved or closed accepted major incidents E03; the timeline is generated once on resolution E04. In the workspace, a draft review record E06 is created on resolution E05, to be published within the configured 24 hours. E07

    The workspace review, in execution order NEW

    #RecordWhat it does
    1Business rule Insert PIR Records On Resolved (sn_sow_inc)when an accepted major incident is updated in Resolved, inserts a sn_sow_inc_post_incident_review in state Draft, builds the timeline and fills the draft; on every later update while Resolved it rebuilds the system events E11 E12 E13
    2Business rules Add/Remove flagged events to PIR and Update pir data - Incident taskkeep the timeline in step with flagged activity entries and incident tasks E14 E15
    3Screen Post Incident Report SNC and its conditionthe record page tab, for readable accepted major incidents in Resolved or Closed that have a review E08 E09
    4Page Post Incident Report SNC and its data brokerssummary, timings, related records, contributors, timeline, settings, publish E10 E18
    5Route Post Incident Report and UI page post_incident_reportthe preview, in an iframe, and the PDF download E32 E29 E30

    The review is created only on Resolved (state 6): a major incident closed without being resolved gets none. The tab's condition also accepts Closed. E11 E09

    The review record NEWsn_sow_inc_post_incident_review holds one row per major incident (incident_ref), with

    state Draft or Published, report_draft (HTML), co_authors (users), timeline_events and timeline_settings (JSON). E06 E27 The draft is filled once, on insert, with Summary, Impact and Resolution from the incident's short description, description, business impact and resolution notes. E12

    Timeline NEW

    SourceEvents
    The incidentcreated, proposed, accepted as major incident, resolved, closed E13
    sys_activity_supplementaljournal entries flagged in the activity stream E13 E14
    incident_taskcreated and closed (Closed Complete, Closed Incomplete, Closed Skipped) E13

    Each review starts with the defaults in the admin setting timeline_settings (sn_sow_cfg_stg, scope sn_sow_itsm_admin): newest first, grouped within 5 minutes, milestones shown. E16 E13 Changing the defaults affects only reviews created afterwards; the date, time and time zone in that setting are not read by this code. E13

    The incident task query is limited to one row, so only one task of the major incident reaches the timeline. E13 This looks like a defect in the base code; report it rather than change it.

    Co-authors NEW

    Candidates are active users with itil, or sn_incident_write when the ITSM roles plugin is active, labelled with their responsibility from the major incident's communication plan contacts. E26 co_authors references users. E27

    Read as written, the candidate list identifies users by user name and the save stores what it is given, and the save reports "Failed to Save Co-authors" unless the update returns the incident's sys_id, which an update of the review never does. E26 Check on screen that co-authors are saved and then get their rights before relying on them.

    Deadline and preview NEWsn_sow_inc.pir.publish.hours (24 on pdi2 E07; read and write role incident_manager E33)

    sets publish by = resolution time + the hours. Up to 72 hours it is shown in hours, beyond in days; empty or 0 removes the deadline. The banner turns to warning after 70 % and critical at 100 %. E28 Only the banner tells: no notification, job or flow watches the deadline. E34

    Preview opens the page named in sn_sow_inc.pir_pdf_preview_sow_page (value redacted in the mirror) in an iframe E29 E32; the UI page post_incident_report renders the report, shows the timeline only when Show timeline events in final report is on, and exports the PDF. E30

    Open questions OPEN QUESTION

    1. Is the ITSM roles plugin (com.snc.itsm.roles) active? It decides whether co-author candidates and editors are itil or sn_incident_write users.
    2. Can co-authors be saved, and does the save confirm it (see Co-authors above)?
    3. Can the people who should add co-authors read sys_user_has_role?
    4. Should every user who can read a major incident be able to change its review's timeline settings?
    5. Should a reminder (notification or flow) watch the publish deadline?
    6. Should the classic Post Incident Report section and the workspace review both stay, or one be retired?
    7. Does only one incident task reaching the timeline match what the team sees?
