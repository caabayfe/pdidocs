    Follow an outage

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    When many people have the same problem

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Report. Report the problem as usual, even if colleagues already have.
    2. Joined. The service desk links all the reports to one major incident. Nothing changes on your side: you keep your own number.
    3. Updates. Updates for everyone affected appear in your activity tab, and you get an email for each.
    4. Fixed. When the failure is fixed, your incident is resolved with it, and you get the usual email.
    5. Still broken. If it still doesn't work for you, reopen it as usual.

    Download the video (MP4)

    When a failure stops many people at once, such as email down for everyone or an application nobody can open, the service desk runs one response for all of them, called a major incident. This page says what you see while that response lasts.

    Before you start

    • Report the problem as usual, even if colleagues already have (see Report an incident). Each report helps the team see how wide the failure is.
    • Keep your own incident number: nothing changes on your side when your report becomes part of the bigger response.

    Follow it

    1. Open your incident in Employee Center. Its page looks the same as before: it doesn't name the bigger response.

    2. Read its Activity tab. The team's updates for everyone affected appear there, starting with "Comment copied from Parent Incident:".

    3. Check your email: for each update you get "Comment added to …" with your incident number.

    4. For news about services, open the System status quick link on the Employee Center home page. It shows the Current Status of services, the Planned Maintenance over the next 5 days and the Status History.

    What happens next

    When the failure is fixed, your incident is resolved with it. Its activity says "Resolved based on resolution of Parent Incident." and you get the usual "… was resolved" email.

    Emails you get

    WhenEmail
    The team posts an update for everyone affected"Comment added to …"
    The failure is fixed and your incident is resolved"… was resolved"

    Tips

    • Don't report the same failure twice: add what you see to your existing incident's Activity instead.
    • If it still doesn't work for you after the fix, reopen your incident (see Confirm the fix, or reopen).
    • If the System status page says nothing is wrong while you still can't work, follow your incident's updates: they come from the team working on the failure.
