    Using Employee Center

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    Employee Center is the IT website for everyone who works here: you search it, report problems, order things, approve your team's orders and follow everything you asked for. It's at /esc on the instance.

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Home. The home page has quick links, popular topics, articles recommended for you, and your active items.
    2. Search or browse. Search the catalog and the help articles in one box, or browse the topics in the help center.
    3. The page you need. Open an item to order, a form to report a problem, or an article. The heart keeps it in your favorites.
    4. My Requests. Everything you reported or ordered is in My Requests.
    5. My Tasks. What waits for you, such as approvals, is in My Tasks.
    6. The bell. And the bell shows what changed on your requests since you last looked.

    The home page

    From top to bottom:

    • Search: type what you need, such as laptop, VPN or password.
    • Quick links: shortcuts, here System status (is a service down?) and Request Standing Desk.
    • Popular topics: Email accounts, Software issues, Accessories, Computers, Software, Developer services, Technology services, Phone and mobility.
    • Recommended for you: help articles you may need.
    • My active items (right): how many Tasks, Requests and Surveys you have open. Choose one to open its list.
    • My favorites: what you marked with the heart.

    Search or browse

    Search when you know what you want. The results mix items you can request (with their price) and help articles; choose Catalogs or Knowledge Bases on the left to keep only one kind, and filter by topic.

    Browse when you don't. Topics nest: Help center › Technology services › Hardware › Computers. Each topic page lists its Support resources, articles and requests together; set Filter by to Requests to see only what you can order, and Sort by to A-Z to scan the list.

    See Find what you need for ordering and Report an incident for problems.

    Follow what you asked for

    • My Requests has two tabs: Submitted requests and Drafts. Switch View between Open requests and Closed requests.
    • My Tasks lists what is waiting for you, with Open and Completed.
    • The bell shows a count when something changed: a request created, approved, rejected or completed, or a comment on your item. Mark one as read, keep it as a reminder or clear it; Mark all as read clears the count.

    Tips

    • Mark items you order often with the heart: they appear in My favorites on the home page.
    • Read the article first: for many questions, Recommended for you or a search answers it faster than a ticket.
    • Can't find it? Report it as an incident and say what you need in your own words: see Report an incident.
