    Choose which emails you get

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    IT sends you emails about your incidents, requests and approvals. You can switch most of them off, and you can subscribe to a few more, such as when an incident affects your office. It's done in notification preferences, not in Employee Center.

    Transcript (4 steps)
    1. Start. Every email from IT ends with an unsubscribe link and a preferences link.
    2. Open. Or open my notification preferences, in the self service menu. Employee Center's profile and bell have no settings.
    3. Switch off. Under system notifications, turn off the emails you don't need, one by one. None of the emails IT sends employees is mandatory.
    4. Subscribe. Choose create notification, and pick location affected, to hear when an incident affects your office.

    Emails and the bell

    There are two kinds of notification:

    • Emails: "Incident … was created", "Request … was created", approvals waiting for you, and so on. These are what your preferences control.
    • The bell in Employee Center: the same news, on screen. You can mark each one as read, keep it as a reminder or clear it, but the bell has no settings. See Using Employee Center.

    Where to change them

    Employee Center has no notification settings: neither your profile nor the bell has them. Use one of these instead:

    • The link at the bottom of an IT email. Every email from IT ends with an unsubscribe link and a preferences link.
    • My Notification Preferences, in the Self-Service menu of the instance. Nobody needs a special role for it.

    Switch an email off

    1. Open your notification preferences.
    2. Under System notifications, find the email, for example Comment left on incident. Search needs 3 characters.
    3. Turn its switch off. The page saves it for you.

    Allow notifications at the top switches all of them off at once. Don't: you would also miss the approvals waiting for you and the password reset link.

    Some emails are mandatory: the page says "This is a mandatory notification and cannot be turned off or unsubscribed". None of the emails IT sends employees on pdi2 is mandatory.

    Subscribe to more

    A few notifications are subscribable: you receive them only if you ask. The useful one for employees is Location affected: "Your subscribed location … is being affected by INC…". You are told when an incident affecting your office, or a building it belongs to, is updated.

    1. In your notification preferences, choose Create notification.
    2. Select notification: choose Location affected.
    3. Choose the location, then where you want to be told (Email) and, if you like, a schedule.
    4. Save.

    You can subscribe in the same way when an incident affects a system, a group or a cost centre.

    Knowledge articles

    To hear when a help article changes, open it in Employee Center and choose Subscribe. You manage those subscriptions on the Subscription & Notification Preferences page, which the knowledge emails link to.

    Tips

    • Turn off what you don't need, one by one, rather than everything.
    • Email Digest groups some emails into one, if you prefer fewer messages.
    • Turning an email off doesn't stop the work: your ticket goes on, and the bell and My Requests still show it.
