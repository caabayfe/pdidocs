    Reset or change your password

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    You can reset a password you've forgotten on the Forgot password page, and change a password you know from the Password Reset menu. To reset it yourself you must first enrol your mobile phone. If you can't, call the service desk.

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Enrol. While you can still sign in, open password reset enrollment and register your mobile phone. It is not done for you.
    2. Identify. On the forgot password page, type your user name, fill in the captcha, and choose next.
    3. Verify. Type your email address, then the six digit code sent to your phone, within five minutes. Both checks are required.
    4. Reset. You get an email called password reset. Open its link within an hour and choose your new password.
    5. If it fails. After three failed attempts you are blocked for twenty four hours. Call the service desk instead.

    Reset a forgotten password

    1. Open the Forgot password page. It has three steps: Identify, Verify and Reset.
    2. Identify: type your User name, fill in the Captcha, and choose Next.
    3. Verify: you are asked for two things, and both are required:
      • your email address, as IT has it;
      • a 6-digit code sent to your mobile phone. Type it within 5 minutes.
    4. Reset: you get an email, "Password Reset", with a link. Open it within 1 hour and choose your new password: at least 8 characters, with upper and lower case letters and a number.

    After 3 failed attempts you can't try again for 24 hours. Call the service desk instead.

    The page resets your password, but it doesn't unlock a locked account. If your account is locked, call the service desk.

    Enrol your phone first

    The text-message code only reaches you if you registered your mobile phone beforehand: it isn't done for you. Do it now, while you can still sign in:

    1. Open Password Reset Enrollment, or Enroll in the Password Reset menu; in the catalog it is under Can We Help You?.

    2. Follow the Steps to enroll: add your mobile number and confirm it with the code you receive.

    3. Choose Submit.

    Your email address needs no enrolment.

    Change your password

    When you know your password and want a new one, open Change Password, in the Password Reset menu or the catalog's Can We Help You? category. Type your current password and the new one twice. The same rules apply: at least 8 characters, upper and lower case letters and a number.

    Your Employee Center profile has no change-password link.

    A password for another application

    For a system other than this one, ask the service desk. Signed in as an IT person, you also see the Password Reset form: "Request a reset of a password for a service or an application". It asks whose password, which application, and how to send you the new one (Telephone, SMS or Email), and it logs an incident for the service desk.

    An employee doesn't see that form: it is open only to IT roles. Get support offers the Request password reset card only to administrators; you see the phone number.

    When to call the service desk

    Call +1-200-120-2020, 9am to 5pm PST, when:

    • you didn't enrol your phone, or the code doesn't arrive;
    • you failed 3 times and are blocked for 24 hours;
    • your account is locked;
    • you need a password reset for another application.

    For how the service desk resets a password, see Reset a caller's password. On pdi2 the service desk can't reset it from its workspace yet, so expect them to log an incident and come back to you.

    Tips

    • Enrol your phone today: you can't do it once you're locked out.
    • Never tell anyone your password, including the service desk. They never need it.
    • The reset link works once and only for an hour; if it expired, start again.
