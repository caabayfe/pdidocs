    Rate an article or say what is wrong

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    Tell the authors whether an article helped. A vote or a few stars take a second; a flag with a comment tells them what to fix, and they write back when it is done.

    Before you start

    You are on an article in Employee Center: see Find and read an article. The Helpful?, Rate this article and Post a comment options are at the bottom of the article; Flag Article is under Actions at the top.

    Say whether it helped

    1. At the bottom of the article, answer Helpful? with Yes or No.
    2. You see "Thank you for your feedback!".

    Give it stars

    1. Under Rate this article, choose one to five stars.
    2. You see "Thank you for your feedback!". The article's average rating, shown at its top, is updated.

    A No or a low rating is counted, but nobody is asked to act on it and nobody writes back. To have something fixed, flag the article.

    Transcript (8 steps)
    1. Read. Find an article by searching Employee Center or browsing its knowledge page, and open it.
    2. Votes. Helpful, the star rating and comments are counted on the article. On pdi2 they ask for no reason, so nobody is asked to act on them.
    3. Flag. To have something fixed, open actions and choose flag article. Write what is wrong, and submit.
    4. Task. The flag becomes a knowledge feedback task for the article's author, or its last reviser. They are emailed.
    5. Fix. The assignee works on it, asks the reader for more if needed, and edits the article or creates a new one.
    6. Resolve. Resolving needs a resolution code and notes. The reader is emailed and asked to accept.
    7. Accept. If the reader accepts, the task closes, and the assignee is told.
    8. Reject. If the reader rejects, it goes back to the author. If they do neither, it stays resolved.

    Flag an article that is wrong

    1. At the top of the article, open Actions and choose Flag Article.
    2. Flag this article opens. Write what is wrong in Add a comment (mandatory): what is incorrect, missing or unclear, and what you expected.
    3. Press Submit. You see "Article has been flagged".

    Your flag goes to the article's author as a task, with your comment.

    Comment on an article

    1. At the bottom, choose Post a comment, write it and press Submit: "Comment posted."
    2. You can reply to and like other readers' comments, attach up to three files, and delete your own comments.

    Comments are visible to everyone who reads the article. A comment is not a flag: nobody is asked to act on it. To have something fixed, flag the article.

    What happens next

    WhenYou get
    The author needs more from you"Feedback Task … has been updated": "We need more information in order to process your feedback on article …", with a link to answer
    The author has acted on your flag"Your Feedback Task … has been resolved": "The feedback you submitted … was resolved. Please review and accept."

    When it is resolved, open the link in the email and choose Accept if the article is now right, or Reject if it isn't: it goes back to the author.

    Tips

    • One problem per flag, and say exactly where it is in the article.
    • Flag rather than comment when something must change: only a flag reaches the author.
    • Use stars and Helpful? on every article you read: they decide which articles appear as most useful and top rated.
