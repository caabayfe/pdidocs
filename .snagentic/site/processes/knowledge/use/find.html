    Find and read an article

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    Knowledge articles answer common questions and explain how to fix common problems. Look for one before you ask for help: it is often quicker.

    Before you start

    • You only see the articles you are allowed to read. If a colleague's link says you can't open it, the article is in a knowledge base you can't read.
    • Search with two or three words that describe the problem, as you would say it, for example password or VPN slow.

    Search

    1. Type your words in the search box at the top of Employee Center.
    2. The results show what matches, the catalog items you can order first, then knowledge articles.
    3. Open an article to read it.
    Transcript (8 steps)
    1. Read. Find an article by searching Employee Center or browsing its knowledge page, and open it.
    2. Votes. Helpful, the star rating and comments are counted on the article. On pdi2 they ask for no reason, so nobody is asked to act on them.
    3. Flag. To have something fixed, open actions and choose flag article. Write what is wrong, and submit.
    4. Task. The flag becomes a knowledge feedback task for the article's author, or its last reviser. They are emailed.
    5. Fix. The assignee works on it, asks the reader for more if needed, and edits the article or creates a new one.
    6. Resolve. Resolving needs a resolution code and notes. The reader is emailed and asked to accept.
    7. Accept. If the reader accepts, the task closes, and the assignee is told.
    8. Reject. If the reader rejects, it goes back to the author. If they do neither, it stays resolved.

    Browse

    1. Open the Knowledge page of Employee Center.
    2. It shows the Knowledge Bases you can read, their Categories, the Top Rated Articles and the Most Viewed Articles.
    3. Choose a knowledge base or a category to see its articles.

    Read an article

    The article page shows, at the top, who wrote it, how old it is, how many times it was viewed and its average star rating. Opening it counts as a view.

    WhereWhat you can do
    Topmark it as a favourite (the heart), Subscribe to it, and open Actions to flag it
    BottomCopy Permalink, answer Helpful?, Rate this article and Post a comment
    Sidethe Most Viewed, Most Useful and related articles
    • Copy Permalink copies a link that always opens the article: "Permalink copied successfully".
    • If an article was retired and replaced, you are shown the new one with "The article … has been retired and replaced with the current article."
    • An article past its review date shows "This knowledge item has expired".
    • You always see the latest published version of an article.

    To say whether it helped, or that it is wrong, see Rate an article or say what is wrong.

    Keep it for later

    • Favourite: the heart at the top keeps the article in your favourites.
    • Subscribe: you are told about changes to the article: "You are now subscribed to …. You will be notified of any new activity according to your notification settings."
    • Send it to yourself: where the page offers it, Send this article to yourself to read later emails you the article, with the subject "Article: …" and a View the article link.

    The Knowledge Portal

    The older Knowledge Portal is still switched on, at /kb. It shows the same articles; use Employee Center.

    Tips

    • No result? Try fewer words, or another word for the same thing.
    • If an article nearly answers your question, say what is missing: the authors are told. See Rate an article or say what is wrong.
    • Still stuck? Report an incident.
