    6. Check article quality

    For: Quality reviewer, Knowledge managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    What AQI is NEW

    The article quality index (AQI) is a review of an article against a checklist of yes-or-no questions. Each question has a weight; the weights add up to 100. The article's score is the sum of the weights of the questions answered yes. E09 E10 A score of 70 or more passes. E11

    The checklist on pdi2 is Default AQI Survey: ten questions of weight 10 each. E01 E02

    #Question
    1Is the knowledge article unique?
    2Is the content accurate and relevant?
    3Is the knowledge article complete?
    4Is the title appropriate?
    5Does the knowledge article meet readability guidelines?
    6Are the external links valid?
    7Has user criteria or security been set properly?
    8Are the Knowledge base and Category fields filled out accurately?
    9Has the proper metadata been entered in the knowledge article?
    10Has the knowledge article been checked for grammar and spelling?

    E02

    Where you can use it NEW

    Only articles in a knowledge base with a checklist can be reviewed. E04 On pdi2 that is only KCS Knowledge Base (demo data); IT, Known Error and Knowledge have no checklist, so no article there shows Perform AQI. E03

    You need one of the roles knowledge_coach, knowledge_domain_expert or knowledge_admin, and you must be able to read the article. E04 E20 As delivered, drafts, articles in review and published articles can be reviewed. E04 E21

    Review an article NEW

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Start. On the classic form of an article in a knowledge base with a checklist, choose perform A Q I. On pdi2, only the KCS demo knowledge base has a checklist.
    2. Answer. Tick each of the ten questions the article meets. Each one is worth ten points.
    3. Submit. Write your comments for the author and choose submit. After that, nobody can change the review.
    4. Score. The score is the sum of the yes answers. Seventy or more passes. It becomes the article's latest A Q I.
    5. Email. The reviser, or else the author, is emailed the score, passed or failed, and your comments.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. Open the article on the classic form and choose Perform AQI. E05
    2. A checklist opens with one line per question, and you as the reviewer. E05 Tick each question the article meets. E09
    3. Write your Comments for Author/Version Author. E08
    4. Choose Submit AQI. E08

    If you leave before submitting, the review stays In Progress: open the article again and choose Continue AQI. E06 E14 If the article has meanwhile been replaced by a newer version, Discard AQI on the old version throws your review away: "The AQI review you had previously initiated has been discarded." E07

    Perform AQI is a button of the classic form; nothing in the mirror puts it on the Knowledge Center's article form. E05

    What happens when you submit NEW

    • The score is calculated and becomes the article's Latest AQI; the field appears on the article only once a review is submitted. E10 E17
    • The author is emailed, or the reviser if the article has one, with the author in copy: "AQI Review for KB…", "An AQI review was performed on an article that you created/updated.", with the score, Passed or Failed, your name and your comments. E12 E13
    • After submitting, nobody can change the review. E16

    Who can see the results: the quality reviewers, the article's author and reviser, and its knowledge base's owner and managers. E15

    Your lists NEW

    The Knowledge Center's Article quality index card E18 and the Article Quality Index list group hold E19:

    ListShows

    Tips

    • Answer yes only when the article fully meets the question: every no costs 10 points, so four no answers fail it. E02 E11
    • Put what needs fixing in the comments: they are the only part of the email that tells the author what to change. E12
    • Finish what you start: a review in progress blocks changes to the checklist and its questions for everyone. See Set up knowledge bases.
