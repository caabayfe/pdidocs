    2. Publish an article

    For: Author, Knowledge managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    Two ways to publish NEW

    Each knowledge base chooses its publish flow. As delivered, a knowledge base without one gets Knowledge - Instant Publish. E05

    Publish flowWhat happens when you choose Publish
    Instant Publish (the default)the article is published at once E06
    Approval Publishthe article goes to Review, the approvers are asked, and it is published once approved E07

    Which flow each of pdi2's knowledge bases uses is not in the mirror; unless it was changed, it is Instant Publish. E05

    Publish your draft NEW

    Transcript (7 steps)
    1. Create. Open the knowledge center and choose create an article. Choose the knowledge base and the template. On this instance, only the known error template is switched on, besides the standard article.
    2. Draft. Write the article and save it. It is a draft, version zero point zero one, seen only by you and the knowledge base's owner and managers.
    3. Publish. Choose publish. If the knowledge base uses approval publish, the article waits in review for its owner and managers, who approve it in your approvals. Rejecting needs a comment. With the default instant publish flow, there is no wait.
    4. Published. The article is published as version one point zero, at once, or on its scheduled publish date, which must be at least twenty four hours ahead.
    5. Checkout. To change a published article, choose checkout. A new draft, version one point zero one, opens with you as its reviser. Readers still see the published version.
    6. Republish. Publish the new version, as before. It becomes version two point zero.
    7. Outdated. The previous version becomes outdated, through the knowledge outdate article flow, and its views are added to the new one.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. Open the draft in the Knowledge Center and save your last changes.
    2. Choose Publish. You see it on a saved draft when you may publish it: you are its author or reviser and may contribute to the knowledge base, or you own the knowledge base. E01 E04
    3. If guests (people who haven't logged in) can read the article, you are asked: "This article may be visible to unauthenticated users after it is published. Do you still want to proceed?" Choose Publish or Cancel. E01 E02 E03
    4. The message tells you where it went: "This knowledge article has been published", "This knowledge item is in review" (waiting for approval), or "Article is approved and will be published on Scheduled Date". E11

    When it is published, its Published date is set to today. E20

    Publish on a set date NEW

    Fill in Scheduled publish date before you choose Publish. The article waits as Scheduled for publish and is published on that date; with Approval Publish, it waits for the approvers first. E06 E07

    The date must be at least 24 hours ahead, or the change is refused with "The Scheduled Publish Date must be set at least 24 hr after the current date." E10

    Take it back NEW

    While the article is in Review or Scheduled for publish, Recall brings it back to draft, so you can change it. Pending approvals are cancelled. E04 See Update a published article for the steps.

    Approve or reject NEW

    If a knowledge base uses Approval Publish, its owner and managers approve its articles. Ownership groups are off on pdi2, so nobody else is asked. E08 E09

    1. You receive " is awaiting approval." E16
    2. In the Knowledge Center, open the Your approvals card. It lists the approvals waiting for you on articles. E12 E13
    3. Open the approval, read the article, and choose Approve or Reject. E14 E15
    4. To reject, write a comment first: "Comments are required when rejecting an approval". E15

    An approved article is published at once, or on its scheduled date. E07

    These emails are switched on for pdi2. E19 With Instant Publish nobody is emailed.

    Republish a retired article NEW

    A retired article can be put back: Republish is offered to knowledge admins on the latest version of a retired article. E21

    Tips

    • Check the knowledge base before you publish: an article in a public knowledge base can be read without logging in. The confirmation tells you so. E02
    • Set a scheduled date when an article must appear with a change or a release, not before. Remember the 24-hour minimum. E10
    • The approvers are people, not a group: if the owner and managers are away, the article waits. E08
