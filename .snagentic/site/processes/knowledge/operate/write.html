    1. Write an article

    For: AuthorWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    Where to write NEW

    Write articles in the Knowledge Center (/now/knowledge-center). Its home page, the Knowledge Hub, has Create an article under Actions, the knowledge bases with their article counts, and the cards Your articles and Your approvals. E01

    You need the knowledge, knowledge_manager or knowledge_admin role to open it. E02 To create an article you must also be allowed to contribute to at least one active knowledge base; each knowledge base decides who may. E08 Q26

    pdi2 has four knowledge bases: IT, KCS Knowledge Base (demo data), Knowledge and Known Error. E01

    Create a draft NEW

    Transcript (7 steps)
    1. Create. Open the knowledge center and choose create an article. Choose the knowledge base and the template. On this instance, only the known error template is switched on, besides the standard article.
    2. Draft. Write the article and save it. It is a draft, version zero point zero one, seen only by you and the knowledge base's owner and managers.
    3. Publish. Choose publish. If the knowledge base uses approval publish, the article waits in review for its owner and managers, who approve it in your approvals. Rejecting needs a comment. With the default instant publish flow, there is no wait.
    4. Published. The article is published as version one point zero, at once, or on its scheduled publish date, which must be at least twenty four hours ahead.
    5. Checkout. To change a published article, choose checkout. A new draft, version one point zero one, opens with you as its reviser. Readers still see the published version.
    6. Republish. Publish the new version, as before. It becomes version two point zero.
    7. Outdated. The previous version becomes outdated, through the knowledge outdate article flow, and its views are added to the new one.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. In the Knowledge Center, choose Create an article. E01
    2. A page asks "What type of article do you want to create?". Choose the Knowledge Base and the Article Template. E03 E04 Q41
    3. Choose Create Article. The article opens in the editor. E04
    4. Fill in the short description (the title readers search for) and the body. Choose a category of that knowledge base.
    5. Save. The article is saved as a draft: only you and the knowledge base's owner and managers see it. E08 E10

    If you choose a knowledge base you can't contribute to, saving fails with "You do not have permission to contribute to this knowledge base." E09

    Templates NEW

    Tips

    • Search before you write: the article may already exist. On the classic form, Search for Duplicates searches for the short description in a new tab. E19
    • Write the short description in the words people use when they search.
    • An article written from a resolved incident starts the same way. See Create and link knowledge from an incident.
    • An article saved without a knowledge base goes to a default one; always choose it yourself. E11
