    What is not sent

    • Expiry warnings are off: nobody is told when an article's Valid to date is near. Watch the Knowledge Center's Expiring Articles list instead. E14 E15
    • A gap reported from an incident sends no email: the assigned to you email is only for feedback about an existing article. E04 See Create and link knowledge from an incident.
    • The approval flow names nobody for "… is approved.", and names the article's reviser for "… is rejected.": whether the author of a first version is told either way still needs checking. E13
