    The life of an article

    Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)

    The states

    An article is written as a draft, published for readers, replaced by newer versions, and eventually retired.

    Each state

    StateWhat it means
    Draftbeing written; readers don't see it E02
    Reviewwaiting for the knowledge base's approvers
    Scheduled for publishapproved; published on its scheduled date
    Publishedreaders can find it
    Pending retirementwaiting for approval to retire
    Retiredwithdrawn; readers no longer find it
    Outdatedan older version, replaced by a newer published one E10

    E01

    What moves it on

    Each knowledge base names a publish flow and a retire flow. As delivered, a knowledge base without them gets the instant flows, so nobody approves. E03

    FromToHowWith the instant flowsWith the approval flows
    DraftPublishedPublishat once E04after approval E05
    DraftScheduled for publishPublish with a scheduled publish datewaits for the date E04after approval, waits for the date E05
    PublishedPending retirement, RetiredRetireat once E12after approval; rejected, it stays published E11
    PublishedOutdateda newer version is publishedautomatic E10automatic E10
    • The approvers are the knowledge base's owner and managers; ownership groups are off on pdi2. E06 E07
    • A scheduled publish date must be at least 24 hours ahead: "The Scheduled Publish Date must be set at least 24 hr after the current date." E08
    • Versioning is on: to change a published article you check out a new version, which starts as a draft. E09
    • When retiring, you can name a replacement article. E13
    • An article's Valid to date marks when it should be reviewed; the Knowledge Center lists those expiring in the next 90 days. E14

    Feedback tasks

    Feedback from readers, and gaps reported from incidents, become knowledge feedback tasks. E15

    StateWhat it means
    Newraised; nobody has acted on it yet
    Work in progressthe assignee is working on it
    Awaiting informationthe assignee asked the person who raised it for more
    Resolvedthe assignee says it is done; the person who raised it can accept or reject
    Closedfinished

    When it is resolved, the person who gave the feedback can Accept (it closes) or Reject (it goes back to Work in progress). E16 Its resolution code says what was done: Updated article, Created article, Updated search configuration, No action or Others. E15

    See Work feedback and knowledge gaps.
