    How knowledge management works

    Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    Articles and knowledge bases

    A knowledge article answers a question or explains how to fix something. Articles live in knowledge bases: each knowledge base has an owner and managers, and decides who can read its articles and who can write them. E10 E14

    Readers find articles, rate them and say when one is wrong; authors write and update them; the knowledge base's managers keep them right and, where the knowledge base asks for it, approve them before they are published or retired. E10 E16

    How it goes

    1. An author writes a draft in a knowledge base they may contribute to. E12 E14
    2. The author publishes it. Each knowledge base has a publish flow: as delivered, articles are published at once; with an approval flow, the knowledge base's owner and managers approve it first. Ownership groups are off on pdi2. E09 E10 E11
    3. Readers rate it and flag problems; on pdi2 only a flag becomes a feedback task for the article's author. E16
    4. To change a published article, the author makes a new version; the old one becomes outdated when the new one is published. E15
    5. When an article is no longer right, it is retired, at once or after approval, and readers can be pointed to a replacement. E09

    The owner and managers of a knowledge base get the knowledge_manager role automatically. E13

    Support agents write articles from resolved incidents and report missing ones: see Create and link knowledge from an incident.

    Where you start

    Knowledge is worked in the Knowledge Center (/now/knowledge-center), under Knowledge Center in the navigator, for people with knowledge, knowledge_coach, knowledge_manager or knowledge_admin. E03 E04 E06 Its lists are grouped as Articles, Ownership Groups, Feedback Management, Administration, Article Quality Index and Article Optimization. E07 Creating, editing or listing articles from the classic navigator takes people with knowledge, knowledge_manager or knowledge_admin to the Knowledge Center too. E05

    The Service Operations Workspace also has a Knowledge list group (all articles, your published and unpublished articles, your feedback tasks), for support agents who write articles. E08

    Readers use Employee Center; the older Knowledge Portal (/kb) is still active. E01 E02 Start with Write an article.
