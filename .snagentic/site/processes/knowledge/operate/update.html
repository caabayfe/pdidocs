    3. Update a published article

    For: AuthorWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    How updating works NEW

    Versioning is on for pdi2. E01 You don't edit a published article; you check out a new version, change it, and publish it. Readers keep seeing the published version until the new one is published. E07 E09

    Editing a published article in place is off: minor edits are switched off, so no field of a published article can be changed directly. E02 E03 Q10

    Check out a new version NEW

    Transcript (7 steps)
    1. Create. Open the knowledge center and choose create an article. Choose the knowledge base and the template. On this instance, only the known error template is switched on, besides the standard article.
    2. Draft. Write the article and save it. It is a draft, version zero point zero one, seen only by you and the knowledge base's owner and managers.
    3. Publish. Choose publish. If the knowledge base uses approval publish, the article waits in review for its owner and managers, who approve it in your approvals. Rejecting needs a comment. With the default instant publish flow, there is no wait.
    4. Published. The article is published as version one point zero, at once, or on its scheduled publish date, which must be at least twenty four hours ahead.
    5. Checkout. To change a published article, choose checkout. A new draft, version one point zero one, opens with you as its reviser. Readers still see the published version.
    6. Republish. Publish the new version, as before. It becomes version two point zero.
    7. Outdated. The previous version becomes outdated, through the knowledge outdate article flow, and its views are added to the new one.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. Open the published article in the Knowledge Center.
    2. Choose Checkout. It is offered on the latest published version, to anyone who may contribute to its knowledge base, and to its owner and managers. E04 E06
    3. A new version opens with "A new version of the article is created for revision." It is a draft, you are its reviser, and its version goes up by 0.01 (for example 1.0 to 1.01). E04 E07 If the new version got a new Valid to date, the message says so. E05
    4. Make your changes and save.
    5. Publish it, as for a new article. Q35

    If someone has already checked it out, you see "You cannot checkout this article as it is already checked out." Work on their version, or ask them. E04

    The new version keeps the attachments, keywords, tags and related articles, and the views and ratings of the old one. E07 The article's subscribers see the checkout in its activity. E16

    When the new version is published NEW

    VersionBecomes
    the new onePublished, with the next whole version number (1.01 becomes 2.0) E08
    the one readers had until nowOutdated E09 E10

    Outdated versions are kept, and can't be changed. E02

    Recall a version waiting for approval NEW

    While your version is in Review or Scheduled for publish, choose Recall to bring it back to draft: "Moved the article back to draft state". E11 Change it, then publish again.

    Go back to an older version NEW

    The knowledge base's owner and managers can choose Make this Current on an outdated version. It creates a new draft from that version, which they then publish. It isn't offered while another version is checked out. E12

    Compare two versions NEW

    In the article's versions, select two versions and choose Compare. The Knowledge Center shows them side by side. E13 E14 Q40 Select only two: "You can compare only two versions of a knowledge article." E15

    Tips

    • Check out only when you are ready to make the change: while your version is checked out, nobody else can check the article out. E06
    • Retiring an article retires all its versions, not just the one you have open. E17
    • For an article that is wrong in a way readers have reported, start from the feedback task, so that the task is closed with the update. See Work feedback and knowledge gaps.

    Open questions OPEN QUESTION

    • Q10 Should minor edits be switched on for a few fields (for example Valid to, category, keywords), so that a typo needs no new version and no new approval? Decision, for the process owner
    • Q35 Is a new version made with Checkout approved again under Knowledge - Approval Publish, as a new article is? Check on screen, for the platform team
    • Q40 Where does the Knowledge Center show an article's versions, and do Checkout, Recall, Make this Current and Compare work as described? Check on screen, for the platform team
