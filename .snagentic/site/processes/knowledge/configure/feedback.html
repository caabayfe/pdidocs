    So on pdi2 only flags reach the tasks: helpful and rating votes, and comments, never carry a reason. E09 Q02

    2. What the feedback record does NEW

    In order:

    1. Before: a rating outside 1–5 is refused; session and user are filled; the comment is sanitised; the article summary is copied; clearing a flag ticks Resolved. E06
    2. After: Set Flagged Status flags the article while it has flagged feedback. E07
    3. After insert, with a reason: Knowledge Feedback Task Creation creates the task. E09 canCreateFeedbackTask lets everything through while glide.knowman.feedback.enable_explicit_feedback is false. E10 E26
    4. Async: the article's rating and helpful count are recalculated. E08

    3. Assignment NEW

    Knowledge Feedback Task Assignment runs for tasks about an article, on insert, without a parent E11:

    1. the article's author;
    2. KM Advanced Installer is active, so: the article's ownership group and its manager when it has one, otherwise its reviser when it has one. Q19

    Gaps reported from a task (incident) have a parent, so they stay unassigned and are worked by knowledge admins, knowledge_manager and knowledge_domain_expert. E10 E11 With no assignee, they send no email. E18 shared/Q06
