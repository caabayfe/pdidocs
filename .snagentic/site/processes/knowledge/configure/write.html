    Writing articles - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-19Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)Operator guide: Write an article

    The applications NEW

    ScopeWhat it gives authors
    globalthe article table, its rules, the classic form and the launchers
    sn_km_centerthe Knowledge Center workspace: home, lists, the create page, its Publish actions
    sn_km_uibthe record page's actions in Next Experience: the template selector, Publish, Approve, Reject, Compare
    sn_ui_knowledgethe knowledge components, among them the article template selector

    1. Who can open the Knowledge Center NEW

    The Knowledge Center pages are readable by knowledge, knowledge_manager and knowledge_admin. E01 E02 Contributing to a knowledge base is a separate check, made by its user criteria through SNC.KnowledgeHelper.canContribute. E16 E19 Q26

    2. How people arrive NEW

    EntryGoes to
    Knowledge Center › Create an articlethe kb_interceptor page E12
    Knowledge › Create New (knowledge_create.do)the Knowledge Center when the redirect applies, else the classic form E07 E09
    New on an article related listknowledge_create.do, as above E10
    knowledge_edit.do, knowledge_list.dothe Knowledge Center record or list, or the classic one E08
    kb_knowledge.do?sys_id=-1always the classic form: no launcher is involved E11

    The redirect applies when the Knowledge Center plugin is active, sn_km_center.glide.knowman.enable and sn_km_center.glide.knowman.redirect.enable are true, and the user has one of the three knowledge roles. E03 E04 E06 All three hold on pdi2, so a knowledge user using the module is redirected. The create launcher redirects only for a new article or one from a feedback task; from any other source (an incident, for example) it keeps the classic route. E07

    The redirect does not cover the article form itself: the walk opened kb_knowledge.do?sys_id=-1 and stayed on the classic form. E11 Bookmarks, links in emails and list New buttons outside the launchers still reach the classic UI. Q11

    3. Template chooser NEW

    1. New on kb_knowledge opens the template selector when the user can create, versioning is installed, and canContributeToTemplates passes; otherwise the plain form. E13
    2. canContributeToTemplates needs only one active template. E14 On pdi2 that is Known Error article; KCS Article, What Is, How to and FAQ are inactive. E15 Q17 So everyone who may contribute sees the chooser, with the standard article and Known Error.
    3. In the Knowledge Center, a list's New opens a new record page instead of the pop-up. E13

    4. When the draft is saved NEW

    Before insert on kb_knowledge, in order:

    RuleDoes
    Populate Default KB if Empty (100)fills an empty knowledge base with a fixed sys_id E17 Q24
    Set article in draft state on insert (100)forces Draft E18
    Check User CanContribute To Kb (101)refuses the insert if the user can't contribute E19

    After insert, Versioning : Initialize Version sets version 0.01. E20 Versioning is on. E24

    Who can then read, edit and delete the draft: the knowledge base owner, a knowledge admin, and its author or reviser while they may contribute. E16 E21 The Knowledge Center lists My Articles and Unpublished show them. E23

    5. Editor NEW

    The Knowledge Center uses the Enhanced Content Editor: sn_km_center.glide.knowman.ece.enable is true. E05 E06 The Knowledge Center's own Publish actions depend on it (see Publishing - configuration).

    Search for Duplicates exists only on the classic form, for itil or knowledge, on drafts and articles in review, and only while the machine-learning knowledge plugin is inactive. E22
