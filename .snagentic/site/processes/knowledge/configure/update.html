    3. Outdating the previous version NEW

    On publish, incrementMajorVersion calls outdateOldVersion: every other Published version of the same article becomes Outdated, and its view count is added to the new one. E11 When the knowledge base has a publish flow (pdi2's default), this fires Knowledge - Outdate Article (knowledge__outdate_article), which marks the article outdated and sets its stage for the list views E11 E12; otherwise the state is set directly. The flow is kept out of the flows a knowledge base can choose. E13 Its executions are not in the mirror. Q30

    5. Retiring and deleting versions NEW

    Retiring an article retires all its versions, without running their rules. E20 Deleting a version moves the latest flag to the newest remaining one, unless it is outdated. E21
