    Retiring articles - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-19Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)Operator guide: Retire or replace an article

    The pieces NEW

    ScopeWhat it gives retiring
    globalthe Retire, Republish buttons on the classic form, KBKnowledge, KBVersioning, the retire flows, the versioning rules, the approval email
    sn_km_uibthe Knowledge Center's Retire action and its Retire Confirmation Modal
    sn_km_centerthe Knowledge Center's Republish action and the Expiring Articles and Retired lists
    sn_km_portalwhat a reader sees in Employee Center when an article is retired

    canRetire, with versioning on E34: the article is not external, it is the latest version and published, and the user is an admin user, the knowledge base's owner or a manager, or its author or reviser with contribute access. E07 E08 The KCS restriction in canRetire applies only when the KCS Capabilities plugin is active. E07

    2. The modal and the replacement NEW

    The picker offers published articles other than this one, in knowledge bases of the same application (kb_knowledge_base.application), searched by number or short description, 100 at a time. E02 Retire Knowledge then calls retireKnowledgeById or retireKnowledgeWithReplacementByIds. E03 The server checks again that the replacement is published, different and readable, and writes replacement_article before the retire flow starts. E07

    3. The retire flow NEW

    1. retireKnowledge starts the knowledge base's retire workflow or flow (kb_version 3); a version 2 knowledge base is set to Retired directly. E07 E09
    2. The legacy Retire workflow field is inactive, so kb_retire_flow is used. E10 E11 A new knowledge base without one gets Knowledge - Instant Retire. E12 Q24
    3. The flows:
    FlowBehaviour
    Knowledge - Instant Retireno approval E13
    Knowledge - Approval RetirePending retirement, then approval; rejected, back to the previous state E14 E36 Q15
    1. Approvers: the owner (if active) and managers; ownership groups are off. E15 E16 They get KM: Approve article for publishing, which fires for every approval on kb_knowledge, publish or retire. E17
    2. On Retired, Versioning : Retire all versions retires every other version of the article, with business rules off. E18

    4. What readers get NEW

    • Employee Center's Knowledge Article Content widget and the Knowledge Center's Get Replacement Article follow replacement_article to the latest accessible version, through any chain. E20 E21
    • Without a replacement, a retired article opens only for isAdminUser. E22 Q27
    • Lists and search use getKnowledgeEncodedQuery: published, active, Valid to after yesterday. E27

    5. Republish NEW

    Republish (classic and Knowledge Center) is offered on the latest retired version to isAdminUser; the Knowledge Center's needs the enhanced editor too. E23 Q27 It sets the other retired versions to Outdated and this one to Published. E08 Update Replacement Article References clears the replacement. E19

    6. Valid to and expiry NEW

    • Default: today plus the knowledge base's article_validity days, else 2100-01-01. E24 Q16 It is recalculated when the article's knowledge base changes. E25 A UI policy guards zero or negative validity on the knowledge base. E26
    • Lists: Expiring Articles (next 90 days) and Retired in the Knowledge Center's Articles group. E28 E29
    • The monthly job runs on day 1 but does nothing while the property is false; the email Knowledge articles expiring next month is active but never queued. E30 E31 E32 E33 Q09
    • The classic form shows the Retired date only once it is filled. E35
