    Publishing articles - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-19Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)Operator guide: Publish an article

    The records NEW

    ScopeWhat it gives publishing
    globalthe publish and approval flows, the approver list, the rules on publish dates, the emails, the classic Publish button
    sn_km_centerthe Knowledge Center's Publish, Republish and confirmation modal, and its My Approvals list
    sn_km_uibPublish, Approve and Reject on Next Experience record pages

    1. The knowledge base's publish flow NEW

    Each knowledge base names a Publish flow (kb_publish_flow); the legacy Publish workflow field is inactive, so flows are used. E01 A knowledge base saved without flows gets Knowledge - Instant Publish and Knowledge - Instant Retire. E02 To require approval, set its Publish flow to Knowledge - Approval Publish.

    2. The Publish button NEW

    In the Knowledge Center, with the enhanced editor on, one of two sn_km_center actions shows on a draft the user may publish E03 E04 E10:

    ActionWhenDoes
    Publish (333db22a)the guest user can't read the articlesaves and publishes E03
    Publish (cbb12c58)the guest user can read itopens the confirm-publish modal first E04 E06 E07

    Both are hidden when the article has merged duplicates waiting. E08 sn_km_uib has the same Publish for Next Experience record pages without the editor check E05; the classic form uses its own button with the same confirmation. E26

    Who may publish: the knowledge base owner, a knowledge admin, the reviser who may contribute, or an ownership group member (off on pdi2). E09 E16

    3. What Publish runs NEWKnowledgeUIActionSNC.publish E08:

    1. If the scheduled publish date changed and is less than 24 hours ahead, refuses (the same check runs as a business rule on every save). E08 E10 E11
    2. Checks canPublish. E08 E09
    3. On a current-style (version 3) knowledge base, starts its publish workflow or flow; on version 2, sets Published. E08
    4. Shows the new state's message.

    Then the flow:

    FlowSteps
    Knowledge - Instant Publishdraft → with a scheduled date, wait for it → publish E12
    Knowledge - Approval Publishask for approval → approved: queue knowledge.approval.approved, then as above; rejected: queue knowledge.approval.rejected E13

    The approval has no due date. E14 On publish, Ensure published date is set sets Published to today. E25 Recall cancels the waiting flow and its requested approvals. E09

    4. Approvers NEWKBWorkflowSNC.getApprovers returns the knowledge base's owner, if active, and its active

    managers. E15 Ownership groups would replace them, but glide.knowman.ownership_group.enabled is false. E16 Owners and managers get knowledge_manager through Update knowledge_manager roles. E17

    They act in the Knowledge Center's My Approvals list (the Your approvals card) E18 with:

    • Approve: for the approver, a delegate, or approval_admin; sets Approved. E19
    • Reject: same, and requires a comment. E20

    5. Emails NEW

    NotificationEventToRecord
    KM: Approve article for publishingan approval is requestedeach approverE21
    KM: Article approved for publishknowledge.approval.approvedparm1: empty in the flowE22 E14
    KM: Article rejected for publishknowledge.approval.rejectedparm1: the article's reviserE23 E14

    The approved and rejected emails are sent only while glide.knowman.enable_approval_notification is true; it is. E24 Because the flow names nobody for approved, that email may reach nobody, and for a first version (no reviser) the rejected one too. E14

    6. Republish NEW

    Republish puts a retired article back. It needs the enhanced editor, the latest version, and an admin user. E27 E10

    Properties NEW

    Propertypdi2Effect

    Open questions OPEN QUESTION

    1. Which publish flow does each knowledge base use? The knowledge base records are not in the mirror; the walk listed four knowledge bases but not their flows.
    2. Should Knowledge - Approval Publish name the author in knowledge.approval.approved and fall back to the author for knowledge.approval.rejected, so that a first version's author is told? E14
    3. Should approvals have a due date, or a reminder, since they wait on named people? E14
    4. Which knowledge bases can the guest user read (public knowledge bases), so that the confirmation appears?
