    2. What each reader sees NEW

    • Articles: what the reader's user criteria allow on each knowledge base; Employee Center names no knowledge base, so all readable ones. E01 A new knowledge base starts readable by every signed-in user, writable by anyone with a role, and closed to guests, until its user criteria are changed. E14 The classic kb_view page is public only if made so. E15
    • Edit Article and Flag Article in Actions: decided by KBViewModel (contribute rights; glide.knowman.show_flag). E07 See Ratings, flags and feedback tasks - configuration for the feedback options.
    • Subscribe: everyone signed in. E08 E09
    • Send to myself: only with a send-to-self configuration for Employee Center, which the mirror doesn't hold. E10

    3. Search NEW

    Employee Center's search returned the catalog item first, then knowledge articles, for password. E17 The classic knowledge search settings apply to the Knowledge Portal and the classic UI: 20 articles a page, with views, rating and author shown, and glide.knowman.search.apply_acls false. E16

    4. Emails and topics NEW

    • Send to myself queues sn_ex_sp.knowledge.send_email for the reader; Knowledge article email sends "Article: …". E10 E11
    • Articles placed in Employee Center topics follow their newest version. E18

    The Knowledge Portal NEW/kb is active but not the default portal, and classic links are not redirected to it. E12

    E13 The feedback emails to readers link to it. See Ratings, flags and feedback tasks - configuration.
