    Article quality index - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-19Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)Operator guide: Check article quality

    The records NEW

    TableWhat it holds
    kb_quality_checklista checklist; pdi2 has Default AQI Survey E01
    kb_checklist_questionits questions, each with an order and a weight; ten of weight 10 E02
    kb_knowledge_base.checklistthe checklist a knowledge base uses E03; only KCS Knowledge Base (demo data) has one E04
    kb_article_checklist_summaryone review: article, reviewer, version author, state, AQI score, comments E05
    kb_article_checklist_answerone answer per question, with its weighted score E05 E07

    All in global. The Knowledge Center (sn_km_center) adds the Article Quality Index list group. E24

    1. Who can review NEWcanPerformAqi: the article's knowledge base has a checklist, the user can read the article,

    holds knowledge_domain_expert, knowledge_coach or knowledge_admin, and the article's state is in glide.knowman.aqi.article_workflow_states. E05 That property has no value in the mirror; the code's default is draft, review and published. E14 Creating a review needs the same roles. E19 Both reviewer roles contain knowledge. E23

    2. A review, step by step NEW

    1. Perform AQI (classic form only) creates the summary, In Progress, and one answer per question. E05 E06
    2. Ticking an answer sets its weighted score to the question's weight. E07
    3. Submit AQI (reviewer or knowledge_admin) sets Submitted. E05 E06
    4. Calculate AQI Score sums the weighted scores into aqi_score, sets the article's latest_aqi, and queues km.notify_about_article_aqied. E05 E08
    5. KM: AQI on Article emails the reviser, or the author, with the author in copy; Passed from 70. E13 E15 E16 The ownership group variant is active but ownership groups are off. E17 E18

    Continue AQI reopens the user's pending review; Discard AQI deletes it on an outdated version. E06 A copy of an article starts without a Latest AQI E09, and the field stays hidden until there is one. E25

    3. Changing a checklist NEW

    • Only knowledge_admin creates checklists and questions. E22
    • Questions can't be changed while the checklist is attached to a knowledge base or has pending reviews; weights can't be negative or total more than 100. E10 E22
    • A knowledge base accepts a checklist only if its weights total exactly 100, and can't switch while its reviews are pending. E11
    • Checklist names are unique. E12

    To change Default AQI Survey: finish or discard pending reviews, detach it from the KCS knowledge base, edit, then attach it again. E10 E11

    4. Who sees results NEW

    OperationWho
    ReadAQI roles, the article's author or reviser, the knowledge base's owner and managers E20
    Writeknowledge_admin or the reviewer, until submitted E21
    Deleteknowledge_admin, or the reviewer until submitted E21
