    6. Accept the risk, cancel or reopen

    For: Problem coordinator, Problem managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    All of these are buttons on the classic problem form.

    Accept the risk

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Decide. During assess or root cause analysis, you may decide the problem isn't worth fixing, or isn't a problem.
    2. Accept Risk. Accept risk asks for the cause, the reason, and any workaround people should keep using.
    3. Closed. The problem closes at once. Incidents waiting for it are not resolved. Their teams should resolve them with the workaround.
    4. Duplicate or cancel. Mark duplicate asks for the original problem. Cancel asks for a reason.
    5. Reopen. Re-analyze sends it back to root cause analysis. On a closed problem, only a problem manager can do it.

    Download the video (MP4)

    Use it when fixing isn't worth it, from Root Cause Analysis or Fix in Progress. E01

    1. Press Accept Risk. If the required fields are missing, the Accept Risk dialog opens. E09
    2. Fill Cause notes, the Risk accepted reason, and the Workaround people should keep using, with Workaround applied if it is. E10 E13
    3. Confirm. The problem closes at once with the resolution code Risk Accepted. E02

    Incidents waiting for it are not resolved: they get the work note "Problem team has accepted the risk of not fixing this problem …" with your reason, and stay with their teams, who should resolve them with the workaround. E03

    Mark it a duplicate

    From Assess or Root Cause Analysis: E04

    1. Press Mark Duplicate. The Mark Duplicate dialog opens. E09
    2. In Duplicate of, choose the original problem. E11 You may see "Marking this problem as a duplicate will move related records to the Duplicate of problem as a background task". E09
    3. Confirm. The problem closes with the resolution code Duplicate. E09

    Cancel it

    From Assess or Root Cause Analysis, when it isn't a problem after all: E05

    1. Press Cancel. The Cancel dialog opens. E09
    2. Write the Canceled reason. E12 E13
    3. Confirm. The problem closes with the resolution code Canceled. E09

    Re-analyze it

    1. Press Re-Analyze, from Fix in Progress, Resolved or Closed. E06
    2. The problem goes back to Root Cause Analysis. From Resolved or Closed, its resolution code, close notes and closing dates are cleared and the reopen count goes up by one. E14

    A closed problem can't be edited otherwise. E08 Only a problem manager can re-analyze it E07, and a duplicate can't be re-analyzed. E15

    What happens next

    Once closed, the problem is read-only until someone re-analyzes it. E08 To keep working on the cause, see Assess and find the root cause.

    Tips

    • Accepting the risk leaves the waiting incidents open: tell their teams so they resolve them. E03
    • Write the reason for the incident teams too: with Risk Accepted they receive it word for word. E03
