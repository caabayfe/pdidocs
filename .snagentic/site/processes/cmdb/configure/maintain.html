    CI data and access - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-17Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)Operator guide: Keep your configuration items right

    Who can edit CIs OPEN QUESTION

    Anyone with itil can create, edit and delete CIs E01 E02. The platform offers a job that moves those rights to the CMDB roles E03; it hasn't been run. Should the service desk keep editing CIs?

    Data owners

    Ask CI owners to fill the Support group E04 and Change Group: most CIs here have neither.

    The CI page and the CI form NEW

    The CMDB Workspace has two ways to show a CI:

    CI page (Explore CI)CI form
    Routeci-recordrecord, view cmdb_browse (CMDB - Workspace) E10
    Built bysn_cmdb_wssn_cmdb_pg_templts: 130 view rules, one per class, 129 of them linked to CMDB Workspace View Rules E11 E12
    Header actionsOpen map, and Configure attributes / Configure resources for admins and the personalize roles E08 E09Create incident, Create change, Open dependency view, View CMDB 360 Data E13 E14 E15 E16

    sn_cmdb_ws.explore_ci.record.enabled is true E05, so every link to a CI resolves to the CI page E06, and the record page redirects any saved CI there. E07 The four form buttons are limited to the cmdb_browse view E17, so on pdi2 they show nowhere for a saved CI: not on services, not on any other class.

    ButtonConditionWould do
    Create incidentcan create incidents and has sn_incident_write E13new incident with this CI

    Open dependency viewsaved record E15classic dependency view in a new window
    View CMDB 360 Datamultisource on, Discovery licence plugin active, saved record E16CMDB 360 preview; off here, multisource is false E18

    On a business process, sn_cmdb_pg_templts also swaps the global Open Dependency View for its own in the workspace view. E19 Its domain separation script hides the domain section of the form when domain separation extensions aren't installed. E20

    Open questions OPEN QUESTION

    1. Keep the CI page? Turning sn_cmdb_ws.explore_ci.record.enabled off (sn_cmdb_admin) brings back the form and its Create incident and Create change, and loses the CI page's sections. E05
    2. If the CI page stays, should Create incident and Create change be added to its header? It has no declarative action bar today. E08
    3. Create incident and Create change are also allowed on a new CI's form, the only case that stays on the form E07 E13 E14; there they would open a ticket for an unsaved CI. Should they be hidden on new records?
