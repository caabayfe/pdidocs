    Say which service is affected

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    Some forms ask which application or service you mean, so that IT knows what is affected.

    When you're asked

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Asked. Some forms, such as report outage or password reset, ask which application or service you mean.
    2. Type. Start typing its name.
    3. Pick. And pick it from the list.
    4. Routed. Your report then reaches the team that runs it.
    5. Not listed. If you can't find it, choose the closest one, and say what you mean in the description. The service desk will correct it.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. On Report Outage, Report Performance Problem or Password Reset, you see a question such as What application is down/unavailable? It must be answered.
    2. Start typing the name of the application or service. A list of matches appears.
    3. Pick the one you mean from the list.

    The forms themselves are described in Report an incident.

    What happens next

    The service desk sees what you picked on your incident, which helps it send your report to the team that looks after it.

    Tips

    • Pick only one: if several applications are affected, name the others in the description.
    • Can't find it? Choose the closest one and say what you mean in the description; the service desk will correct it.
