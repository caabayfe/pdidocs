    3. Keep your configuration items right

    For: CI ownerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    Your CIs

    Transcript (4 steps)
    1. Workspace. Open the CMDB workspace. My work lists the tasks waiting for you, and intelligent search finds a CI by name.
    2. Ownership. On each CI you own, keep the support group, the change group, the owner, and its statuses right.
    3. Audits. Certification audits check CI data on a schedule, such as critical business services every week, assigned to whoever manages the CI.
    4. Attest. You're emailed when you are asked to attest, and you can edit the CIs you own to fix what the audit finds.

    Download the video (MP4)

    Open the CMDB Workspace E01: My work lists the tasks waiting for you, and Intelligent search finds a CI by name. E02 Keep these right on each CI you own: the Support group, the Change Group, Owned by, and its statuses (see Who owns what).

    Certification

    Certification audits check CI data on a schedule, such as Critical Business Services every week, assigned to the CI's Managed by. E04 You're emailed when asked to attest E05; you can edit the CIs you own to fix what the audit finds. E03

    A CI's page in the CMDB Workspace NEW

    Opening a CI in the CMDB Workspace shows the CI page: the sections Summary, Attributes, Tags, Resources, Activity, Relationships, Services and Offerings and Health. E06 Its header has:

    That is all, for every class of CI, not only services. E06 E10 Q16

    Create incident and Create change don't show NEW

    The CMDB Workspace also has form buttons Create incident, Create change, Open dependency view and View CMDB 360 Data. E11 E12 E13 They belong to the older CI form, and on pdi2 CIs always open on the CI page instead E07 E08, so you won't see them. Q02 View CMDB 360 Data would stay hidden anyway: multisource data is switched off. E14

    Instead:

    • To raise an incident or a change for a CI, create it from the incident or change list and set the configuration item there: see Choose the right configuration item.
    • To see dependencies, use Open map on the CI page, or the dependency view from a ticket: see See what depends on a configuration item.
    • To create a CI, see Create a configuration item.

    Open questions OPEN QUESTION

    • Q16 Do classes other than services, such as a server or an application, show the same CI page header? Check on screen, for the platform team
    • Q02 Should CIs keep opening on the CI page, or should sn_cmdb_ws.explore_ci.record.enabled be turned off to bring back the CI form with Create incident and Create change? Decision, for the process owner
