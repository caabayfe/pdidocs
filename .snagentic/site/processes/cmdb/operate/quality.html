    4. Watch CMDB quality

    For: CMDB managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    Health

    Transcript (4 steps)
    1. Health. The CMDB health card has no scores on pdi2, because its jobs are switched off. Turn them on to get scores.
    2. Stale. A CI counts as stale after sixty days without an update.
    3. Duplicates. De-duplication tasks list CIs found twice. Remediate them from the CMDB workspace.
    4. Retirement. The data manager has only the dependent CI policies. No attestation, retirement or certification policy is set up.

    Download the video (MP4)

    The CMDB Workspace shows a CMDB Health card, but it says "CMDB Health Dashboard jobs haven't been run": the score jobs are switched off. E01 E02 A CI is stale after 60 days without an update. E03 Turn the jobs on to get scores. Q03

    Duplicates

    Configuration › De-duplication Tasks lists CIs found twice; remediate them from the CMDB Workspace. E04

    Retirement and archiving

    The Data Manager has only the Dependent CI policies E05: no attestation, retirement or certification policy is set up.
