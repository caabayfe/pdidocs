    See who is on call - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-19Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)Operator guide: See who is on call

    The applications NEW

    ScopeWhat it gives
    global (On-Call Scheduling, com.snc.on_call_rotation)the navigator modules, the On-Call Schedules and calendar pages, the reports E01 E02
    sn_sow_collabresolving a group to its on-call people when it is added to a Sidebar discussion E16

    On pdi2 no shift exists E19, so every page here is empty and the Sidebar resolver never applies. E17

    1. Who can open the pages NEW

    1. The application menu is shown to itil, roster_admin, rota_admin, rota_manager and oc_read. E01
    2. The modules for seeing who is on call have no roles of their own. E02 E08 E11 Overview needs itil or rota_admin. E14
    3. The pages themselves check OCCalendarUtils.canRead: one of the roles in com.snc.on_call_rotation.calendar_read_roles. E03 E04 The property's value is redacted in the mirror; without it, the default is itil, roster_admin, rota_admin, rota_manager. E05 Q17
    4. Changing a group's schedule needs shift-manager access for that group (rotaMgrAccess). E04

    2. On-Call Schedules NEW$oc_groups.do is a UI page that loads the AngularJS app sn.on_call.now. E03 Its labels are in

    the UI script E06:

    • tabs Pinned, My and All On-Call Schedules, with search by group or schedule;
    • a card per group with who is on call now and Roster and escalation details;
    • the person's name, title, email, contact number and manager;
    • a summary of pending actions, gaps, conflicts and time off requests, shown because landing_page.show_pending_actions is true. E07

    The page adds a WebRTC phone when Notify is active, and a direct message button when Slack or Microsoft Teams direct messages are configured. E03 Q09

    3. Calendars, reports and Overview NEW

    ModuleTargetNotes
    On-Call Calendars$oc_calendar_launcher_redirect.do → on_call_rotation_calendar_launcher.do E08 E09the legacy calendar is off E10
    Schedule Reporton_call_schedule_report E11at least one group; under 200 unless Table E12; reports cover at most 40 days E13
    Overviewon_call_dashboard_launcher_v2.do E14 E15itil, rota_admin

    4. Sidebar discussions NEW

    1. The extension instance registers OnCallSidebarGroupMemberResolver with Sidebar's group member extension point. E16
    2. It applies to incident, problem and change request, for a group with any shift, even an inactive or draft one. E17
    3. It adds OnCallRotation.whoIsOnCall for the group, keeping the first user per roster. With nobody on call it adds nobody and posts No members currently on-call for … group. E17

    So a group with only a draft shift is likely to add nobody to a discussion: the resolver applies, but nobody is on call from a draft. Q10 The participant side of Sidebar is documented in Discuss an incident - configuration.

    5. On the incident form NEW

    The form link On-Call Roster and Escalation details would open who is on call for the assignment group. It is inactive on pdi2. E18 Q08
