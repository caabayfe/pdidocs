    Shifts, rosters and members - configuration

    Technical referenceReviewed 2026-10-19Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)Operator guide: Set up and maintain a shift

    The application NEW

    Shifts, rosters and members are the core of the On-Call Scheduling plugin (com.snc.on_call_rotation, scope global): the shift (cmn_rota), its rosters (cmn_rota_roster), their members (cmn_rota_member), and the helper tables behind the Add, Remove and Move forms (on_call_add_member, on_call_remove_member, on_call_swap_member). On pdi2 no shift and no group preference exists. E01 E02 Q01

    1. Roles and access NEW

    RoleGives
    rota_adminevery group's shifts, rosters, members, coverage and time off E03; the wizard module E08; group templates E40
    sn_on_call_admincontains rota_admin E04
    rota_managera group's shifts, when granted for that group E05 E06
    (none)the group's manager, and the users in the group preference's Shift managers E06

    Every write on a shift goes through OnCallSecurityNG.rotaMgrAccess(group), in the ACLs E07 and in the buttons. E13 E14 The wizard filters its group picker with the same rules. E10

    2. Creating a shift NEW

    1. Create / Edit Schedule (rota_admin) E08, or New on the shift list, which redirects to the same $oc_creation_wizard.do for anyone who passes canCreateSchedule. E09 E06
    2. The wizard calls OnCallCreationWizardUtilsSNC E10 and OnCallRotationSNC.createNewRota E11: the shift is inserted as a draft, inactive, on the 2024 schedule engine, in the group's domain; an overlapping draft is deleted with an error. E11 Q18
    3. Rosters and members are created per roster; the catch-all and the group's overlap preferences are saved from the wizard. E10

    The page itself ($oc_creation_wizard.do) is a platform page the mirror doesn't hold.

    Update Rotation Schedules (Roster) recalculates the rotation after each change. E19 The checks: Rotate every above zero and within max_rotation_repeat_count (182000) E20 E21; no monthly rotation on the old engine E20; Number of reminders at most max_rotation_reminder_count (50). E22 E23
