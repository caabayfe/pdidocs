    On-call scheduling

    Plan who in each support group is on call, see who that is now, manage your own shifts and time off, and let the on-call flows assign and escalate urgent work. No group has a shift on pdi2 yet.

    Where: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    Who this is for

    • Service desk agent or resolver — looks up who is on call for a group before calling or inviting them (the itil role, or oc_read for read-only access to the on-call calendar)
    • Major incident manager — finds the on-call people of each group a major incident needs and brings them in
    • On-call group member — a member of a support group with a shift; is on call when the roster says so, receives the on-call notifications and accepts or rejects assignments; no extra role needed
    • Shift manager — the group's manager, or a user with rota_manager named on the group's preferences; manages that group's shifts, rosters, members, time off and escalation
    • On-call administrator — rota_admin (included in sn_on_call_admin): creates, edits and deletes any shift, sets the on-call properties and the trigger rules that start the on-call flows

    What you can do

    • 1. See who is on call
      Find who is on call for a group now or at a given time, from the on-call calendars, and bring them into a discussion.
    • 2. Your shifts, preferences and time off
      See when you are on call, set how you want to be contacted, and ask for time off or cover.
    • 3. Set up and maintain a shift
      Create a group's shift, its rosters and members, and keep coverage, swaps and time off up to date.
    • 4. Set the escalation and trigger rules
      Decide who is contacted, how and in what order, and which records start the on-call flows.
    • 5. When on-call work reaches you
      What happens when an incident goes to a group with a shift, how you are told, and how to accept or reject it.
