    5. When on-call work reaches you

    For: On-call group member, Service desk agent or resolverWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    On pdi2 today NEW

    Nothing runs today. The on-call flows start only from a trigger rule, and pdi2 has none E01 Q02; they act only for groups with a shift, and pdi2 has none of those either E03. The Escalation Log is empty E02, and would stay empty even then: escalation logging is switched off. E26 E27 Q03 An incident assigned to a group is handled as usual: the group's members pick it up.

    What follows is what members and agents will see once a shift and a trigger rule exist. Setting them up is described in Set up and maintain a shift and Set the escalation and trigger rules.

    What happens when work reaches your group NEW

    1. An incident (or another task) is assigned to a group with a shift, and nobody in that group is assigned. E04

    2. The group's trigger rule starts one of the on-call flows, in the background. Any on-call flow already running on the record is cancelled first. E04

    3. The flow does one of these, as the rule says:

      FlowWhat happens to you
      On-Call: Assignthe person on call now is made the assignee, without a message E05 E07
      On-Call: Assign and Notifythe same, and that person gets an SMS E06 E07 Q16
      On-Call: Assign By Acknowledgementpeople are contacted in turn, by email, SMS or a call, until one accepts E08 E09
      On-Call: Escalations by Emailthe same, by email only E10
      On-Call: Conference Call Escalationthe people of each level are called together into a conference call E11

    4. Steps are written to the record's work notes. E04 E07 They are also kept in the Escalation Log, but only while escalation logging is on, and on pdi2 it is off. E22 E26 E27

    Accept or reject NEW

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Start. An incident is assigned to a group that has an on-call shift, and nobody in that group is the assignee yet.
    2. Trigger. The group's trigger rule starts an on-call subflow in the background. On pdi2 there are no trigger rules yet, so today nothing starts.
    3. Contact. With assign by acknowledgement, the people on call are contacted in turn, by email, text message or a call, as their preferences say.
    4. Accept. To take it, reply A C C by email or text, or press one on a call. You become the assignee, and the work notes say you acknowledged it.
    5. Reject. Reply R E J, press two, or don't answer in time, and the next person is contacted, then the next level.
    6. Catch-all. If nobody accepts, the shift's catch-all is told last: one named person, everyone on the roster, or the group's manager.

    When the flow asks you to take the work, the message tells you how to answer E12 E13 E14:

    You gotTo acceptTo reject
    An email, "INC… On-Call Assignment"reply with ACC in the message bodyreply with REJ
    An SMSreply with the message number followed by ACCthe number followed by REJ
    A callpress 1press 2
    • Accept: you become the assignee, and the work notes say … acknowledged. The escalation stops. E16
    • Reject: the next person in the escalation is contacted. E16
    • No answer: after the wait, you may get a reminder; then the next level is contacted. E09

    Your answer counts only while the record has no assignee, and you can accept only a record you can open. E16 If someone assigns the record by hand, the escalation ends. E08 E17

    Email replies work for incidents only: that is the one table the reply rule handles. E15 Q06 SMS and calls need a telephony provider for Notify; without one, only email reaches people. Q04

    When nobody accepts NEW

    The shift's catch-all is told last: one named person, everyone on the roster, or the group's manager. E09 E18 By email the subject is "Task … needs urgent attention". E19 The catch-all can still accept.

    Follow an escalation NEW

    On pdi2 escalation logging is off, so nothing below shows any escalation until it is switched on. E26 E27 Follow the record's work notes instead.

    • On the classic incident form, a Track On-Call Escalation button appears beside Assignment group when the incident has escalations; it is highlighted while one is running. E23 E24
    • On-Call Scheduling › Escalation Log lists every escalation, for itil and rota_admin. E20 You see an escalation if you can open its record, or if you are in its group. E21
    StatusMeans
    Livepeople are being contacted
    Completedsomeone accepted or was assigned, or the steps ran out
    Cancelledstopped, for example by a new on-call flow on the same record

    E22 Each contact shows its channel and the answer: Accepted, Rejected, Auto Assigned or Invalid Response. E25 Q25

    Tips

    • Answer with the exact keyword: anything else is logged as an invalid response. E25
    • Accept from the device that was contacted; replies from an address that isn't on your profile may not be matched to you. E15 E16
    • If you take an incident by hand, the other people stop being contacted. E17
