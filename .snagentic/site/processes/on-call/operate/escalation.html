    4. Set the escalation and trigger rules

    For: Shift manager, On-call administratorWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    When to use this page NEW

    A shift says who is on call. Two more things decide what happens when urgent work arrives:

    PartDecidesSet by
    The escalation policy of the shiftwho is contacted, how, how often and in what order, and who is told lastthe shift manager or the on-call administrator
    A trigger rulewhich records start the on-call flow, and which flowthe same, with limits for shift managers

    Members who want to know what reaches them should read When on-call work reaches you.

    On pdi2 today NEW

    Nothing here is in use yet: no group has a shift E01, there are no trigger rules E02, and the escalation log is empty E03. So no on-call flow has ever run. The steps below are what to do once a group has a shift (see Set up and maintain a shift).

    The default escalation NEW

    Every shift starts with a default escalation built from its rosters E04:

    • One roster: the person on call is contacted first, then the next members of the roster in turn (Rotate through members).
    • Several rosters: the on-call person of the first roster, then of the second, and so on (Rotate through rosters), for example a primary and a backup.

    Each roster sets how hard each person is tried:

    Field on the rosterMeans
    Number of remindershow many times the person is contacted before moving on (1 unless changed) E05
    Time between remindersthe wait between two contacts (15 minutes unless changed) E06
    Time before escalationthe wait before the next person E07
    Forced communication channelalways use Email, SMS or Voice for this roster E08

    Forcing a channel has a risk the form warns about: "If the on-call member does not have an SMS device they will NOT be contacted" (and the same for email). E09

    The catch-all: who is told last NEW

    When nobody accepts, the shift's Catch-all decides who hears about it E10 E11:

    Catch-allThen chooseWho is told
    Notify IndividualCatch-all memberone named person
    Notify AllCatch-all rostereveryone on that roster
    Notify Group Manager(shown)the group's manager

    Set it in the wizard's Escalation Setup step or on the shift. Catch-all wait time is how long the catch-all has to answer. On pdi2 there is no instance-wide value, so it is zero unless you set it on the shift. E12 E13

    Change the escalation NEW

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Start. Open create or edit schedule, or new on my group schedules. In the first step, select the group, name the shift, and set its days and times. You only see the groups you manage.
    2. Members. Add one or more rosters, such as a primary and a backup, with their members in the order they take turns. Each roster rotates weekly unless you change it.
    3. Escalation. Choose the catch-all: one person, everyone on a roster, or the group manager. They are told when nobody accepts.
    4. Review. Check the members and the escalation. The shift is saved as a draft, so nobody is on call from it yet.
    5. Publish. On the shift, choose publish. The shift is now live, and its rosters decide who is on call.
    6. Trigger. Finally, add a trigger rule, so that work assigned to the group starts the on-call subflow. On pdi2 there is none yet.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. Open the shift and choose Edit Escalation and Contact Preferences. On pdi2 this link replaces Edit Escalation Policy, because Notify is installed. E14 E15
    2. To go beyond the rosters, choose Override Escalation Policy on the shift and confirm "Are you sure you want to override default escalation policy with custom escalation ?". The escalation designer opens. E16 E17
    3. In the designer, build escalation sets. Each has a Table and a Condition (for example only priority 1 incidents) and an order; one set is the Default, used when no condition matches. A shift must keep one default set. E20 E21 Conditions must be standard ones, not scripts. E22
    4. Add steps to each set, one per Escalation level. A step can contact rosters, named Users, other Groups or the Group manager, on chosen Devices or a forced channel, with a Number of reminders and a Time to next step (15 minutes unless changed). E23
    5. To go back to the rosters' order, choose Reset Escalation Policy and confirm "Are you sure you want to reset custom escalation policy with default escalation ?". E18 E19

    The designer refuses two steps at the same level ("Step with escalation level - … already exists …") E24, reminders without a time between them E25, and a step that escalates back to the shift's own group or loops through other groups. E26

    A group can also have one escalation policy for several of its shifts (Apply to all Shifts, or a list of shifts): "This group already has an escalation policy defined" stops a second. E27 E28

    Contact preferences NEW

    Members choose how they want to be contacted in their own preferences (see Your shifts, preferences and time off). On the default escalation, Override user preference on the shift makes the shift's own contact attempts win instead. E29

    How people are asked NEW

    ChannelTo acceptTo reject
    Email, {number} On-Call Assignmentreply with ACCreply with REJ E30
    SMSreply with the message number and ACCthe message number and REJ E31
    Voicepress 1press 2 E32

    Rejecting moves the work to the next person in the escalation. E30 What the member sees is on When on-call work reaches you.

    Add a trigger rule NEW

    Without a trigger rule, a shift is only a calendar: nothing contacts anyone. E39

    1. Open On-Call Scheduling › Trigger Rules and choose New. Administrators and rota_manager see the module E33; anyone who manages a group may create a rule. E34

    2. Choose the Table (for example Incident), a Name and the Execution Order. E02

    3. Say when it runs E35:

      FieldChoose
      Run triggerRun once, or Every time trigger field changes with the Trigger fields
      Trigger whenCondition matches, with a Condition, or Default for the table

    4. Choose the Trigger action: Subflow, and the Trigger subflow E35 E36 E37:

      SubflowIn short
      On-Call: Assignassigns the person on call
      On-Call: Assign and Notifyassigns and tells them
      On-Call: Assign By Acknowledgementcontacts people in turn until one accepts
      On-Call: Escalations by Emailthe escalation, by email

      E38 Script is offered to administrators only. E36

    5. Make it Active and save.

    When a matching record is assigned to a group with a shift and nobody in that group is assigned yet, the rule starts the subflow, after cancelling any on-call subflow already running on the record. The steps are written to the record's work notes. E39 Only subflows are supported on this release, with the new trigger engine. E43 E44

    Limits NEW

    • A rule that fires whenever the assignment group changes applies to every group, so its Group is cleared. E41 Only an administrator can create one: others get "Security constraints prevent this operation". E40
    • A table can have only one Default rule: "Only one default trigger rule per table can be defined." E42

    Tips

    • Start with one default trigger rule on incident and the Assign By Acknowledgement subflow, so that someone confirms they have it. E38 E42
    • Always set a catch-all, and a catch-all wait time, so a missed call still reaches a person. E11 E13
    • Prefer the members' own contact preferences; force a channel only when the team agrees. E09 E29

    Not yet seen OPEN QUESTION

    1. The escalation page, the escalation designer and the trigger rule form were not opened on screen; the fields follow the records. E14 E23 E35
    2. Which tables and conditions should start the on-call flows: all incidents assigned to a group with a shift, or only urgent ones?
    3. Which subflow should be used, and should SMS and voice be offered? They need a telephony provider for Notify, which this mirror does not show.
    4. Should a catch-all wait time be set for the instance (the property has no value)? E13
