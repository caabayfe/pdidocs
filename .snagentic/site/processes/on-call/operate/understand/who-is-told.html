    When urgent work reaches a group

    These aren't notification records: the on-call flows contact people themselves, by each person's contact preferences (email, SMS or voice), or by the channel an escalation step forces. E14 E15 E17 On pdi2 the escalation log is switched off, so contacts and answers are not recorded. E18 E19 Q03 SMS and voice need a telephony provider for Notify; until one is set up, only email reaches people. Q04 See When on-call work reaches you.
