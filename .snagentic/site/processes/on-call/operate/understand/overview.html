    How on-call scheduling works

    Checked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)

    What it is for

    Some support groups need someone available outside office hours. On-call scheduling plans that person: each group can have one or more shifts E03, each shift has rosters that rotate through their members, and time off or cover replaces a member for a while. When urgent work reaches the group, the on-call flows find who is on call and contact them in turn until someone takes it.

    On pdi2 today

    The On-Call Scheduling application is installed and its menu is active, as delivered on this instance E02, but no group has a shift yet: the list of shifts is empty E01. So:

    • nobody is on call, and the on-call calendars show nothing;
    • the on-call flows never run, because they only act for groups with a shift E04;
    • inviting a group to a Sidebar discussion adds the whole group, not just who is on call E06 (see Discuss an incident with colleagues).

    Everything below describes what the platform does once a group has a shift.

    How it goes

    1. An on-call administrator or the group's shift manager creates a shift for the group, adds rosters and members, and publishes it.
    2. Members check when they are on call, set how they want to be contacted, and arrange time off or cover.
    3. An administrator adds a trigger rule: when an incident (or another task) is assigned to a group with a shift and nobody in that group is assigned yet, the rule starts an on-call subflow E04. Only subflows are supported on this release E05.
    4. The subflow creates an escalation: it assigns the person on call, or contacts the people in the escalation policy one after another, by email, SMS or voice, until one accepts.

    Where you start

    On-call scheduling is worked in the classic interface, under On-Call Scheduling in the navigator E02. The Service Operations Workspace has no on-call pages on pdi2: they need a plugin the instance doesn't have E07, and the page doesn't open E08. Start with See who is on call.
