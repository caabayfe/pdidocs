    2. Your shifts, preferences and time off

    For: On-call group memberWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    On pdi2 today NEW

    No group has a shift on pdi2 E01, so nobody has on-call time yet: My Schedule Report comes back empty, and no reminders are sent. You can already set your contact preferences, ready for when your group gets a shift.

    See when you are on call NEW

    1. Open On-Call Scheduling › My Schedule Report. E02
    2. Choose a period: today, tomorrow, this or next week, month or quarter, or your own dates. E03 Q20
    3. The list shows each period you are on call, in every group you belong to, by start time. If you are a shift's named catch-all, those periods are listed too. E03

    For who else is on call, see See who is on call.

    Set how you are contacted NEW

    Transcript (4 steps)
    1. Your shifts. My schedule report lists when you are on call, in all your groups, for the period you choose.
    2. How to reach you. In my preferences, contact attempts sets the order and the device: email, text message or a call. Contact overrides changes it for a while.
    3. When. Add an availability, so the preferences know when they apply. Until you do, the page asks you to create one.
    4. Time off. On pdi2, members can't add their own time off. Your shift manager records it, and a cover takes your shift.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. Open On-Call Scheduling › My Preferences. E04
    2. Contact Attempts: the order in which the on-call flows try to reach you, and by what: email, SMS or a call. E05 E19 E20 Q22 The numbers and address come from your user profile: your email, mobile phone or business phone. E07 E08 E09
    3. Contact Overrides: a different way to reach you for a while. E05
    4. Availability: when the preferences apply, from an existing schedule or one you define. Until you create one, the page says Please create an availability. E05 E06

    Keep the mobile number on your profile up to date: it is what an SMS or a call goes to. E08

    Reminders and your calendar NEW

    • Before your on-call time you get "On-Call Schedule: …", as the roster's reminder settings say. E10
    • Your shift manager can send you a calendar subscription, "On-Call calendar subscription for your … rotation in the … group". E11 It is a link with a personal key that your calendar app can subscribe to; don't forward it. E12

    All on-call emails are listed in Who is told what.

    Time off and cover NEW

    On pdi2 members can't record their own time off: time off is set to Not allowed E13, and no group overrides it, because no group has on-call preferences. E14 E16 If you try, you get User does not have write access for this group. E15 Ask your shift manager to record it. Q05

    Once a group allows it, you add time off on the group's on-call calendar:

    The group's settingWhat happens
    With approvalthe request waits for approval; you are emailed when it is submitted, approved or rejected E14 E17
    Without approvalyou must propose a cover; it is granted at once, and the shift managers are told E14 E15

    When time off is granted, the proposed cover is asked to take your shift. E18 The states of a request are in The life of a shift and an escalation.

    Tips

    • Set your contact attempts as soon as you join a group with a shift, so that the flows reach you the way you prefer. E05 E19
    • Check My Schedule Report for next week before you plan time away. E03
