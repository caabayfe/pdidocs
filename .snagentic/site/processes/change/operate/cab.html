    5. The change advisory board

    For: CAB member or managerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror d13485ddb833)

    Who's on it

    A CAB definition says which changes go to a board's meetings and who sits on it; pdi2 has one, Network CAB, for normal and emergency changes with category Network and risk Moderate or higher. E04 Every other change that needs CAB approval (a moderate-risk server change, for example) gets the approval from the CAB Approval definition and is decided in Approvals › My approvals, without a meeting E10 (see Who approves). Its meetings are run by a CAB manager E05; members are the board's people and groups, not a role.

    Before the meeting

    Members get an invitation with a calendar attachment; answering it records whether you come. E06

    In the meeting

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. The board. pdi2 has one change advisory board, the network CAB, for network changes of moderate risk or higher. A CAB manager runs its meetings.
    2. Invitation. Members get an invitation with a calendar attachment. Answering it records whether you come.
    3. Workbench. In the meeting, open the CAB workbench: the calendar, then the meeting.
    4. Agenda. For each change on the agenda, the board approves or rejects, or moves on with next, pause, or demote.
    5. Decision. The decision becomes the change's CAB approval.

    Download the video (MP4)

    Open Change › CAB Workbench E01: the calendar, then the meeting. E02 My CAB Meetings lists the ones you are on. E09 For each change on the agenda the board can Approve or Reject, or move on with Next, Pause or Demote. E03 When the change's approval becomes approved or rejected, its agenda item's decision and the meeting notes follow. E07 On pdi2 the meeting link opens the workspace's CAB meeting page. E08

    Tips

    • Set the change's Category to Network when it is network work: only then is it put on the Network CAB's agenda. E04
    • Members who can't attend answer the invitation with Decline, so the CAB manager knows. E06
