    1. Raise a change

    For: Change requesterWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror fcd5bcad66af)

    Where it comes from

    The change fixes…Start fromThen
    a problemthe problem's form menu: Create Normal Change, Create Standard Change or Create Emergency Change E11 E12 E13the change opens with the problem's short description, description, CI and priority, and the message "Change CHG… created"; the problem points to it E11. See Fix and resolve.
    an incidentChange › Create New (below)set the change in the incident's Related Records › Change request E14; when the change has fixed it, resolve the incident with Resolved by change E15. See Resolve an incident.

    Start it

    Transcript (5 steps)
    1. Kind. Create new under change shows a card for each kind of change. For routine work, look for a standard change first.
    2. Describe. Describe it, with the configuration item or service, the category, and the assignment group. The group is required before it leaves new.
    3. Plan. In planning, write the justification, the implementation plan, the risk and impact analysis, and the backout and test plans. Approvers read these.
    4. Schedule. Set the planned start and end dates.
    5. Risk. Press calculate risk. It isn't calculated on save. A start less than three days away makes the risk high, and the risk decides who approves.

    Download the video (MP4)

    Open Change › Create New. E01 The page Create a change request shows a card per kind: choose Normal, Emergency, a standard change or a template, then Continue. E02 For routine work, look for a standard change first (see Use a standard change).

    Plan it

    On the form E10:

    1. The top: Short description, Description, Configuration item or Service, Category, Assignment group. E03 The group is required before it leaves New. E09
    2. Planning: Justification, Implementation plan, Risk and impact analysis, Backout plan and Test plan. E04 Approvers read these.
    3. Schedule: Planned start date and Planned end date. E05

    Set the risk

    Press Calculate Risk under Related Links. E06 Risk isn't calculated on save, only when you press it. E07 A start less than three days away makes the risk High. E08 The risk decides who approves (see Who approves).

    Then request approval: Get it approved.

    Tips

    • Raising it from the problem copies the details and links both records: prefer it to a blank change when a problem exists. E11
    • Fill the plans before pressing Request Approval: approvers read them. E04
    • Plan the start at least three days ahead, or the risk becomes High. E08
