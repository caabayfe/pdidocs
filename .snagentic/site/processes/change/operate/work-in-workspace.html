    9. Work a change in the workspace

    For: Change requester, ImplementerWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)

    What the workspace gives you NEW

    The Service Operations Workspace shows a change request with a header, a side panel and a Related records tab. On a new normal change it looked like this E01 E02 E03:

    PartWhat is there
    HeaderSave; More actions with Compose Email and Delete
    Side panelRecommendations, Agent Assist, Collaborate, Attachments, Response Templates, Templates, Email template
    Related recordsAffected CIs, Impacted Services/CIs, Approvers, Change Tasks, Problems, Task SLAs, Outages

    The steps to plan, approve, implement and close are the same as on the classic form: see Raise a change, Schedule and implement and Review, close or cancel. This page covers what the workspace adds.

    Find your changes and change tasks NEW

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Find. Changes and change tasks come to you in the workspace's task lists: assigned to you, and assigned to your group.
    2. Take. Select a change and choose assign to me. It refuses if you are not in the change's group, and fills in the group if you have only one. Change tasks don't have it: set the assignee yourself.
    3. Open. The change opens with save and more actions in the header, the side panel on the right, and related records such as change tasks and approvers.
    4. Search. Agent assist searches with the short description. The change related incidents source lists the active incidents opened since the change started.
    5. Link. On such an incident, link to incident makes your change the incident's caused by.
    6. Discuss. Collaborate starts a sidebar discussion about the change. Nobody is emailed about it, so mention the people who must act.

    Download the video (MP4)

    The workspace has no list of its own for changes. Changes and change tasks come to you through the Tasks lists, which hold every kind of task:

    ListShows
    Assigned to youactive tasks assigned to you, changes and change tasks among them E04
    Assigned to your groupactive tasks of your groups that are assigned to someone else or to nobody E05

    Filter the list on the task type when you want only changes or change tasks. What the lists show for a change task hasn't been checked yet. Q27

    Take a change: Assign to me NEW

    From the list: select one or more changes in a Tasks list and choose Assign to me. You need itil or sn_change_write. E06 E07 The workspace refuses when E08:

    • you can't change Assigned to: "You do not have permission to update assigned to";
    • the change has a group you aren't in: "Assigned to user … must be member of Assignment group …";
    • the change has no group and you are in several: "… is member of multiple groups, please select one as Assignment group or add a primary group …". With one group, it is filled in for you.

    On the form: Assign to me shows on an active change that nobody is assigned to, when you can change Assigned to and are in its group. E07 If the group is required, empty, and you are in several groups, a Select Assignment Group dialog asks which. E09 The change is saved at once.

    Assign to me isn't offered for change tasks, neither in the list nor on the form. E07 Set Assigned to on the task yourself.

    Add a change task NEW

    Change tasks are listed under Related records › Change Tasks. E03 The Implementation and Post Implementation tasks are created for you when the change is implemented (see Schedule and implement).

    The workspace also has a Create Change Task action: under More actions on a saved change, for itil and sn_change_write, it opens a Create Change Task dialog and then the new task. E10 It wasn't in More actions on the change that was walked. E01 Q23

    Changing the type NEW

    If you change Type on the form, State becomes read-only until you save. E11 The script would also narrow the state choices, but only for changes without a change model. E12

    Find answers with Agent Assist NEW

    Agent Assist works for changes as for incidents (see Find answers with Agent Assist): open the Agent Assist icon in the side panel E02, and it searches with the Short description of the active change or change task. E13 E14 E15 A search needs at least three characters. E37 What differs is where it looks Q25:

    SourceSearches
    Default Sources (the default)knowledge articles, pinned articles and catalog items together E16
    Incidentsincidents created in the last 6 months, any state E22
    Change Related Incidentsactive incidents opened since the change's actual start (or planned start), not yet linked to a change as their cause E17 E18 Q02
    Open Changesactive changes created in the last 6 months E19
    Closed Changeschanges closed in the last 6 months E20
    Problemsproblems created in the last 6 months E21

    Wildcard search is off for changes, so type whole words. E16

    Link to Incident is the change's own action. On a Change Related Incidents result, it asks "Now linking CHG… to INC…", with boxes for work notes and additional comments, and then makes your change the incident's Caused by: "CHG… has been identified as a possible cause for INC…". E23 E24 Q26 Use it when the incidents started after your change and look like its result. It shows only on change requests, and only for incidents with no Caused by yet. E23

    The knowledge actions are the same as for incidents: Attach puts a link to the article in the change's Additional comments E25; Helpful, Flag Article, Full View and Order also work. There is no Copy Resolution for changes.

    The Recommendations icon next to it (itil or sn_change_write) stays empty: it needs AI Search, which is switched off. E26 E27 E28

    Discuss a change NEW

    Sidebar discussions work for changes as for incidents (see Discuss an incident with colleagues). What differs:

    On a change requestOn a change task
    Collaborate › Chat in the side panelyes E02 E29no: the Collaborate chat is only for incidents and changes E29 Q10
    Open Sidebar discussion on the Next Experience formyes E35yes E35
    Suggested participantsOpened by, Requested by, Assigned to, and the person on call for its group when on-call scheduling is in use E30none E30
    A group with on-call shiftsadds only who is on call now E38not resolved to the on-call people E38

    Nobody is emailed about a discussion on a change. The emails that tell a caller about an incident discussion go only to a record's requester, and no requester is set up for changes or change tasks. E31 E32 shared/Q03 Colleagues get no email either E33; they see the discussion in their Sidebar discussions panel, and a mention sends a push notification to the mobile app. E34

    Only people who can open the change can read its discussions. E36 Invite a business approver or a CAB member only if they can open changes.

    Tips

    • Use Assigned to your group to pick up new changes, and Assign to me to take one: it checks the group for you. E05 E08
    • After an incident starts during your window, look at Change Related Incidents in Agent Assist: it lists exactly those. E18
    • Write in the discussion that you have linked an incident: Link to Incident writes to the incident, not to the change. E24
    • Mention people in a discussion when it is urgent: outside the workspace, it's the only alert they get. E33 E34
