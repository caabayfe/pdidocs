    4. Approve as a technical approver

    For: Technical approverWhere: Service Operations Workspace, the classic incident formChecked against pdi2 on 2026-10-20 (mirror 9f3658cfb43d)

    You're asked to approve a change as a member of the team doing it (in Assess), as its manager, or as a member of the CAB Approval group (in Authorize). This page shows where to answer and what follows. See Who approves.

    In the workspace

    Transcript (4 steps)
    1. Open. Open my approvals. Earlier approvals show too, so sort or filter by state.
    2. Read. Read the change: the implementation, test and backout plans, the window, the affected services, and the risk.
    3. Approve. If it is sound, press approve.
    4. Reject. A rejection needs a comment saying why. If something is missing, reject with the question, and the requester fixes it and asks again.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. Open Approvals › My approvals in the Service Operations Workspace. E01 The list shows approvals for changes, requests and catalog tasks, yours and your delegations'.
    2. Filter or sort by State: the list has no state filter, so answered approvals show too. E01
    3. Open the approval and read the change it is for.
    4. Press Approve. E02 Or write a comment and press Reject; without a comment the workspace answers "Comments are required when rejecting an approval". E03

    On the classic form

    1. Open the approval from the email's Click here to view Approval Request link E09, or from Self-Service › My Approvals.
    2. Press Approve or Reject: the buttons show only on a requested approval that is yours or delegated to you. E04
    3. To reject, fill Comments first: it becomes mandatory when the state is Rejected E06, and the button refuses with "Comments are required when rejecting an approval". E05

    What happens next

    YouWhat follows
    Approvewhen the approvals it needs are in, the change moves on; its assignee gets the Change approved email E07
    Rejectthe assignee gets the Change rejected email E08; the change comes back to be fixed

    If the change is on a CAB meeting's agenda, its agenda item follows your answer. E10

    What to check

    The implementation, test and backout plans, the window, the affected services and the risk. If something is missing, reject with the question: the requester fixes it and asks again.

    Tips

    • When you reject, write the question you need answered: that is what the requester fixes.
    • Prefer the workspace to an email reply: it shows the change's plans before you decide.
    • Approvals for requests and catalog tasks appear in the same list (see Manage a request). E01
