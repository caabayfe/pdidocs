    Order something

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    Ordering takes a minute: open the item, answer its questions, order, confirm. You then get a request number and an email, and the request appears in My Requests.

    Before you start

    • Find the item: search for it, browse a topic, or use a quick link such as Request Standing Desk (see Find what you need).
    • Check its Price and Delivery Time on its page.
    • For a new colleague, use the New Hire bundle instead (see Find what you need).

    Order it

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. Find it. Search Employee Center for what you need, or browse the topics on the home page.
    2. The item. The item's page shows its price, the delivery time, and its questions. Answer them, and set the quantity if you need more than one.
    3. Order. Press order now. Some items say submit or request. There is no cart: each order is one item.
    4. Confirm. Confirm the order, and add delivery details or special instructions if useful.
    5. Status. You land on your request's status, with its number, the expected delivery, and its stage.
    6. Later. Not ready? Save as draft keeps it under My Requests, to finish later.

    Download the video (MP4)

    1. Open the item. Its page shows the description, the price, the Delivery Time and its questions. The Standard Laptop, for example, shows $1,100.00 + $100.00 Annually, 9 Days, and asks which optional software to add and anything else you need.
    2. Answer the questions. Required ones have a red asterisk. Until they're answered, the Required information box beside the button lists what's missing. Set Quantity if you need more than one.
    3. Press Order Now. Some items say Submit or Request instead. There is no cart: each order is one item. To order several items in one go, use a bundle such as New Hire (see Find what you need).
    4. Confirm. The Order Confirmation window shows who it's for and lets you add Delivery Information and Special Instructions, such as the room where it should go. Confirm to order.
    On the item pageWhat it does
    Order Now / Submit / Requeststarts the order; you confirm in the next window
    Save as Draftkeeps your answers under My Requests › Drafts, to finish later
    the heartadds the item to My favorites

    What happens next

    You land on your request's status page: its number (REQ…), what you ordered, the expected delivery date and its stage. You also get the email "Request REQ… was created". If the item needs approval, it waits first for the manager of the person it's for (see Who approves). Follow it in My Requests: see Your request's statuses and emails.

    For someone else

    At checkout you can't change who the order is for: it's for you. Items that are often for someone else ask it as a question, such as Requested for on Request DocuSign access, already filled with your name. Change it there before you submit.

    Tips

    • Not ready? Save as Draft, and finish it later from My Requests › Drafts.
    • Write useful Special Instructions: where to deliver, when you are in, who can receive it.
    • Order one thing per request: each order is one item anyway, and each is followed on its own.
