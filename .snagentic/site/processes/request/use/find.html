    Find what you need

    Last checked 2026-10-20

    The catalog is the list of everything IT can give you: devices, software, access and services. Each thing you can order is an item, with its own page, price and questions. You find items in three ways: search, browse by topic, or start from a bundle that orders several at once.

    Transcript (6 steps)
    1. New or broken. First, is it something new, or something that stopped working? Something new is a request. Something broken is an incident.
    2. Search or browse. Search for it, or browse a topic such as computers, and filter by requests.
    3. Bundles. For a new colleague, the new hire bundle orders the laptop, phone, email and VPN together.
    4. The item. The item's page shows the price, the delivery time, and its questions. Required ones have a red asterisk.
    5. Approval. If the total is over one thousand dollars, the request needs an extra approval.
    6. Order. Then order it.

    Is it a request or a problem?

    You…It's a…Use
    need something you don't have yet: a laptop, software, accessrequestan item from the catalog (this page)
    have something that worked and now doesn'tincidentReport an incident

    Some forms in the catalog report problems rather than order things: Create Incident, Report Outage, Report Performance Problem and Password Reset. They create an incident, which you follow the same way. Only IT staff see Password Reset; to reset your own password, see Reset or change your password.

    Search

    Type the thing you need in the search box at the top: laptop, monitor, VPN, Adobe. The results show items with their price, and help articles. Choose Catalogs on the left to see only items. Search is the quickest way when you know roughly what it's called.

    Browse by topic

    Open Help center › Technology services, then a subtopic such as Hardware, then Computers. Set Filter by to Requests to see only what you can order, and Sort by to A-Z. The popular topics on the home page (Computers, Software, Accessories…) are shortcuts to the same pages.

    Bundles: order several things at once

    A bundle asks a few questions and then orders the right set of items for you in one go (see Order something for how ordering works).

    • New Hire: for a manager preparing for someone who joins. It asks the hiring manager, the team, whether they work remotely, the office, and the device and phone type. It always adds an email account and VPN access, a desk when they aren't remote, and the laptop, monitor and phone that fit.
    • Request Developer Project Equipment: hardware and software for a development project. It asks "Android or iOS?" and "What software do you require?". It isn't in a topic: find it by search.

    Before you order

    On an item's page, check its Price, its Delivery Time and its questions; Order something walks you through it.

    Who has to say yes depends on the item and the total:

    • a request worth more than $1,000 in total needs one more approval; below that it's approved automatically;
    • most devices and access items are approved by the manager of the person it's for, then by that person's department head.

    Tips

    • Mark items you order often with the heart, to find them in My favorites.
    • Ordering for a new colleague? Use New Hire instead of ordering each item.
