    Request management

    When you need something new (a laptop, software, access, a service), you request it from the catalog in Employee Center; it is approved if needed, fulfilled by the right teams and delivered.

    What you can do

    • Find what you need
      Browse the catalog by category, search it, or start from a bundle such as New Hire.
    • Order something
      Find the item in Employee Center, answer its questions and order it.
    • Follow your request
      See where your request is, add information, and know what to expect.
    • Your request's statuses and emails
      What each status and stage means, and every email you get, from order to delivery.
    • Approve a request
      Approve or reject what your team member ordered.
