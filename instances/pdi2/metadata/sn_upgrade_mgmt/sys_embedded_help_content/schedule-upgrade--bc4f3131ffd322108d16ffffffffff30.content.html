What to expect

  • Upgrade Scheduled: Your upgrade is scheduled and will begin at the specified date and time.
  • Pre-Upgrade Notifications: You will receive notifications as the upgrade time approaches to remind you of the scheduled event.
  • Monitoring the Upgrade: Once the upgrade starts, you will be able to monitor its progress in real-time through the upgrade status dashboard.
  • System Availability: Be aware that there may be periods of limited availability or performance impacts during the upgrade process.
  • Post-Upgrade Actions: After the upgrade is complete, you will need to review the upgrade summary and address any post-upgrade tasks, such as verifying functionality and reviewing skipped records.
