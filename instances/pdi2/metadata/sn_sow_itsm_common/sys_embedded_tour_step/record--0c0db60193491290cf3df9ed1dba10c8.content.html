The dashboard section provides a summary of various assignments such as incidents and catalog tasks for Tier 1 and additional problem and change tasks for Tier 2. You can get an overview of catalog tasks and unresolved incidents assigned to you or your team, and also the tasks delegated to you by other agents.

Important:

  • When you select any part of the donut, the associated records are displayed in a list view below the donuts.
  • When you select the number at the center of the donut, all records associated with that card are displayed in a list view below the donuts.
