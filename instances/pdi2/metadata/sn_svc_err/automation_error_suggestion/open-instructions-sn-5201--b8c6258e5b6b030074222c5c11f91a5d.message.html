The probe cannot be found, or some logic is preventing it from being located.

Remediation choices:

  • Verify that the probe exists.
  • Verify that the probe should be triggered.
  • Verify that no ACLs are blocking it from being triggered.
  • Verify that there are no Business Rules preventing it from being found from GlideRecord.

Troubleshoot the problem as described in KB1142301: Probe not found if the above remediations didn't work.
