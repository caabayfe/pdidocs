Unload Data in a Form You Can Use

You've seen how ServiceNow can show you just the information you need. Now we'll show you ways to convert that information to other useful formats. We'll continue to use Incidents for this example, so navigate back to Incident > All.

This time, right-click anywhere in the list header. You'll see four "Export" options: Excel, CSV, XML and PDF. Selecting any of these will convert the list into the chosen format. Depending on your browser, you'll then be prompted to either save or open the resultant file. The best thing is that all this takes less time than it probably just took you to read this paragraph.
