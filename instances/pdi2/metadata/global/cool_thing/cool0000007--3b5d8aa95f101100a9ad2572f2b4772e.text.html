Natural Language Searching

In ServiceNow, you can easily search for specific text across most fields. Navigate back to Incident > All and Search "for text".

Type "network" in the search box, and hit [Enter] to see the results. ServiceNow returned all records containing the word "network", regardless of where that text actually appeared. You don't need to specify which field to search. You don't even have to know which fields are available.
