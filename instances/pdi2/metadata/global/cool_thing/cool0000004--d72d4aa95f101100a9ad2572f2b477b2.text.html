One-click Sorting

A great thing about ServiceNow is that it behaves like you expect it to. Need to sort on a field? Just click the field header. Want to reverse the sort? Click it again. You can also right-click a field header to select an ascending or descending sort directly.

To define a more complex sort (with primary and secondary fields, for example), click the filter toggle next to the list breadcrumbs, and click the "Add Sort" button. For two-field sorting, click "Add Sort" again to add a second field. Set the primary sort field first, secondary field next, and specify the sort direction for each. When ready, click "Run":
