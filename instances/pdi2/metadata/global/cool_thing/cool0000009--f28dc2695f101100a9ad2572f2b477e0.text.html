View Reports

Reports may seldom be exciting, but they are certainly essential. Fortunately, ServiceNow makes them easy. Let's view one now. Navigate to Reports > View / Run, type "Incidents" in the search box, and click "Open Incidents by Category". This report can show you where your people are spending their time and energy.
