Service Operations Workspace - Learning

Use Service Operations Workspace Learning to review learning courses and paths and complete them.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_wfo_cfg_itsm.employee

Procedure

  1. Select the Overview tab to analyze the status of your learning tasks and recommendations.
  2. Select the Discover tab to get recommended learning, filter and find course catalogs that are relevant to you, and analyze learning task details.
  3. Select the My learning tab to analyze tasks that are pending completion and when they're due.
