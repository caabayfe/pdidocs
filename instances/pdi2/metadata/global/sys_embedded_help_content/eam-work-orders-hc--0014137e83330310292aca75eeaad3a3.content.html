Work order details

View details for your work order and corresponding work order tasks.

Use the following tabs to view and manage your work order:
  • Details: View details for your work order.
  • Work Order Tasks: Create and view work order tasks for your work order.
  • Part Requirements: Create and view asset or part requirements for your work order.
  • Checklist items: Create and view checklist items for your work order.
  • Requested Items: View all assets and parts that you need to source for your work order.
  • Pick Up Asset Tasks: View pick up tasks for the enterprise asset that is associated with your work order.
  • Attached Knowledge: View attached knowledge articles that are related to your work order.
