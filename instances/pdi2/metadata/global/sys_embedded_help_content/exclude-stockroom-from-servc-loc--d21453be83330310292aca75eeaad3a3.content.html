Exclude a stockroom from service locations

Exclude a stockroom from service locations so that the stockroom isn't displayed in the Stockrooms missing service locations important actions card in the Inventory view.

Before you begin

Role required: inventory_admin

About this task

Note: You can't exclude a stockroom that already has associated service locations. You must first remove the associated service locations and then exclude the stockroom from service locations.

Procedure

  1. From the list of available stockrooms, select the stockroom that you want to exclude from service locations.
  2. On the Details tab, select the Exclude from service locations check box.
  3. Select Save.
