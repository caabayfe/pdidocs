Associate an asset

Add all the assets that are involved in a safety incident or observation.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_ohs_im.manager or sn_ohs_im.agent

Procedure

  1. In the Asset field, select the asset that was involved.
    The fields related to the selected asset are auto-populated from the asset record.
  2. In the Asset involvement field, enter the type of its involvement in the incident or observation.
  3. Optional: If required, add more assets that are involved.
