Create, modify, or delete a schedule entry

The Schedule Entries related list contains the definitions of the time periods that you want to include in or exclude from the schedule.

  • To create or modify a schedule entry:
    1. Enter or modify the entry Name.
    2. Enter or modify the schedule entry fields.
    3. Click Submit or Update.
  • To delete a schedule entry, click Delete.
