Incident categories

Classifying incidents by categories and subcategories improves the clarity and granularity of report data. The categories and subcategories can also be used by the platform to automatically assign an incident to a specific fulfillment group to work on it.

Review the default incident categories and sub-categories to determine whether they align with your business needs.

To modify or delete a default category or sub-category, click the corresponding choice in the list.

To add a new category, click New.
