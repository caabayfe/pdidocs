Discovery performance metrics aggregated by status

Use the roll-up by status data to ensure that the processing times for probes/patterns and sensors remain consistent for a specific Discovery.
  1. Sort the list by Discovery status to see the aggregated processing times for the probes and patterns that ran during a specific Discovery.
  2. Filter by a specific status to display metrics available for probes and patterns that ran in that status.
  3. Open a record to see the probe/pattern statistics for the selected status.
