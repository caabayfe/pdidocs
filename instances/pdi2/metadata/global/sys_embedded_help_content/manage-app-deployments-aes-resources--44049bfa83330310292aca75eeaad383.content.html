Managing app deployments in AES

Admins can monitor and gatekeep app development in the pipeline using App Engine Studio (AES) and the App Engine Management Center (AEMC) dashboard.

When the citizen developer has defined the data model, experience, automation, and security, it is time for the AES admin to submit the app for production deployment in the AEMC. The app is then deployed to predefined instances where it is tested, staged, approved, and deployed to your production environment for final publishing.
