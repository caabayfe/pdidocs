Asset analytics view

Get a detailed view of all your hardware assets, their overall performance, and the total cost of ownership (TCO) of your assets.

Select any widget or chart to view in detail and take the required actions. For details on the widgets, scroll down to the Asset total cost of ownership table.

You can filter and narrow down your results by selecting a location, stockroom, and model category.

Note: TCO reports support domain separation when Domain Extension Installer (com.glide.domain.msp_extensions.installer) and Domain Support (com.snc.pa.domain_support) plugins are activated.
