Create a Workplace Contract Service

Create workplace contracts and specify the contract-related details such as the type of contract, the terms and conditions, assets involved and more.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_wsd_la.workplace_central_user, sn_wsd_la.manager or sn_wsd_la.admin

Procedure

  1. In the Workplace Contract section, select the Contract Model and specify the Contract number.
  2. In the Financial section, specify financial details.
  3. In the Terms and conditions section, specify the legal information in the contract.
  4. Click Save.
