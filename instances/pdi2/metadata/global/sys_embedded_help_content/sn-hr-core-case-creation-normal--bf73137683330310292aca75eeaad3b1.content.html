HR case creation

  1. After successfully searching for the employee requesting HR services, fill in the employee information and case details.
    FieldDescription
    COESelect the Center of Excellence associated with the HR case you are creating. Selecting a COE filters the choices for HR service.

    You can also select an HR service first and the associated COE populates.

    HR serviceThe HR service the employee inquired or requested. HR services filter by:
    • COE selected.
    • HR criteria for the Subject person.
    • Limit services filter on the Case Creation Configuration form.
    Opened forThe person requesting the HR service.

    When you make an HR service request for yourself, the Subject person and Opened for person are the same.

    It can also be the person requesting the HR service on behalf of an employee, such as a manager.

    Subject personThe person the HR service request is for.
    • Click Create Case when finished.
