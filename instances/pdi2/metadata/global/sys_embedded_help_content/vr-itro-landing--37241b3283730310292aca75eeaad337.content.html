Monitoring your remediation progress

Click a card to view more vulnerability information. Alternatively, click a remediation task record (VUL) on this page to open it. Use remediation tasks, preferred solutions, and vulnerable CIs records to monitor your remediation progress, view important vulnerability data, and resolve vulnerabilities with UI actions.

Monitoring your remediation progress and viewing vulnerability data

After you click a card, view more vulnerability information:

Assigned remediation tasks
Open a task record and view your remediation progress and review vulnerability data for tasks and vulnerable items (VIs) assigned to you and your groups. Click the Overview, Solutions, Details, and Change Requests related items links to see more information. Use the UI actions to help you fix vulnerabilities.
Preferred solutions on vulnerable items (VIs)
View the preferred solutions and other related information that you can use to fix a vulnerability assigned to you or your groups. Open a record (VS) to see details such as associated CVEs and links to solutions.
Vulnerable CIs
See the configuration items (CIs) that are assigned to you or your groups that have VIs. After its initial population, this scorecard shows updated counts of your vulnerable CIs after new VIs that have vulnerabilities are added, or when there are state changes to existing VIs.

On the left of the page, click the Home icon to return to the this page.
