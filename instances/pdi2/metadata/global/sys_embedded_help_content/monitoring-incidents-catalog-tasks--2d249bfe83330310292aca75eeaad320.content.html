Monitoring assignments assigned to you or your group in the tier-1 landing page

From the Overview section, get an overview of incidents and catalog tasks assigned to you or your group. Prioritize these tasks and provide resolution.

First-level cards

When you click View all for a card, all records associated with that card are displayed as second-level cards in a grid view or a list view.

When you click any donut area of a first-level card, the associated incident or catalog task details are displayed as second-level cards in a list or grid view.

Second-level cards

For second-level cards, you can perform the following actions:

  • Reassign or assign the records
  • Open the list view in a new tab
  • Download the list view in various file formats or share them through email
  • Edit the columns selected for display in the list view
  • Reset the column width in the list view
