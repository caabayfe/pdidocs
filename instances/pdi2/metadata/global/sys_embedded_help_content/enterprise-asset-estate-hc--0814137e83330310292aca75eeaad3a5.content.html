Enterprise asset estate

Create and manage enterprise assets in the Enterprise asset estate view. You can also view details such as asset lifecycle by state, disposed assets this year, and asset requests.

You can create assets for enterprise, construction, facility, retail, industrial, medical, tactical equipment, transportation, and wearable.

Learn about important asset related actions that you need to perform and follow through on them. For example, you maybe missing a PO number, an asset tag or asset function.

You can narrow your results by using the Location, Stockroom, and Model category filters.
