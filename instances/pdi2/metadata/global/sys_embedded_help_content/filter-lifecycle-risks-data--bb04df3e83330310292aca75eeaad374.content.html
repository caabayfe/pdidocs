Technology Portfolio: Filter technology lifecycle risks data

Apply filters to refine technology lifecycle risks and to see technology lifecycle risks only for the production instances.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_apm.apm_analyst

Procedure

  1. On the Home page, select Technology Portfolio tab.
  2. Select the radio button for 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months, or 18 months to filter the data.
    You can see how many business applications, application services, hardware models, software products or servers are at risk of End of Support, End of Extended Support or End of Life occurring over the selected time period.
  3. Select the toggle button beside Show only production instances to see the lifecycle risks only for the production instances.
  4. Select View all technology lifecycle risks to view the entire list of lifecycle risks across all business applications.
