Target

Targets are goals your organization wants to achieve. Targets show the difference between the desired score at a certain date and the actual score of an indicator.

Targets are usually created and changed in either the classic Analytics Hub or in KPI Details on a Workspace Experience. As an administrator, you can also add or edit a Target record directly here.

All targets apply to a specific indicator. You can apply targets to specific combinations of breakdown element and time series on that indicator.

Targets can apply either globally or personally. Personal targets have a specific owner. As an administrator, you can change the owner of a personal target.

By default, a target is active from its start date until it is superceded by a new target value for the same indicator/breakdown element/time series combination. You can override this and manually set a target as active or not.

All current, past, and future values for the target are listed on the target record. In a separate list, you can control which users receive email notifications when the status of a target changes.
