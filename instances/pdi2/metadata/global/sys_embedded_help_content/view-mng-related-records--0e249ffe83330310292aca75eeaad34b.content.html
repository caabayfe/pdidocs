View and manage related records

View and manage the related records that are associated with the incident record.

Before you begin

Role required: itil

About this task

The Related records tab displays all the entity records or items associated with the incident grouped under the related list. The related lists include Task SLAs, Affected CI, Impacted Services/CI, Problem, Change, and so on.

Procedure

  1. On the Incident record, click the Related Records tab.
  2. Click each related list and view the related records associated with the incident record.
  3. On each related list, use the following options as required.
