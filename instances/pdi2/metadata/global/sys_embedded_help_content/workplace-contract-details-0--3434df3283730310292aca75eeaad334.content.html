Workplace Contract details

View all the details related to the Workplace Contract.

You can check various details of the contract. The details are displayed under various tabs as follows:
  • Details: View details of the contract such as the contract model, the vendor, the currents state of the contract, the start and end date and more. You can also see the financial-related and Renewal-related details.
  • Workplace Contract Services: View details of contract services if there are any. Know the cost of the service per year and what type of service is it. To create a contract service use the New option.
  • Contract locations: Location of the contract.
  • Assets Covered: View details of the assets covered under the contract. Know the location of the asset and the date when it was added and removed.
  • Approval History: View approval-related information.
  • Contract History: Know when the contract has started and ended previously.
  • Documents: View documents related to the contract and more details. You can remove or link a document.
