Success portal view

Track the progress of your Hardware Asset Management (HAM) application with success goals, assign activities to track the success of your goals, and mature your HAM program with predefined maturity items.

Success portal helps you perform the following actions:
  • Track the progress of your HAM application through success goals.
  • Track the progress of your success goals by creating success activities.
  • Mature your HAM program with predefined maturity items.

Select any widget or chart to view the performance of your application.

Note: The Success portal supports domain separation when the Performance Analytics - Domain Support plugin (com.snc.pa.domain_support) is activated. To filter the data by domain, select a domain at the top of the dashboard.
