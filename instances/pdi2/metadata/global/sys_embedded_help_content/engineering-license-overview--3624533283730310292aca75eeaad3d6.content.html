Engineering license overview

Keep track of your engineering applications license position and usage by viewing product usage reports in the Engineering license overview dashboard.

View reports on normalized products and publishers that belong to engineering applications such as AutoCAD, GIS. You can view reports on specific metrics for a product or publisher such as the total cost, potential savings, top denied products and users.

To narrow your results based on products or publishers, use the Filter by list in the left-hand corner of the dashboard. You can further narrow your results based on the date, user, or license server.
Note: Only products and publishers that belong to engineering applications and are listed in the Engineering Application License [samp_eng_app_license] table appear in the filter. If no product or publisher is selected, the cumulative data for all products and publishers that belong to engineering applications appear.

All the reports are updated daily or whenever a new reconciliation result is available.
