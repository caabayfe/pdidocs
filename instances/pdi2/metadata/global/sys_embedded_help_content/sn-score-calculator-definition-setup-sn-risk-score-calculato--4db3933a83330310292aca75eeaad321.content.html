Risk Transformation Criteria for Operational Technology Obsolescence Management

Roles required: sn_risk_score_calc.admin role or sn_risk_score_calc.user

There is already a default Risk transformation criteria, if you want to modify the existing Risk Transformation Criteria or add a new Criteria, follow the steps below:

  1. To modify the Risk transformation criteria of a Risk Definition, configure the details of a transformation criteria in the Score transformation criterias section.
  2. To create new Risk Transformation, Navigate to All > Risk score calculator > Risk calculator definitionOn the form, fill in the fields.
  3. Open the Risk calculator definition where the Risk transformation criteria need to be added.
  4. Select the Calculation factor related list.
  5. Open the Risk factor where the Risk transformation criteria needs to be added.
  6. Select New in the Score transformation criterias section.
  7. This opens an empty form to provide the details of Risk transformation criteria.
  8. Provide the following details for Risk transformation criteria: Display text, score, field value.
  9. Select Update.
