Monitoring incidents that need immediate attention

Analyze the status of incidents that have a critical priority and those that have not been closed in the last 24 hours.

Happening Now

Identify the most urgent issues and escalations that may need your immediate attention so that it can be resolved quickly. Monitor the number of incidents that have breached the SLAs or at the risk of a breach and any reported escalations.

The Happening Now section displays the number of incidents for the following categories:
  • Open P1 Incidents: incidents with the highest priority that are not yet assigned to agents.
  • Incident SLA Breached: incidents where the service level agreement has been breached.
  • Incident Not Updated in 24 Hours: incidents that have not been worked on in the last 24 hours.
  • Incident SLA at Risk: the number of incidents with SLAs that are at the risk of breaching soon.
  • Escalations: incidents that have been flagged because those issues were not addressed in a timely manner.
