Create a business calendar filter option

Define date and time filtering options for a business calendar. These filtering options are in addition to the standard date and time filtering in the Now Platform.

  1. On the form, fill in the fields.
    Period
    Number of periods that should be traversed forward, backward, or both, for the business calendar.

    If the business calendar reflects a week:

    • Provide a filter option for the current week by entering 0.
    • Provide a filter option for a previous period by entering a negative number.
    • Provide a filter option for a future period by entering a positive number.
    Calendar
    Name of the business calendar that you want the filter options to belong to.
  2. Select Submit.

You can only associate a business calendar with a single filter option. To create multiple filtering options, you have to create multiple filtering option records, each with its own period entry.
