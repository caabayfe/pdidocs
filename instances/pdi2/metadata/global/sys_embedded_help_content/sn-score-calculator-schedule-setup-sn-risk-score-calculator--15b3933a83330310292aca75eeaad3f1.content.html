Risk Calculator Schedule for Operational Technology Obsolescence Management

Roles required: sn_risk_score_calc.admin role or sn_risk_score_calc.user

There is already a default Risk calculator schedule, if you want to modify the existing Risk calculator schedule or add a new Risk calculator schedule, follow the steps below:

  1. To modify an existing Risk calculator schedule criteria of a Risk Definition, select the info icon beside the schedule.
    Configure the form fields as per your requirement and update the Risk calculator schedule.
  2. To create new Risk Calculator schedule, Navigate to All > Risk score calculator > Risk calculator definitionOn the form, fill in the fields.
  3. Open the Risk calculator definition where the new Risk calculator schedule need to be added.
  4. Select the search icon.
  5. Select the New button.
  6. Select the Calculation factor related list.
  7. Open the Risk factor where the Risk transformation criteria needs to be added.
  8. Select New in the Score transformation criterias section.
  9. This opens an empty form to provide the details of Score calculator schedule.
  10. Provide the following details for Risk calculator schedule: Name, Description, Source, primary table, source ID column.
  11. Select Update.
