Gantt view of TPM and TRM lifecycle timelines

You can view the TPM and TRM life cycle timelines of business applications, and their associated application services like software products and hardware models, in the form of a Gantt chart.

The TPM and TRM application timelines can be viewed in the following timeline views, in the Gantt chart.
  • Day
  • Week
  • Month
  • Quarter
  • Year
  • Autofit
    Note: Autofit is selected by default.

TPM view

The TPM view of the Gantt chart displays the following phase related information:
  • End of support
  • End of extended support
  • End of life

TRM view

The TRM view of the Gantt chart displays the following phase related information information:
  • TRM phase
  • Start date
  • End date
