Engineering License Overview dashboard

Use the Engineering License Overview dashboard to gain insights into license usage for all your engineering applications. View reports on product usage, license consumption, denials, total spend, and optimization opportunities.

Overview

View your current software spend based on reconciling the software entitlements. You can also view your potential savings generated by reallocating or returning un-used or under utilized licenses.

You can also view the top used products, top active users, top idle users, top denied products and users, and products usage by location.

Click on any pie chart to see additional details. For example, click on the Top Idle Users pie chart to see details on a particular user such as the total idle duration (duration refers to the amount of time the product is used or not), session duration, usage date.

License Usage Summary tab

View your license usage daily trend. You can apply filters for metric types or license management servers.

The blue line indicates the total quantity of licenses allocated to a product or publisher. The green line indicates the consumption of licenses and the red line indicates the denials for that license type.

You can also see the list of all licenses that exist in your environment, along with the license server that hosts each license, and their expiration dates.

Denials Summary tab

View denials by products and license servers. You can analyze the denials further by date, by users or license servers.
