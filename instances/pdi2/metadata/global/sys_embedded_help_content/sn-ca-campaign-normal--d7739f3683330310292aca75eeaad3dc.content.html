Manage campaigns

Table 1. Content Automation - Campaigns
FieldsDescription
StateThe status of the campaign. Read only.
  • Draft: The status when a campaign is first created. When content and bundles are attached to the campaign, the Publish button appears in the form header.
  • Published: Activates all content for an audience. Once a campaign is published, settings, audience, and content cannot be modified. You cannot pause a campaign once the state is Published.
    Note: The campaign must be in the Published state to launch based in the start time. Scheduled jobs and properties affect when and how a campaign is launched.
AudienceThe audience the campaign is directed to. Audiences are defined from Manage Audiences under Content Delivery.
Note: After a campaign is in the Published state, users can no longer be added.
Tracking onIndicates that analytics for your campaign are being captured.
Tracking profileIdentifies the profile to use for content automation analytics. Profiles determine what users to exclude in data capture based on the country they reside.
