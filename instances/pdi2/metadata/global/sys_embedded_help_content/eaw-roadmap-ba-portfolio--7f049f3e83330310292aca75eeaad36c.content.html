View a roadmap of a business application from Portfolio page

View the roadmap of your business applications in the Strategic Planning Workspace and align them with the organization's strategy. Creating a portfolio plan helps you plan, prioritize, and roadmap the work for your business application.

Before you begin

Ensure that the Strategic Planning plugin (com.sn_apw_advanced) (v4.0.2 or later) is installed.

Role required: sn_apm.apm_user and sn_align_core.apw_user

Procedure

  1. Select the More Actions menu ()
  2. Select View roadmap.
    You’re navigated to the Planning page of the Strategic Planning Workspace. A temporary portfolio plan is created in the Strategic Planning Workspace with all the planning items that are associated with the business capability, sub-capabilities, and associated business applications.
    Note: The temporary portfolio plan is meant for preview purpose only. To plan, prioritize, and roadmap the work for your business capability, copy the temporary portfolio plan or create one.
  3. Optional: Copy the portfolio plan for your business capability to plan, prioritize, and roadmap the work for your business capability.
