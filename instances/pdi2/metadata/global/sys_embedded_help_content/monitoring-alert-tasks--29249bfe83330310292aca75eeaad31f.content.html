Monitoring alerts assigned to you or your group

From the Overview section, get an overview of alerts assigned to you or your group. Prioritize these and provide resolution.

Alert cards

  • Alerts assigned to you: Displays all alerts assigned to you.
  • Assigned team alerts: Displays all alerts assigned to members of your team.
  • Unassigned team alerts: Displays all alerts assigned to your team, but not assigned to a specific team member.
  • Monitored services: Displays all services monitored by Service Operations Workspace
  • Open alerts: Displays all alerts that are in an Open state.

When you click Show all records for a card, all records associated with that card are displayed in a list view underneath the cards.

When you click any donut area of an alert card, the associated details are displayed in a list view underneath the cards.
