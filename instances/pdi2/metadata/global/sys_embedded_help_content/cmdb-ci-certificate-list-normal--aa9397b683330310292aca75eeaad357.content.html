List of unique certificates

Certificate Inventory and Management allows Discovery to automatically scan for certificates on specific ports through your existing CI-based Discovery schedules. The Unique Certificate [cmdb_ci_certificate] table is then populated with the TLS certificate chains, signed by the certificate authority and root.

After certificates have been discovered, a scheduled job automatically checks the Unique Certificate [cmdb_ci_certificate] table for expiring and expired certificates. Certificate tasks and incidents are then created and assigned. You can also manually request new certificates and renew certificates.
