Open schedule view

View and edit reservations created in your workplace using the Schedule view in Event planner.

Select Open schedule view beside Active reservations to open a schedule view that displays all the locations of your organization. If you had already applied a location filter on the schedule view in the past, the filter is retained and the locations are displayed accordingly.

The schedule view provides a clear calendar-based view of the reservations and the reserved room. The calendar displays all the reservations created on a space for the entire day or week depending on the time-line that you selected. You can view reservations created in any campus, building, or floor of your organization.
