Major Incident properties

PropertyUsage
Create major incident from candidateProvides option to promote the major incident candidate to a major incident or create a new major incident record and associate the candidate as a child record.
PIR export ui page urlProvides option to associate custom UI page to export post incident report.
Major Incident Management Group (sys_id) to whom the Major Incident should be re-assigned on promotion to a 'Major Incident'Provides option to auto-assign a major incident to different assignment groups based on the actions taken on the incident and also on conditions related to the assignment group.
