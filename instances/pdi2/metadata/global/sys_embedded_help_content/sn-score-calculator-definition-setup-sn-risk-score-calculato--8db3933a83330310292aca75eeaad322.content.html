Risk Definition for Operational Technology Obsolescence Management

Role required: sn_risk_score_calc.admin role or sn_risk_score_calc.user

Perform the following steps to modify or create a Risk Definition:

  1. To modify an existing Risk Definition, configure the form fields according to your requirement.
  2. To create new Risk Definition, navigate to All > Risk score calculator > Risk calculator definition > New.
  3. Provide details in the Risk Calculator Definition empty form.
  4. Select Update.
