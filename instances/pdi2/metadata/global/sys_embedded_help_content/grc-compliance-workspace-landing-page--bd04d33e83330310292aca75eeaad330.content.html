Monitoring policy and compliance tasks

The Compliance Workspace provides real-time data related to policies, control objectives, controls, issues, and policy exceptions. Monitor this data to know the high-risk status of non-compliant authority documents, policies, and entities, and fulfil the tasks to accomplish your goals.

Compliance Management overview

Components in this section display the number of authority documents, policies, and entities that are compliant and non-compliant. Use the list to ascertain the least compliant ones. Click the link to take a detailed view of each of the authority documents, policies, and entities and complete the necessary task to mitigate the non-compliant risk.

Control assurance

Components in this section give you an overview of control tests, indicators and attestations that are ongoing and overdue. Click the number count to take you to a list of that component.

Tracking

Components in this section help you to track the issues that are open and overdue, acknowledgement campaigns that are ongoing and with overdue responses, and policy exceptions that open, with overdue approvals, and those that would expire in the next seven days.
