Review major incident notifications

Creating an email notification involves specifying when to send it, who receives it, what it contains, and if it can be delivered in an email digest.

  1. To create a new notification, click New and complete the form sections.
  2. Fill in the fields at the top of the Notification form, as appropriate.
  3. Fill in the fields on the When to send tab.
  4. Fill in the fields on the Who will receive tab.
  5. Fill in the fields on the What it will contain tab.
