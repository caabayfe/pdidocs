Pallets

Pallets are used to track and manage assets in your inventory as a group. You can easily move a group of assets between locations or dispose of them as a group. View the list of pallet records. Search and select the record to view the details. You can also export the list or create a pallet asset.

You can select New to create a pallet asset. After creating a pallet asset, you can add base, hardware, bundle, consumables, and other pallet assets to the pallet. The predefined pallet types are pallet, bin, box, and container.
