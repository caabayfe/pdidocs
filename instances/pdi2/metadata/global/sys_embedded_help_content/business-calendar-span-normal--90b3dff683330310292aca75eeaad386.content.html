Create a business calendar entry

Create business calendar entry records that define the individual time periods that make up a business calendar. You create a business calendar entry for every individual time span in the calendar.

  1. On the form, fill in the fields.
    Name
    Unique reference name for the business calendar entry.
    Start
    Start date and time for the business calendar period.
    End
    End date and time for the business calendar period.
  2. Select Submit.
