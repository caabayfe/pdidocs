Understand machine learning business value

Once you have successfully deployed your use case, you can use the Predictive Intelligence dashboard to communicate the business value that implementing machine learning brings to your organization.

The Predictive Intelligence dashboard showcases the value machine learning brings by correlating net automation with some commonly used business KPIs. You can customize the dashboard by changing the time horizon or replacing existing metrics with other business metrics that are more relevant to your organization.

Use this expansive cockpit view to readily demonstrate the aggregate performance of the relevant use cases to your stakeholders as you roll out more use cases.

ROI for a well-implemented use case is self-evident and can pay for itself. The Predictive Intelligence dashboard helps you justify new machine learning initiatives with your sponsors and evangelize its benefit among others within your organization.
