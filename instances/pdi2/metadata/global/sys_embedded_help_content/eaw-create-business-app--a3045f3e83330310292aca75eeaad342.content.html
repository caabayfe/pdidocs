Create a business application

Add a business application that your organization wants to introduce based on its functions and the business process it fulfills.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_apm.apm_analyst

Procedure

  1. Expand Business Applications.
  2. Select All.
  3. Select New.
  4. On the form, fill in the fields.
    For field information, see Business application form.
  5. Select Save.
