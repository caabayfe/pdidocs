Set up field level restrictions for your DLP incidents

Role required:
  • sn_dlir.admin - Create, edit, and delete.
  • sn_dlir.analyst - View (read-only).

Set field level restrictions in DLP incidents to protect sensitive information from being exposed. You can use field level restrictions to control the users or groups who can access specific fields in the DLP incidents.

  1. To create a Field Level Restriction, click New.
  2. On the form, fill in the fields.
  3. In the Applies to tab, define the field level restrictions for specific users or groups.
  4. In the Allow access to following fields only tab, select the required DLP fields and Custom fields that you want provide the users or groups access to.
  5. Click Submit.
