Create, modify, or delete a welcome page section

  • To create or modify a welcome page section:
    1. Enter or modify the Short description.
    2. In the Condition field, enter the following:gs.getUser().getCompanyRecord().name.toString() == "Company"
      Company is the name of your company.
    3. Complete or modify the other fields in the form as needed.
    4. Click Submit or Update.
  • To delete a section, click Delete.
