Overview of Zero Copy Connectors

Unify business and technology data from across your organization to enrich workflows and power AI features in the ServiceNow AI Platform.

Key benefits

  • Access enterprise data from external data warehouses including Snowflake, Google BigQuery, and Amazon Redshift, data lakes such as Databricks, and databases, including Oracle.
  • Retrieve data from external sources in real time without copying any data to your instance using zero copy connections.
  • Fuel AI agents and workflows on the ServiceNow AI Platform with external data using data fabric tables.
  • Provide data to AI-powered features on the ServiceNow AI Platform, expand workflow automation, and enrich metrics and analytics with enterprise data.

Available connectors

View primary and community connectors that support zero copy connections from your instance.

Using zero copy connections, you can connect to external data lakes and databases and access the data directly within the ServiceNow AI Platform, without copying it into your instance. Examples of data sources that you can access include:

  • Databricks
  • Google Big Query
  • Oracle
  • Snowflake
  • Amazon Redshift

The connection admin can create a zero copy connection by selecting Connect.

Established connections

Manage access to your external data, view available schema and tables, and update connection details in your established connections. Plan your integration strategy by viewing external data and determining how to model the data for consumers.

Determine whether established connections are working and available or require updates.

Data stewards, who are responsible for creating data fabric tables, can view the schema and tables from an external source by selecting an established connection.

The connection admin can grant data steward access or update connection details by selecting an established connection.
