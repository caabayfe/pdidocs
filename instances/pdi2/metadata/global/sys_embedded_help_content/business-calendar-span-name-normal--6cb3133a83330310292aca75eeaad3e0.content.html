Create a business calendar entry name

Create a name for a business calendar entry. You create this record so you can easily identify a specific business calendar entry when you use it in a business calendar.

Otherwise, you can create a business calendar entry name at the time you create a business calendar entry.

  1. On the form, fill in the fields.
    Short name
    Abbreviated name for the business calendar entry. For example, Q1.
    Long name
    Longer name for the business calendar entry. For example, Fiscal Quarter 1.
    Label
    Unique label for identification of the business calendar entry when you perform filtering.
    Calendar
    Name of the business calendar that you want the business calendar entry to belong to.
  2. Select Submit.
