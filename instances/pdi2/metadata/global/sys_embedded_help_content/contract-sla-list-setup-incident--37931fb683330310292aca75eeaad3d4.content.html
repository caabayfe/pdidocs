Service level agreements (SLAs)

SLAs help your service desks track and maintain their performance in comparison to previously agreed service level targets. For example, you can use an SLA to ensure that the P1 incidents are resolved within 8 hours.

To create a new SLA definition, click New.

To modify or delete an existing definition, click the definition name in the list.
