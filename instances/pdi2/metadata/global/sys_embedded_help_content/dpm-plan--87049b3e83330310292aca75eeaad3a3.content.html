Plan for your solutions

Plan within the context of your solutions (services or applications).

Plan tab

The plan phase of the plan-build-run life cycle enables you to view and edit plan details within the context of your solutions.
  • Select View roadmap to see the roadmap dependencies in the ServiceNow® Alignment Planner Workspace application.
  • Select View backlog to rearrange and prioritize the backlog, and to move some items into the pipeline.
  • View the summary of what's planned for the solution. Select a card to see a list of the stated records.
  • View a list of demands for the solution. Select a demand record to view its details, tasks, stakeholders, risks, issues, and more.
