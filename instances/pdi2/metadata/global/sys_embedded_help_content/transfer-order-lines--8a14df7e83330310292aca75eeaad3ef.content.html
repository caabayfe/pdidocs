Transfer order lines

Transfer order lines enable the transfer of multiple assets on one transfer order.

Actions such as shipment preparation can take place at the transfer order line level. Pre-allocated assets can be included in a transfer order line, but can only be transferred in their full quantity. You can't transfer the same asset on two different transfer orders at the same time.

Note:
  • You can delete a transfer order only if the transfer order is still in the draft stage.
  • You can cancel a transfer order or a transfer order line while it is in Requested or Shipment Preparation stage.
