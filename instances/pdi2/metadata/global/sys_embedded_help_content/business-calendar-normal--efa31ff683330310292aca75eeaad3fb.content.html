Create a business calendar

Create a definition record for a parent or child calendar so that you can easily identify a specific business calendar and use it elsewhere in the ServiceNow AI Platform.

  1. On the form, fill in the fields.
    Name
    Unique identifier for the business calendar record.
    Label
    Unique singular label for identifying the business calendar when you perform filtering. For example, Workweek.
    Plural label

    Unique plural label for identifying the business calendar. For example, Workweeks.

    Parent
    Parent business calendar to the current business calendar that you are creating.
    Timezone
    Time zone to use for the business calendar when you specify the starting and ending times for the business calendar.
  2. Select Submit.

Create one or more business calendar entries.
