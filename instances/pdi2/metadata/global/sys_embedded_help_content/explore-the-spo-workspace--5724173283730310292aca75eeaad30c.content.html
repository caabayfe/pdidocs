Explore the SPO Workspace

You can use the SPO workspace as a simple automated tool to shop for goods and services that you need at work.

SPO provides you with a seamless procurement experience, which includes:
  • Unified stack: Enables integration with all the digital products that exist in the procurement stack.
  • Consumer-grade experiences: Simplifies procurement-related complexities for people and meet them where they are, with desktop, mobile, portal integration, and more.
  • Intelligent fulfiller experiences: Empowers fulfillers to be strategic by automating their work and guiding them to important information and key decisions.
SPO comprises the following applications:
  • Shopping Hub: Provides a streamlined, e-commerce like experience for your employees to self-service requests, and source or purchase products and services through the procurement organization.
  • Source-to-Pay Common Architecture: Provides an architecture to store purchase orders, requisitions, sourcing requests, and other objects that are commonly used across the source-to-pay business processes.
  • Sourcing and Purchasing Automation: Provides workflows and automation for sourcing requests, negotiations, and purchase requisitions.
  • Procurement Case Management: Enables all your employees to request services from the procurement team, and allow the procurement team to manage those requests.
  • Playbooks for Sourcing and Procurement Operations: Provides a set of pre-built playbooks, workflows, and experiences for your employees, sourcing, and procurement to automate work that is typically managed through emails and spreadsheets.
  • Source-to-Pay Workspace: Provides a single environment for your Procurement Specialists to work on purchase requisitions, sourcing requests, negotiations, procurement requests, and more.
  • Advanced Work Assignment for Source-to-Pay Operations: Provides configurations to support automatic routing, queuing, and assignment of procurement cases, emails, and live agent chat conversations.
  • Performance Analytics for Sourcing and Procurement Operations: Provides a set of pre-configured metrics and dashboards to assess spend, operational efficiency, and team performance across the Sourcing and Procurement Operations product.
  • Shopping Hub Mobile: Enables Shopping Hub and other employee experiences for procurement within the Now Mobile application.
  • Virtual Agent for Sourcing and Procurement Operations: Provides pre-configured topics for sourcing and procurement within a conversational interface, for employees to raise requests, find knowledge, and complete tasks.
  • NLU support for Sourcing and Procurement Operations Virtual Agent: Provides Natural Language Understanding (NLU) models to enhance the virtual agent conversation interface, using natural human utterances to detect the correct conversations intended by employees.
  • Finance Common Architecture: Maintains primary data such as Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) sources, legal entities, accounting periods, and so on.
  • Common Service Delivery: Stores Service Task and Service Request tables, as well as other infrastructure that forms the basis of the Finance and Supply Chain Workflow products.
