Statement item drilldowns

Drill down within a statement item to view the key field that has the data retrieved from the source table. You can also view the mapping to the field that has the source data.

Review the existing list of statement item drilldowns to drill down to the subset of each of their data.

To modify or delete a statement item drilldown, click the record in the Name column.

To create a statement item drilldown, click New.
