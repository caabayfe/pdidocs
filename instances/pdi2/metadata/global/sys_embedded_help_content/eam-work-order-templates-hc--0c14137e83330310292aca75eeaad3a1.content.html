Work order template details

View details for the work order template that automatically populates information, generates appropriate tasks, and creates asset or part requirements for your Enterprise Asset Management work orders.

To modify your work order template, select View Or Edit. When the corresponding Work Order Templates form opens in a new tab, modify any details as needed. For instructions on how to modify your work order template, refer to the Create a template for your Enterprise Asset Management work orders link in the Related information section at the end of this topic.
