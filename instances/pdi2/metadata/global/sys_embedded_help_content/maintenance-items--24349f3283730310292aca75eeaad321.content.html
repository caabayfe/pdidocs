Maintenance items

Maintenance items are filtered based on your selection of a model or location in the Details tab.

If you have selected Location in the Plan type field of the Details tab, the floors, spaces, and areas associated with your selected building are displayed as maintenance items.

If you have selected Model in the Plan type field of the Details tab, models and assets are displayed as maintenance items.

Note: If your selected building or model doesn't show a maintenance item, select Go back to select another building or model.
