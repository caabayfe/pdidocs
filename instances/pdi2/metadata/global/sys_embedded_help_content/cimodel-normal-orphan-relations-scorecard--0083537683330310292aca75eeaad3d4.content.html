Orphan Relations Scorecard

Role required: itil has read access, itil_admin (on top of itil) has full access.
Configure the threshold for best, at risk, and critical state definition for orphan relationships metric.

An orphan relationship is a relationship that is missing either a parent CI, a child CI, or both.

  1. Select configuration item at the top of the Hierarchy list.
  2. If need to, click New to display the scorecard slider.
