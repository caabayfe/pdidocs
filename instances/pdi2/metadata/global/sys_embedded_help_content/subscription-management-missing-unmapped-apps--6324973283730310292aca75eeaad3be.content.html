Missing custom applications

Custom applications that Subscription Management can't recommend product subscriptions for must be mapped through the Custom Application list.

If you don't have an App Engine subscription and criteria for recommending a subscription aren't met, Subscription Management can't recommend subscriptions. Unmapped custom applications without subscription recommendations are counted in the Unmapped Custom Applications report on the Subscription Management Overview, but aren't shown in the Unmapped custom applications tab. Any custom application missing from the Unmapped custom applications tab must be mapped to a product subscription through the Custom Applications list.
