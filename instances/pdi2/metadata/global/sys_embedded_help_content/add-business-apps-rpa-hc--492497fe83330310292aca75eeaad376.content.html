Assign a business application to a bot process

Assign a business application to a bot process for an effective event correlation or to get information about assigned business applications. For example, you might want to see information about the SAP application.

Before you begin

  • Create a bot process. For more information, see Configuring a bot process record in RPA Hub .
  • Verify that the business application exists on your instance. To add a new business application, navigate to All > Self-service > Business Applications.

Role required: sn_rpa_fdn.rpa_release_manager or sn_rpa_fdn.rpa_admin

About this task

You can add any credentials for assigned business applications. If you remove a business application, the associated application credentials are also removed.

You can't remove or edit an associated business application for a published or a retired bot process.

Procedure

  1. On the Business Applications tab, select Add.
  2. On the Available Business Applications tab, select the required business application.
  3. Select Add.
  4. Select Save.
  5. Optional: To remove a business application, select the business application, and select Remove.
