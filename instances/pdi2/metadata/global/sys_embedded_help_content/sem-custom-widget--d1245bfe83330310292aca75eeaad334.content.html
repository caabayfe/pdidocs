Security Exposure Management Custom Widget

Use custom widgets to extend the Findings view with additional data visualizations or tailored summaries that meet your organization’s needs. You can create widgets, configure how they appear, and manage their visibility in the Visualization library.

This feature enables you to:
  • Add custom widgets to the Findings view
  • Configure widget properties and display settings
  • Manage widget visibility in the Visualization library
