Incident close codes

A close code provides the reason for the resolution of an incident. For example, the Not Solved (Too Costly) code indicates that even though an incident is closed, the reported incident is not solved because of budget constraints.

Review the default incident close codes to determine whether they align with your business needs.

To modify or delete a default close code, click the corresponding choice in the list.

To add a new close code, click New .
