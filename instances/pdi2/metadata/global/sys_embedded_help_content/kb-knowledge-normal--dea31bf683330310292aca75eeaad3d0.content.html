Create a knowledge article

Users with at least one role can create and edit knowledge articles as knowledge contributors to share information across the organization.

Depending on the configuration of the knowledge base, the publication process and field options for an article may vary.

  1. On the knowledge article form, fill in the fields.
    Knowledge base
    An article can only be associated with one knowledge base.

    You can change the knowledge base, but only if the selected article template is available in the knowledge base.

    Category
    The category for this article when browsing from the knowledge homepage. The options are determined by the categories defined in the selected knowledge base.
    Short description
    The title of the article. This title appears when browsing and searching for knowledge article, and at the top of the article.
    Article body
    Content for the article. Use the editing functions in the HTML editor to create content.
  2. Submit the article.

    You can provide further details to an article by filling in the additional fields.

    Follow the next steps in the publication workflow defined for the knowledge base.
