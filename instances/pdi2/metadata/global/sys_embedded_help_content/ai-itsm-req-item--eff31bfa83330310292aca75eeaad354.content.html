Requested item forms and lists

A requested item helps fulfillers track and resolve an employee's request.

In the base system, requested item form, list, and related lists are preconfigured.

You can review and update the form layout based on business requirements. You can also use the Implementation Plan Manager Agent that provides conversational & simplified experience for requested item form and list configurations.
