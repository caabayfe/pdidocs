View risks associated with business applications

View risk details of the business application in the context of your solutions. Risk data is pulled in from the ServiceNow® Governance, Risk, and Compliance application.

Risk tab

The risk tab shows you the related risks for the business application.
  • View the overall risk score and select Risk Count to view details of each risk item.
  • View the compliance summary.
    • Select Controls out of compliance to view the associated records.
    • Select Attestations to view the associated records.
  • View the associated engagements.
    • Select Total engagements to view the associated records.
    • Select In progress to view the associated records.
  • View the associated issues and tasks.
    • Select Total issues to view the associated records.
    • Select Risk Response Task to view the associated records.
    • Select Remediation Task to view the associated records.
