Complete and close the Pick Up task for an enterprise asset

Complete and close the Pick Up task for the enterprise asset that is associated with your assigned work order.

Before you begin

Role required: wm_agent

About this task

Before you can begin working on an enterprise asset, it must be picked up from its designated stockroom.

Procedure

  1. On the Details tab of the Pick Up task, modify the fields as needed.
  2. Select Close Task.

Result

The state of the Pick Up task changes from Open to Closed Complete.
