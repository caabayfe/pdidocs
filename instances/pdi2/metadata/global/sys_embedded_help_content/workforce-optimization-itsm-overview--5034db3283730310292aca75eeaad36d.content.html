Getting started with Workforce Optimization for ITSM

Manage and maintain the productivity of your workforce from a single location using Workforce Optimization for ITSM.

This application enables you to do these tasks:
  • Use queues to route the agents based on their assigned groups and skills set.
  • Monitor and measure team performance and provide feedback using assessments.
  • Predict and recommend skills for agents.
  • Create opportunities to coach and train your teams.
