Create schedule in the team calendar

Manage your team's schedule by creating a schedule plan that covers a span of time and includes your team's work shifts. Add agents to the shifts based on their skills and availability.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_shift_planning.admin

About this task

You can view the schedule of all agents in the assignment group that you directly or additionally manage.

Note: Your administrator can set user preferences for the pagination size to view schedule plans.

Procedure

  1. Click the Show Schedules icon () icon.
    Tip:
    • Search schedules: Type keywords in the Schedules search field to access related schedule plans easily.
    • Filter schedules: Select the filter icon () and then select the desired schedule states using the Schedule filter and the desired start and end dates using the Dates filter to narrow down the display of schedules in the team calendar.
  2. Assign schedule plans to your groups.
    In the Assignment groups section, assign the schedule plan to your groups.
    • To assign it to all groups you manage, select Groups I manage.
    • To assign it to specific groups, select Specific groups.
  3. Create a schedule plan.
    1. In the Schedules panel, click the plus () icon.
    2. In the Name field, enter a name for the schedule plan.
    3. In the Start date field, enter the date to start the schedule plan.
    4. In the End date field, enter the date to end the schedule plan.
    5. Click Save.
  4. Add a work shift and agents to the shift in the schedule plan.
    1. Click Add Shift.
    2. In the Shift field, select the shift you want to add to the schedule plan.
    3. In the Agents field, add one or more agents to the shift.
  5. Click Save.
