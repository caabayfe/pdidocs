Problem Management

Problem Management supports the ITIL process to find and fix the root cause of issues that result in incidents. You can record problems, associate incidents, and assign them to appropriate groups. You can create knowledge from problems, request changes, escalate, and manage problems to its resolution and reporting.

Resolving a problem includes the activities required to diagnose the root cause of incidents and to determine the resolution for the problem. Problem resolution and elimination of root cause often calls for applying a change to the configuration item in the existing IT environment.

The Problem form captures information about a problem. This information is used to address the problem and help prevent the recurrence of incidents.

Review the default Problem form to determine whether it aligns with your business needs.

To modify the form layout, click the context menu and select Configure > Form Layout.
