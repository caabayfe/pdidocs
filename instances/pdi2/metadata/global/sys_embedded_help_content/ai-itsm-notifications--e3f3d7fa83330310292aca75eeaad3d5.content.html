Notifications for employees and fulfillers

Notifications keep employees and fulfillers informed at every stage of incident resolution.

In the base system, notifications align with the default Employee Center notification template.

You can configure the notification template. Update notification details such as who receives it, when it is sent, and what (content), and so on. You can also use the Notification Agent that provides conversational & simplified experience for notification configuration.
