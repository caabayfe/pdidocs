Sign up for your preferred shifts

Specify one or more shifts for a schedule plan based on your preference.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_shift_planning.agent

Procedure

  1. Select the Schedule () icon.
  2. Select the Sign up shifts tab.
    Select the Overlapping time-off icon to analyze if the time off you’ve requested or the ones that have been approved overlap with the shifts available to you for sign-up.
  3. In the Add shift field, select the shift for which you prefer to sign up.
    You can only sign up for shifts in the schedule plan that haven’t been published yet.
  4. Select the plus (+) sign and sign up for additional shifts.
  5. Select Submit.
