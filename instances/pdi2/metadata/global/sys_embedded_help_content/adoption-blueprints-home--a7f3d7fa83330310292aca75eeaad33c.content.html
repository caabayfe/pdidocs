Adoption blueprints

Adoption blueprints display the applications and features available to you and the recommended solutions to achieve your organizational needs.

Each of the available blueprint focuses on a strategic goal that might be in-line with your business growth.

Adoption Blueprint enables you to do these tasks:
  • Provides a set of suggested applications and features best suited for the organizational goal
  • Enables discovery of entitled apps based on your subscription
  • Recommends order of implementation
    Note: The recommended applications don't need to be installed at all or in the order listed.
  • Provides application details and options to activate and configure
