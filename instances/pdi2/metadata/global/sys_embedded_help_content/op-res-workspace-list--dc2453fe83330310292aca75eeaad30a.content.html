Managing your Operational Resilience tasks on the List page

Manage your Operational Resilience tasks and monitor the metrics in Operational Resilience Workspace. On the List page, you can configure your operational resilience tasks.

List page in Operational Resilience Workspace

The List page in Operational Resilience Workspace displays the configurable resilience tasks, such as the importance and impact tolerance assessment or a scenario analysis in the list format.

The List page displays the following task records:
  • Business service: Set up your business services in Operational Resilience Workspace and monitor the resilience metrics on the dashboard. The Service form consolidates the attributes, risks, issues, control failures, and other areas of a business service that need attention​.
  • Importance assessment: Analyze the importance rating and impact tolerance duration of a business service by responding to the assessment. Review the rating that is generated based on the questionnaire response. After completion of the analysis, approve the assessment in Operational Resilience Workspace.​
  • Scenario analysis: Analyze the impact of a scenario and an event on a business service and complete the associated response tasks. Determine if any service was breached. After completing the evaluation, approve the scenario analysis in Operational Resilience Workspace.
  • Self-attestations: Verify the resilience status of a business service. Determine if any service was breached. After the verification is complete, certify the status of the business service by signing and generating the self-attestation report. Upload the PDF of the self-attestation report in Operational Resilience Workspace.
