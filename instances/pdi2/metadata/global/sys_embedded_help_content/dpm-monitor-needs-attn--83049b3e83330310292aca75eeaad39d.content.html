Track the status of your solutions

Track the status of service-related items that you own or that may impact solutions that you own. You can monitor changes, incidents, outages, audits, and risks that interest you.

View items that need attention

Your homepage displays a set of default solution cards that you’re associated with based on system-defined fields. Each solution card lists Needs attention attributes, which vary depending on the solution type. At a glance, you can see each card's Needs attention attribute name and the number of times it affects the solution. Also, the color and status icons determine if the Needs attention attribute is severe, moderate, or good.

Note: For the default threshold values for these symbols, see Digital Portfolio Management Workspace homepage.

You're encouraged to personalize your homepage to see different solution cards from the default and to set your own Needs attention attributes. After you personalize, the default set of cards no longer display. Your updates are saved so your view is unique each time you access your homepage. Updates that you make, including Needs attention attribute thresholds, are also unique to your view.

To get the most updated solution data on your homepage, select Refresh ().

If you see an empty state (no solution cards), then personalize your homepage to add items.
