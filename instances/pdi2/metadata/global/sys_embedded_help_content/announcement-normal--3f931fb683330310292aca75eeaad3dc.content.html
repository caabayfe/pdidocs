Create an announcement

Announce important information to your users in a banner or within an announcements widget instance in a portal.

  1. On the form, enter an internal name for the announcement and then fill in the following fields.
    Title
    Title text that appears in the announcement.
    From
    The date and time that the announcement begins to appear.
    To
    The date and time that the announcement ends. If left empty, the announcement appears until it's deactivated.
    Summary
    The body text of the announcement.
    Type

    Where the announcement displays: in an announcement banner or an instance of the announcements widget.

  2. Select Active to activate the announcement or clear the checkbox to hide an announcement.
  3. Select Submit.
