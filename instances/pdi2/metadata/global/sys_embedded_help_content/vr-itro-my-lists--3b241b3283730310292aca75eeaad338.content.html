Use the list view

Use the list view to monitor your remediation progress, view Vulnerability Response records assigned to you and your groups, and see status of your approval requests and exceptions. You can also create your own lists.

Lists

In the left panel, the Lists tab displays default categories of records that are automatically organized by vulnerability type. Click a filtered list, Assigned to me or Assigned to my groups, for a vulnerability category such Remediation tasks or Vulnerable items, for example. You and other remediation owners in your groups can view the records on these lists.

My Lists

Create custom lists that only you can see. To create a custom list:

  1. Select the My Lists tab. In the lower left of this page, click New list.
  2. In the modal, click Start from existing and search for a list that you can use as a template.
  3. Edit the List Name.
  4. Select the columns you want for your list.
  5. Add and save filters to display only the specific data that you want.
  6. Sort results for display.
  7. Click Create.

Alternatively, click Create your own to set your own template. Follow steps 4-7.

Your new list is displayed under My Lists. If you leave the list module, the list you see displayed when you exit is the list you see when you return.

To delete a list, with the list displayed on the My Lists page, in the upper right, click the gear icon.
