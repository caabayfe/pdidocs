Staleness Scorecard

Role required: itil for reading, itil_admin (on top of itil) for full access.
Configure the threshold for best, at risk, and critical state definitions for the stale CIs metric. A CI is stale if it was not updated within the Effective Duration time period that is specified in the staleness rule that applies to the class.
  1. If need to, click New to display the scorecard slider.
