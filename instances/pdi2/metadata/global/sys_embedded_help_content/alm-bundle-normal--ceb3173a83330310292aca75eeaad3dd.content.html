Asset bundles

Use existing consumable and hardware assets from your stockrooms to create asset bundles. Asset bundles are used to track, reserve, or deploy a group of assets as a single entity. Asset bundles are based on bundles models.

Assets that are in the In stock status and available substatus can be added to an asset bundle. An asset bundle has the default status of Build

Asset bundles go through the entire asset life cycle. Assets that are part of asset bundles are excluded from transfer order, sourcing, and disposal order flows. No CIs are associated with an asset bundle.
