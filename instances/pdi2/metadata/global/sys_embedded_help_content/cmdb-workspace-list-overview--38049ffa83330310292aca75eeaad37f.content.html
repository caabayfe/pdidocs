CMDB List

View and evaluate high-level attributes of CIs or export the list into an external file. The CMDB List page enables you to find and review CIs by class.

To change the attributes that appear in the CI list, click List Actions > Edit columns. This enables you to easily find and filter for the CIs you're most interested in, or that need your attention. You can configure the columns of the list view if you want to view attributes unique to a CI class. Configuring the list view with additional attributes is the only way to display unique attributes in the CMDB Workspace.

You can access specific records to review CI details and attributes inherited from the [cmdb_ci] table.

Click New to create a CI record by class.

Export a list to view your CI records in a different file format, or to enable processing of your CIs using an integration. The CMDB Workspace exports your list with the attributes displayed in your list columns.

Customized CI lists are an alternate way for you to organize and find your CI records. Click My Lists > Add new list to create your own CI lists. You can either extend an existing list, including a custom list, or create a new list based off a table in your CMDB.
