Configure the age chart for DLP incidents

Role required:
  • sn_dlir.admin - Create, edit, and delete.
  • sn_dlir.analyst - View (read-only).

Configure the age chart that appears in the Data Loss Prevention (DLP) Ops portal. This chart shows the count of open incidents by the number of days.

  1. To create a DLP Age Chart Configuration, click New.
  2. On the form, fill in the fields.
  3. Click Submit.
