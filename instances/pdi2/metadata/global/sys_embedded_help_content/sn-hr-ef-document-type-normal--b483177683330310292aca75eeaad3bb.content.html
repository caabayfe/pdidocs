Add or modify document types

  • When employee documents are attached to an HR case, you can configure the documents to be moved automatically when the case is closed. Assign a topic detail associated with an HR service that has the Automatically move attachments box checked.
  • Associate a security policy. Security policies determine who can access employee documents and determine purge authorization.
  • Associate a retention policy. Retention policies determine how long to keep a document and who the document is applicable to.
  • Place a legal hold. Legal holds temporarily prevent document purging or changes to the document.
  • Allow access to employees. Employees can view their employee documents through their HR profile or associated HR cases.
