Financial planning

View, plan, re-forecast, and track the financials.

This application enables you to do these tasks:
  • Manage cost plans
  • Manage expense lines
  • Generate labor costs
View the following financials breakdown:
  • Estimate at completion (EAC) – Sum of all actuals for past fiscal periods added to the functional cost for future fiscal periods.
  • Estimate to completion (ETC) – Total of foreseen budget requirement to complete the project for the entire future fiscals.
  • Total actuals to date – Actual costs fiscal period.
