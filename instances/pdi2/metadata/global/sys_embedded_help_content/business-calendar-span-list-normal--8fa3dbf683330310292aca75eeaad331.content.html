Business calendar entries

You can create individual business calendar entries for every business calendar time span. For example, if you are creating a Fiscal Year 2020 business calendar with 13 fiscal periods, you create individual business calendar entries for each fiscal period. For each fiscal period, you specify start and end date times.

Select New to create a new business calendar entry.

Name
Unique reference name for the business calendar entry.
Calendar
The name of the associated calendar.
Start
Start date and time for the business calendar period.
End
End date and time for the business calendar period.
