Daily Schedule job for risk roll-up

This task requires admin role.

Use the OT vulnerability risk roll-up calculator to calculate the risk score of the OT devices at each level of the equipment model. The overall risk score is rolled up to the parent equipment model entity.

Execute the following scheduled jobs to compute the risk associated at the equipment model entity level.

Risk Rollup for VITs by Equipment Model Entity
Calculates risk roll-up for OT Devices assigned to specific sites.
Risk Roll-up for VITs pertaining to OT devices with No sites Assigned
Calculates risk roll-up for OT Devices with No sites Assigned
Note: Scheduled Jobs are automated pieces of work that you can perform at a specific time or on a recurring schedule. Set these scheduled jobs as active in order to execute at a scheduled time. By default, the schedule is set to execute daily.
