Configure findings

Configure how your audit results (findings) for your policies are processed and assigned for remediation.

Before you begin

Role required: SPC Admin Group and SPC Analyst Group

Procedure

  1. Open a policy record.
  2. Select Configure findings.
    1. Select one option.
  3. Select Save configuration.
    If your policy returns matches, they are processed according to your settings and displayed in the workspace under Findings on the List page in the workspace and in the Configuration Compliance application.
