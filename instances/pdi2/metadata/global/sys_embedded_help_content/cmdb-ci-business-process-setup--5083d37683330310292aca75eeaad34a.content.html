Create, modify, or delete a business process

  • To create or modify a business process:
    1. Enter or modify the business process Name.
    2. Enter or modify the Asset tag.
    3. From the Assigned to list, select a user to whom the business process is assigned to.
    4. Enter or modify the Category.
    5. Enter or modify the Fault count.
    6. Select or modify the Installed date and time.
    7. Select or modify the Status from the list.
    8. Click Submit to add the new record or Update to save the modified record.
  • To delete a business process, click Delete.
