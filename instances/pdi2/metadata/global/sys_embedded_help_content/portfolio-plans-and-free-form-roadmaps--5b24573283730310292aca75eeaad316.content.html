Portfolio plans and Free-form roadmaps

Build plans and track the progress of your work.

  • Portfolio plans are personalized portfolios that you create using a lens.

    They help you prioritize, align, roadmap, and track work in the perspective of a department, strategic investments, or others. For a quick overview video, click What are portfolio plans?

  • Free-form roadmaps provide you with the flexibility of planning and tracking work from different units of your company, such as work across multiple portfolios.
