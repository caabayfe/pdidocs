Choices

Choices are the dropdown options available for users on form fields. From the List view, you can update one or more items to change labels, languages, or active states.

Element
Form field where the Choice is used as an option
Hint
Optional. You can provide context to users about what they're choosing.
Sequence
Order Choices are presented to users. If left empty, the list defers to alphabetical ordering.
