Create a deployable

Specify the quantity and environment type of deployable to create and the CMDB services to connect them to.

Before you begin

Role required: admin

Procedure

  1. Fill in the details for the deployable.
  2. Connect each deployable to a service in the CMDB by choosing the service from the list of Available services in the CMDB that the deployable is associated with.
    The deployable holds the config data for its associated service.
    Note: You must connect each deployable to a service.
  3. Select the cmdb_ci to link the deployable to a CI in the CMDB, or create a CI to link to.
