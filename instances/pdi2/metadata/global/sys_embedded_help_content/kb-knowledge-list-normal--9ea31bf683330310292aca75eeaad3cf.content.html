Knowledge articles

The Knowledge list shows the knowledge articles in your knowledge base(s).

Navigate to My Knowledge Articles to see a list of the articles designating you as the Author.
  • Click the column headers to sort, filter, and edit data.
  • Click the article number to view and edit the article details.
  • Select any row(s) and choose an action in the Actions on selected rows drop-down list to update the article(s).
  • Click New to create a new article.

The Knowledge list includes the following significant columns:

Number
Number of the article
Short description
The title of the article. This title appears when browsing and searching for knowledge article, and at the top of the article.
Author
The primary author of the article
Category
The category for this article when browsing from the knowledge homepage
Workflow
The publication state of the article, such as Draft, In Review, or Published
Updated
The date and time the article was last updated
