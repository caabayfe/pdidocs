Create or modify a group

  1. Enter or modify the group information in the form fields.
  2. Click Submit or Update .
  1. In the Group Roles related list, add, modify, or delete the roles assigned to the group.
  2. In the Group Members related list, add, modify, or delete the users associated with the group.
  3. In the Groups related list, add, modify, or delete the children groups of the group.
