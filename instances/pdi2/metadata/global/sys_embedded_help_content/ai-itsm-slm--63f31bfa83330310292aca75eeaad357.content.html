Service Level Management

Service level agreements (SLAs) define the amount of time for a task to reach a certain condition, to verify that incidents are closed or resolved according to the expectations set for customers.

In the base system, SLAs and their associated flows are preconfigured for Incident Management. Response and resolution SLAs are defined for incidents with priority P1 through P4.

You can review and update the definitions, flows, and notifications that are available with Service Level Management according to the incident process. You can also use the SLA Management AI Agent that provides conversational & simplified experience for SLA configuration.
