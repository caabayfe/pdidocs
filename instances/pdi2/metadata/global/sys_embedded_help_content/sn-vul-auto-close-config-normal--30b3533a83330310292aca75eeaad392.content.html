Configure Auto-close for stale detections and retired CIs

Activate this feature to automatically close the VIs associated with a CI if it transitions to Retired. Role required: sn_vul.vulnerability.admin.

As imported data updates configuration items in the CMDB, CIs might be decommissioned or moved to retired, archived, or deleted states as they move through their life cycles. Retired CIs are eventually archived or permanently deleted from the CMDB, but with this feature, you can automatically close the VIs that are associated with a CI when it transitions to Retired in the life cycle.

  1. Review the following key terms and field descriptions and fill in the fields.
    Detections last found
    This search option uses a date and time provided by the third-party scanner. This term refers to the most current, or latest date and time that detections were found again by the scanner.
    Assets last scanned
    This search option uses a date and time provided by the third-party scanner. This term refers the most current date and time an asset was last scanned by a third-party scanner.
  2. Choose one with the Stale Detections tab selected: Automatically close stale detections based on when detections were last found, or when assets were last scanned.
  3. Enter the age (number of days) of the stale detections or retired CIs that you want closed.
  4. Select the Active check box to activate the scheduled job.
  5. To automatically close the vulnerable items associated with CIs, with the Configuration Item Lifecycle tab selected, select Auto-close VIs linked to retired CIs check box.
  6. Click Update.

If a CI is updated to Retired, its associated VIs are closed with the substate, CI decommissioned. If the state of a discovered item is updated to CI decommissioned, the associated VI is closed, and the Notes section is automatically updated. For stale detections and retired CIs, new and existing VIs and detections reported for the same CI are automatically created or updated to the Closed state. If a CI moves back to its original state from the Retired state, the Discovered Items entry is updated as well. VIs are automatically opened if related data is ingested again from the scanner.
