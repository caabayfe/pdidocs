Incident states

An incident is created with the New state. It follows a process that finishes with the Closed or Canceled state. A series of rules ensure that the progression is controlled and standardized.

Review the labels of the default incident states to determine whether they align with your business needs.

To modify or delete a default state label, click the corresponding choice in the list.

To add a new label, click New .

Note:Do not modify the Value field of a state, because it may have an unintended effect.
