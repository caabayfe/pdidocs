My Work

Use the My Work view to manage your tasks. These tasks typically relate to data compliance tasks for the CMDB, such as attestation and other life-cycle tasks.

Attestation tasks confirm the existence and state of your IT infrastructure and software applications. Depending on whether a CI exists, you can attest or reject a CI in the task. Cancel the task if it was incorrectly created.

Note: Your assigned role determines the actions available to you.

My work

This card shows you attestation tasks that are currently assigned to you or your assignment group. If you're a CMDB Admin, this card also shows unassigned attestation tasks.
Open tasks
These are tasks that are currently assigned to you or to an assignment group you belong to.
Overdue tasks
These are currently overdue tasks that might require your immediate attention. Due dates for tasks are determined by the associated data policy.

My groups

This card shows the task assignment groups you are a member of, as well as the associated user group manager. The data management policy of the task assignment group determines how the task is processed.
