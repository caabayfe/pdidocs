NLQ Synonyms

Enrich the system's NLQ capabilities by adding your own synonyms that match well to your business or industry-specific queries. Synonyms provide the system with alternative words and phrases that NLQ uses when searching your data. Some predefined synonyms are provided out of the box, and you can add your own by selecting the New button.
