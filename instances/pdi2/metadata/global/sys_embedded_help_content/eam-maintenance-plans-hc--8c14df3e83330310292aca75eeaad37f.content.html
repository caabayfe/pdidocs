Maintenance plan details

View details for the maintenance plan that you are using to manage and schedule routine maintenance for your enterprise assets.

Use the following tabs to view and manage your maintenance plan:
  • Details: View details for your maintenance plan, including the enterprise assets that the maintenance plan is applied to.
  • Maintenance Schedules: View and create schedules for your maintenance plan.
  • Maintenance Plan Records: View and create associations between the available maintenance schedules and the enterprise assets that the maintenance plan is applied to.
