Missing custom tables

Custom tables that Subscription Management can't recommend product subscriptions for must be mapped through the Custom Inventory Table list.

If you don't have an App Engine subscription and criteria for recommending a subscription aren't met, Subscription Management can't recommend subscriptions. Unmapped custom tables without subscription recommendations are counted in the Unmapped Global Custom Tables report on the Subscription Management Overview, but aren't shown in the Unmapped global custom tables tab. Any custom tables missing from the Unmapped global custom tables tab must be mapped to a product subscription through the Custom Inventory Table list.
