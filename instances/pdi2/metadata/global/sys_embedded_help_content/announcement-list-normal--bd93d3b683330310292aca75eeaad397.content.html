Announcements

View the announcements configured for portals on the instance. See whether they are active, any dates they will display on a portal, and the type. You can access all announcement records from this list by selecting the announcement name.

From
Date and time that the announcement begins to appear.
To
Date and time that the announcement ends. If left empty, the announcement appears until it's deactivated.
Type
Where an announcement displays: in an announcement banner or an instance of the announcements widget.
