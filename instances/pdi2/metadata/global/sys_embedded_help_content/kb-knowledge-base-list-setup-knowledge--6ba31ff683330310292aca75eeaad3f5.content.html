Knowledge bases

Use a knowledge base to store, analyze, and share the collective ideas, experiences, and information that your users possess. The benefits of a knowledge base include the following:
  • Users have one source of information that is easy to search.
  • Information in the knowledge base is up-to-date, as knowledge articles have a defined life cycle: create, review and update, publish, and retire.
  • Access to the knowledge base content can be controlled based on user profiles.
  • When a user starts entering a short description to report an incident or a problem, a contextual search can display a list of the relevant knowledge base articles. If any of the displayed articles addresses the issue, the user does not need to report the incident or the problem.

Review the default knowledge bases in the list and customize them to align with your business needs.

To modify or delete a knowledge base, click the title of the knowledge base in the list.

To add a new knowledge base, click New.
