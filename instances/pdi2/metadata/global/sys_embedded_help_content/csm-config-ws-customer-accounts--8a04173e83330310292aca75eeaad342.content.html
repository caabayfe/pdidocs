Customer accounts

Accounts represent supported external customers.

There are two types of accounts: customer accounts and partner accounts.
  • Customer account: represents an individual customer.
  • Partner account: represents a customer that sells to and supports other customers.
    • A partner can report and manage cases on behalf of customers.
    • A partner can also be a customer.

An account can be a customer account, a partner account, or both. The Customer and Partner fields on the Details tab of the Account form denote the account type.

Each account has a primary contact. This person is an employee of the account and is typically responsible for communication about the account. Accounts can also have additional contacts.
