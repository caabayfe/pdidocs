Keeping track of overdue training, assessments, and approvals

Keep track of training that has not been completed per schedule, the status of coaching assessments, and items pending approval.

Monitor the number of training items that your team has not completed on time and the coaching assessments that have not been closed.

The Teams Overview section displays the number of items for the following categories:
  • Overdue Learning Tasks: the number of training items that your teams needs to complete. You can also view how many of them are due on the current day, and how many were due two weeks or a month ago from the current day.
  • Open Assessments: the number of assessments that have not been closed. You can also view how many assessments are underway and the ones that have been closed in the last two weeks.
  • Pending Approval: the total number of requests that need your approval. You can also view the number of new approval requests that came in on the current day and the ones that you need to approve before the end of the current day.
