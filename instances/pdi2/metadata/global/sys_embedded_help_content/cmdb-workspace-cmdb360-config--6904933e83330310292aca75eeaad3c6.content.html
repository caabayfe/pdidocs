Configure the CMDB 360 dashboard

Configure settings in the CMDB 360 dashboard to determine how your CMDB 360 data is analyzed and aggregated. The settings affect the data that appears on the cards and the records shown when you drill into the cards in your CMDB 360 dashboard.

Before you begin

Role required: cmdb_ms_admin and sn_cmdb_user.

Procedure

  1. Configure Global settings.
    The Maximum number of records to process setting determines the maximum number of records that can appear when you drill into cards in the CMDB 360 dashboard. If the total number of returned records is greater than the specified value, the dashboard trims the list view and card displays accordingly.
    You can use the setting (default value 100,000) to limit the number of records that CMDB 360 must process. The setting applies to these cards:
    • CIs not reported by discovery sources
    • Data mismatch
    • CIs with a single discovery source
    • CIs by number of discovery sources
  2. Configure Potential issues settings.
    The settings affect the calculations for cards on the Potential issues tile and the list of CIs that appear when you drill into those cards.
    1. Configure CIs not reported by discovery sources.
      The Number of days since CIs were last discovered by a discovery source setting determines the number of days used in the calculation of the CIs not reported by discovery sources card. The card shows CIs that are discoverable by multiple sources, but at least one discovery source hasn't reported on that CI in the specified number of days.
    2. Configure Data mismatch.
      The settings affect the calculation of the Data mismatch card and the list of CIs that appear when you drill into the card. The settings determine which classes to use for the card and the relative weight of each of the classes.
  3. Configure Coverage settings.
    The settings affect the calculations for cards on the Coverage tile and the list of CIs that appear when you drill into the cards. These settings determine which classes to use for the cards and the relative weight of each of those classes.
