Create a report

Reports enable you to visualize data and share your visualizations with other users.

  1. On the Data tab or the Data choice list, choose the data to visualize.
    OptionDefinition
    Data sourceData sources configured to be a single source of truth for your business. Use data sources when you create reports to ensure data consistency across reports.
    TableReport directly on table data. Click the blue filter to add data filters.
    External importChoose an existing imported report source, or click the Upload icon to import a new file.
    • Choose how you want to visualize the data on the Type tab/choice list.
    • On the Configure tab or on the choice lists that display, further configure how the data is visualized.
    • Select the Style tab to customize the look and feel of your visualization.
    • Click Save.
    • Click the share icon to share the report with others, add the report to a dashboard, and export or schedule the report.
