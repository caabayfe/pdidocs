SGC Central

Service Graph Connector Central also called as SGC Central enables you to discover, install, and monitor Service Graph Connectors.

This application enables you to do these tasks:
  • Integrated dashboard to gain instant insights into the connection status of your connectors and performance, keeping your connections running smoothly. In addition, quickly debug for seamless integration.
  • Explore and install connectors based on the connection type requirements.
  • Create or modify connections to your needs.
