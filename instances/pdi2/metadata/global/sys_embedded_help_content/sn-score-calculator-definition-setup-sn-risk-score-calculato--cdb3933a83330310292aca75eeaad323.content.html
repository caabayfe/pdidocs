Risk Factors for Operational Technology Obsolescence Management

Roles required: sn_risk_score_calc.admin role or sn_risk_score_calc.user

There is already a default Risk factor, if you want to modify the existing Risk factors or add new factors, follow the steps below:

  1. To modify the Risk Factor of an existing Risk Definition, configure the Risk Factor form fields of the Score Calculation factor related list according to your requirement.
  2. To create new Risk Factor, Navigate to All > Risk score calculator > Risk calculator definitionOn the form, fill in the fields.
  3. Open the Risk calculator definition where the Risk Factors need to be added.
  4. Select the Calculation factor related list.
  5. Select New.
  6. This opens an empty form to provide the details of Risk Factor.
  7. Provide the following details for Risk factor: Name, weightage, source table column, and transform score.
  8. Select Update.
