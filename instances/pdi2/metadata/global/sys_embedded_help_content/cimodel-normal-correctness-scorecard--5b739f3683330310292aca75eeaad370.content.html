Correctness Scorecard

Role required: itil has read access, itil_admin (on top of itil) has full access.
Configure the threshold for best, at risk, and critical state definitions for the correctness KPI.

The correctness KPI is an aggregation of the orphan, stale, and duplicate metrics, according to the correctness scorecard weight settings.

  1. Select configuration item at the top of the Hierarchy list.
  2. If need to, click New to display the scorecard slider.
