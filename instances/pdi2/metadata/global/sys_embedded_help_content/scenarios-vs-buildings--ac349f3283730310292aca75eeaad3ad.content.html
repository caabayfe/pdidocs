View workplace scenarios and buildings

Use the All scenarios section to track the number of scenarios in your workplace regardless of their current state. Use the All buildings section to view available buildings whose scenarios in All scenarios are implemented.

A scenario enables you to visualize spaces and make space allocations on a stack plan based on their assignments. The All scenarios section lists of all scenarios, which you can sort based on the state, building, and so on.

The All buildings section lists all buildings in your workplace in which you can implement space allocations. You can open a building record and view it on a stack plan. The stack plan displays the current scenario that is deployed on the building.
