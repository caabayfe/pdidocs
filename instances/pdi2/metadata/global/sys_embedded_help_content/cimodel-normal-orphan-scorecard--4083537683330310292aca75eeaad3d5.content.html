Orphan Scorecard

Role required: itil for reading, itil_admin (on top of itil) for full access.
Configure the threshold for best, at risk, and critical state definition for the orphan CIs metric.

A CI is determined to be orphan if the CI satisfies the criteria in an orphan rule, or if data is missing for the CI in its respective table or in one of its parents' table.

  1. If need to, click New to display the scorecard slider.
