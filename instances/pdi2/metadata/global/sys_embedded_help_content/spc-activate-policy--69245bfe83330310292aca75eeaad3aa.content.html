Activate a policy

You must activate policies to audit your imported asset data. You must also activate policies so that you can view current data about your assets on the data visualizations displayed on the Home and Custom insights modules in the workspace.

Before you begin

Role required: SPC Admin Group and SPC Analyst Group

Procedure

To activate a policy, select Activate policy.
