Knowledge articles

Knowledge articles enable you to create, maintain, and share information such as self-help, troubleshooting, and task resolution.

Using the Knowledge lists, you can view your knowledge articles (both published and unpublished), all published knowledge articles, and knowledge tasks.

Published and unpublished articles

Unpublished articles include the articles that you have created and that are waiting to be published. A knowledge article is published depending on the workflow setting of its knowledge base. An article can be published immediately, on a scheduled date, or upon approval.

Published articles include the articles that you have created and that have been published. When published, your knowledge articles appear in the My Articles - Published and All Articles lists.

All articles

The All Articles list includes published and retired articles for all users and knowledge bases. Sort by Author, Category, or Knowledge base to narrow this list.

Knowledge tasks

Internal and external users can provide feedback on knowledge articles, which creates knowledge tasks that can be assigned to agents.
  • My Tasks - Feedback: Use these tasks to address articles that were marked as not helpful or that received low ratings from users.
  • My Tasks - Flagged: Use these tasks to address articles that contain missing our outdated information or to create articles for reported knowledge gaps.
