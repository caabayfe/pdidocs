Attest CIs

Complete attestation tasks from the Governance view of CMDB Workspace by reviewing all CIs that need to be attested. Review the CIs and then confirm or reject the existence of each CI. Auto-attest CIs detected by Smart detection as candidates for auto-attestation.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_cmdb_admin (CMDB Admin), sn_cmdb_editor (CMDB Editor), or sn_cmdb_user (CMDB User)

About this task

Attest the CIs of your IT infrastructure and applications to help ensure that your CMDB is free of duplicate or outdated records. After you complete your attestation tasks, you can use de-duplication tasks and CMDB Data Manager to improve the integrity of your CMDB.

Procedure

  1. Optional: Auto-attest CIs using Smart detection.
    You can click Smart detection status in the right hand bar to review and attest CIs in bulk. Smart detection lists CIs found by Discovery or Service Graph connectors and enables you to attest those CIs in bulk.
  2. Review the CIs listed in the Not Yet Reviewed list.
    You can access a CI to view additional CI details. The details shown can vary, depending on your settings or whether features are enabled.
  3. Select CIs.
  4. Click the action to perform for all selected CIs.

    If you need to undo an attest or reject action, select the CIs from the respective list and click Unattest.

  5. After reviewing all CIs in the attestation task, click Submit.
  6. In the Submit attestation dialog box, click Submit again to close the task.
