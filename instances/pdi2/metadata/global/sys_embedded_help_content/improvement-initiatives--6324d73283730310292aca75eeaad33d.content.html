Vendor improvement initiatives

Monitor improvement initiatives to manage tasks for improving a service, process, or tasks related to vendors.

Tracking improvement initiatives

Keep track of the initiatives using the Improvement initiatives list:
  • Number: The unique identification number associated with the improvement initiative.
  • Short description: A brief summary of the main purpose of the contract.
  • State:: The contract status such as whether it is in draft or active state.
  • Priority: The impact or urgency in which the initiative needs to be worked on.
  • CIM Coordinator: User primarily responsible for ensuring the completion of the improvement initiative.
  • Effort estimate: Measure of effort required to complete the task.
  • Benefits: Expected level of benefit achieved by completing the improvement initiative.
