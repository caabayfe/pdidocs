Contract management view

View and manage details of contracts such as Contract number, Contract start and end dates, terms and conditions statements.

The Contract Management view includes important actions for managing your contract, contract expenditure by type and vendor, and the list of expiring contracts.

Select any widget or chart to view in detail and take the required actions. For details on the widgets, scroll down to the Contract management overview table.

You can also use Domain, Type, and Vendor category lists to narrow down your results.

Note: The Domain filter is available only when you’ve enabled the Domain Extensions Installer (com.glide.domain.msp_extensions.installer) and Domain Separation (plugin com.snc.pa.domain_support) plugins.
