View unified map for a business application

View a unified map for your business applications in the Enterprise Architecture workspace to better understand the current architecture.

Before you begin

Ensure the CMDB Workspace (com.snc.cmdb.workspace) plugin (version 4.0.1) is installed.

Role required: sn_apm.apm_user and sn_cmdb_user

Procedure

  1. Select a business application to open it.
  2. Select Open Map.
    The unified map for the selected business application is opened in a new tab within the EA Workspace.
