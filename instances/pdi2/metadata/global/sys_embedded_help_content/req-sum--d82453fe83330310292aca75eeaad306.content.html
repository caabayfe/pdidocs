Summarize requests

Generate unified summaries of Request Management records to get a consolidated real-time status.

Before you begin

Role required: itil

Procedure

  1. From the Details tab, select Summarize.
    The summary is generated in the Details tab.
  2. Optional: When you're finished summarizing a record, you can add the summary to your work notes, copy it, or provide feedback.
