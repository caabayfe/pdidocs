CMDB 360 view

Review aggregated CMDB 360 data for CIs from reporting discovery sources. Use the CMDB 360 view in Configuration Management Database (CMDB) Workspace to track activities and identify potential issues of discovery sources. You can also create custom queries and associated schedules and reports to explore CMDB 360 data.

Access

Additional requirement: Enable CMDB 360.

  • For information about all CMDB 360 dashboard settings, see Configure the CMDB 360 dashboard.
