Agentic workflows

An agentic workflow is a coordinated, multi‑step process where AI agents plan, act, and collaborate to achieve a complex business goal.

In the base system, prebuilt AI agents for requesters such as for incident creation, status checks, and approvals are enabled. Additionally, the following prebuilt AI agents are enabled to improve fulfiller productivity:
  • AI-Assisted Incident Triage
  • Resolution and AI Recommendations

You can remove an agent that is part of the agentic workflow or manage (edit, deactivate, activate, or create) the triggers associated with the agentic workflows.
