Removal candidate form view

View specific details of a removal candidate in the removal candidate form view.

You can also perform the following actions on the removal candidate.
  • Close Complete: Reclaim rights and closes the removal candidate.
  • Close Skipped: Close the removal candidate without reclaiming rights.
  • Reclaim: Reclaim rights for the removal candidate. The removal candidate needs to be in a ready state to be reclaimed.
  • Show Workflow: Graphically displays the entire reclamation workflow of the removal candidate.
  • Delete: Deletes a removal candidate. Select the ellipsis icon to display the Delete action.
Note: The action buttons are dependent on the removal candidate's justification and state.
