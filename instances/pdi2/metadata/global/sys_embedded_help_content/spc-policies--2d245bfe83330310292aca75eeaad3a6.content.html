Policies overview

The policies listed on this page support various use cases that help you identify specific types of tool coverage gaps in your assets. Asset data is imported by service graph connectors and audited by activated polices. Before you activate the policies that are included with the application, review the following information.

Policies, entities, and their relationships

Policies are built on an entity-relationships-properties data model and criteria is created in the condition builder. You can create your own policies to look for an entity or an asset match based on specific criteria. To view how the condition builder of a policy works, select a record from this list to open it.

A policy is activated if true is displayed in the Active column. Open a record and select Activate policy to activate it. The policy starts monitoring assets for matches to its conditions.

More about policies

  • Before you can import data about your assets, you must activate service graph connectors. You can choose the supported connectors that you want to use, download the supported applications from the ServiceNow Store, and activate them.
  • Before you can use policies to audit or monitor your imported asset data, you must get initial insights from your activated service graph connectors.
  • Matching data returned from the policy audits on your assets is used to create returned results (findings). You can view breakdowns and categories of findings in remediation task records (CTR#) on the Findings list on this page and in the Configuration Compliance application.
  • You cannot delete any policies.
  • You can only deactivate policies that have generated findings.
  • A base policy refers to a policy that you use as an extension for a clone or a new policy. The conditions of the base policy are inherited in the new policy so you don't have to enter them again.
  • You can use multiple policies for your use cases. All the conditions in the base policy are also inherited in the child policies.
  • After you activate a policy, you cannot edit it. However, you can clone any policy and create your own custom policies.
  • You might clone or create child policies from any policy record to help you further refine your search criteria and return more refined matches.
