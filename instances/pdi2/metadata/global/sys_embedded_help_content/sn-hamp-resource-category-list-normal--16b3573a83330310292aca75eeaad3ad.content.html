HAM licensing

The HAM Resource Categories catalog item lets you opt in a particular resource category of HAM license that you want to use. If you don't use a particular resource category, you can opt out of it and you don't have to pay for it.

Note: If you haven't opted in a particular resource category of HAM license, the normalization functionalities for the associated models and asset actions for the associated assets don't work.
