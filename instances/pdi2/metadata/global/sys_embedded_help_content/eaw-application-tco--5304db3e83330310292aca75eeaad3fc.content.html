Application Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

The Application Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) feature helps Enterprise Architects to evaluate the cost of business applications and leverage the application costs to prioritize the application portfolio and align with the organization's business strategy.

TCO dashboards

The Portfolio TCO tab in the Dashboards page displays the following dashboards for Application TCO:
  • Business Application TCO for current quarter and previous quarter
  • Business Application TCO trend for year
  • Business Application TCO by application category for current quarter and previous quarter.
  • Business Application TCO by application planned disposition for current quarter
For more details, see Explore the Enterprise Architecture Workspace dashboard.
