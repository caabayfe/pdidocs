Monitoring Now Assist creators

View an account-level summary of your Creator Plus entitlements and track allocations for distinct users across all of your instances.

Total purchased

View the total number of Creator Plus entitlements purchased across all your instances.

Distinct allocated users

Monitor the number of Creator Plus entitlements that are allocated to unique users across all your instances.

Note: Users allocated to a Creator Plus subscription on multiple instances are automatically de-duplicated. When you allocated a user to a Creator Plus subscription on more than one instance, that user counts toward the allocation total only once.

Availability

View the remaining number of available Creator Plus creator entitlements that you can allocate across all your instances. If your Creator Plus subscriptions are over-allocated, consider reducing the number of allocated subscriptions or purchasing more subscriptions.

Instances

View Creator Plus creator entitlements by instance in the Allocated users column.

  • Only instances on Washington DC Patch 2 and higher are listed in the Instances list.
  • If data sharing is restricted on an instance, usage data for that instance is hidden. You can disable the restriction by updating the trust configuration on that instance.

Subscriptions

View Creator Plus product subscription details by selecting a product in the list. On the details page, allocate available Creator Plus subscriptions by adding one or more groups with the Creator role.
