Surveys in Employee Center

Surveys help you create, send, and collect responses from your users to improve their experience.

In the base system, preconfigured employee satisfaction surveys are available to gather feedback post incident resolution and request fulfillment.

You can create or edit an existing survey using Survey Designer. You can also use the Implementation Plan Manager Agent that provides conversational & simplified experience for survey configuration.
