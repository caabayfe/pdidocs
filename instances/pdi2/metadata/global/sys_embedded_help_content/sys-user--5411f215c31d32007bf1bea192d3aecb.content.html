Create or modify a user

  1. Enter or modify the user information in the form fields.
  2. Click Submit or Update .
  1. In the User Roles related list, add, modify, or delete the roles of the user.
  2. In the Group Members related list, add, modify, or delete the group memberships of the user.
  3. In the Delegates related list, add, modify, or delete the delegates of the user.
