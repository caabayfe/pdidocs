Create and manage an email template for your DLP incidents

Role required:
  • sn_dlir.admin - Create, edit, and delete.
  • sn_dlir.analyst - View (read-only).

Create and manage the preconfigured email templates for sending notifications to your end users, user groups, or managers. With these templates, you can coach and communicate with your end users about the Data Loss Prevention (DLP) incidents.

  1. To create a DLP Email Template, click New.
  2. On the form, fill in the fields.
  3. In the Target Users tab, select the recipients or target users for this email template.
  4. In the Email Template tab, define the variables to reuse existing content to add to the email notification.
    Here is an example for Default template for Escalation - Digest: 
    Hi ${target_user.name},The following Incidents have been escalated:${overdue_table}
  5. Click Submit.
