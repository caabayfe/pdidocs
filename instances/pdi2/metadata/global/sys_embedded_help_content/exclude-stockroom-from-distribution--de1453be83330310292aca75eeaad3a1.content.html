Exclude a stockroom from distribution channel

Exclude a stockroom from distribution channels so that the stockroom isn't displayed in the Stockrooms missing distribution channels important actions card in the Inventory view.

Before you begin

Role required: inventory_admin

About this task

Note: You can't exclude a stockroom that is already linked into any distribution channel.

Procedure

  1. From the list of available stockrooms, select the stockroom that you want to exclude from distribution channels.
  2. On the Details tab, select the Exclude from distribution channels check box.
  3. Select Save.

Result

  • The stockroom isn't listed under the Stockrooms missing distribution channels important actions card.
  • In the Distribution channel tab, the New option isn't displayed.
