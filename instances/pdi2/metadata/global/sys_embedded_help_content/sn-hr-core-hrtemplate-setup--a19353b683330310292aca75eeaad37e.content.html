Add or modify an HR template

  1. Complete the form.
    FieldDescription
    Owning GroupThe group responsible for the HR template. Examples: HR, IT, Facilities. Owning groups are defined in the Manage Owner Groups in the Lifecycle Events module.
    PriorityDefault priority for the HR case that determines the impact and urgency for HR case completion.
    Assignment typeDetermines the HR agent or group the HR case or task is assigned to.
    • Named user: Assign case/task to a specific employee.
    • Skills/Assignment group: Select skills and group assigned to the case/task.
    • Click Submit.
