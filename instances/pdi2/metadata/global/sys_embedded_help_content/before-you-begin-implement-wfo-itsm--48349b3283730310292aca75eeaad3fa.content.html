Plan your Workforce Optimization for ITSM implementation

After you set up your teams as groups, you can route work, set up schedules, train your teams, and analyze all aspects of your workforce from one location.

Before you start implementing Workforce Optimization for ITSM, make sure you do the following:
  1. Identify your teams

    Define the different teams within your organizations that you want to manage. Each of these teams will be configured as a group in the User Groups [sys_user_group] table.

  2. Assign a primary assignment group for each member of your team

    A team member can belong to multiple groups but you must identify one group identified as the primary group for each team member and assign it to them.

    The image below shows a representation of assigning a primary group to a team member who belongs to more than one group.
  3. Add a manager to each group

    Each team must have a manager assigned. This manager is someone who would directly oversee the team.

  4. Add additional managers to each group

    For each group that you create, you can also add managers, known as additional managers, who don’t directly manage the group but need visibility into those groups.
