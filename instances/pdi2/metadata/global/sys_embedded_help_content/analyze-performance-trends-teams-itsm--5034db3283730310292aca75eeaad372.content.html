Drill down into your teams' KPI trends to analyze performance

Analyze trends to monitor your team's performance within a KPI group.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_team_perf.team_performance_admin

Procedure

  1. Select the date range for which you want to view the performance.
  2. Drill down into the data for the team.
    You can also review cases/incidents, details of assignment groups, additional managers, and information that is relevant to the team.
