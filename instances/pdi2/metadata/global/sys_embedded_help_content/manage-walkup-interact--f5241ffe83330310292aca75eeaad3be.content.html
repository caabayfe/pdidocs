Before you begin

Role required: sn_walkup.walkup_technician

About this task

Based on the state and assigned status, walk-up interactions are grouped under the following list:

  • My Assigned Walk-ups: Walk-up interactions you’re assigned to resolve or fulfill requests. These interactions are in a Work in Progress state.
  • Open – Unassigned: List of all open and unassigned walk-up interactions for your locations. These interactions are in New, Queued, or Work in Progress state.
  • Closed Walk-ups: All closed walk-up interactions. Closed interactions have either Closed Complete or Closed Abandoned state.
