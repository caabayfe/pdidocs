Leased and lease-end asset details

View details for the leased or lease-end asset that is covered by a lease contract. Leased assets are the enterprise assets that are covered by your non-expiring lease contracts. Lease-end assets are the enterprise assets that are covered by your expiring lease contracts.

Use the following tabs to view and manage your leased or lease-end asset:
  • Details: View details for your leased or lease-end asset.
  • Contract Asset Tasks: View and complete all lease-end tasks that are required for your lease-end asset.
    Note: This tab is available only for lease-end assets.
