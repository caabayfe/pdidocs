Group roles

Create a group role to control access to features and capabilities in applications for all members in a group.

Group roles have the following characteristics.
  • Roles: roles assigned to the group. All members of the group have privileges of these roles.
  • Group Members: lists all users that belong to this group.
  • Groups: groups can belong to other groups. For example, all HR groups roll up under the group HR . All members of the HR group have the privileges of the roles for each group.

Review the list of groups to verify that the intended roles are present in the database.
