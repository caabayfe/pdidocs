Create a labor rate card

Create a labor rate card to record the time worked on a task and associate a rate to the task.

Before you begin

Role required: asset_admin

Activate the ServiceNow® Cost Management (com.snc.cost_management) plugin. For more information, see Activate Cost Management.

Procedure

  1. On the form, fill in the fields.
  2. Select Save.

Result

The labor rate card information is included in the task rate card by default.
