Now Assist skills

Now Assist skills are prebuilt, LLM‑powered capabilities that surface in the right UI touchpoints and can be activated or configured by admins across workflow. For example, summarization, KB generation, and email drafting.

In the base system, Now Assist skills such as KB generation, Incident summarization, Chat summarization, Resolution notes generation, and Email generation are enabled.

You can activate or deactivate these skills based on business requirements.
