Generate email reply recommendations using Now Assist for IT Service Management (ITSM)

Generate recommendations for email replies based on the incident context.

Before you begin

Role required: itil

Procedure

  1. In the Email tab in the Compose section, start composing an email.
  2. Using the Now Assist icon, do any of the following:
    • Use recommendations from email templates to compose the email.
    • Elaborate or shorten the response.
  3. Select Send Email.
