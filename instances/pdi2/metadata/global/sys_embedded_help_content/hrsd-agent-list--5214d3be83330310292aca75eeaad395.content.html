List view for HR Service Delivery Agent Workspace

HR agents view and navigate through lists of different types of HR cases, tasks, and requests.

Types of lists

All HR cases
A list HR cases in various states.
Lifecycle events
A lifecycle event case is an HR case that is fulfilled by a lifecycle event.
Other
Create HR cases for employees or other individuals that require an HR service. Employees can also use the HR Service Portal to create HR cases for themselves.
Interactions
An interaction is a request for assistance made through chat, phone, or in person. You can route interactions by skill, group, or directly to an HR agent.
HR tasks
You can add, view, or work on HR tasks assigned to HR cases.
HR profile
HR Profiles contain confidential employee data, such as name, personal contact information, email addresses, employment history, and contacts.
Employee documents
Employee Document Management provides storage space, a filing system, easily retrieve documents, defining who can view sensitive documents, and when to purge documents.
Knowledge
Create a knowledge article in Agent Workspace to share information across your organization.
Reminders
Create reminders tied to HR cases or tasks to provide timely communication and actions are taken. Use reminders to make certain you follow up by a certain date.
Note:

HR services are categorized under one of the HR Centers of Excellence (COEs). COEs are part of a data model that organizes HR data, services, and processes by functional areas in HR. Administrators enable or disable HR COEs.
