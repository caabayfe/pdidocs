Create and resolve a safety observation

Create safety observations that are reported in your organization.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_ohs_im.operations_manager, sn_ohs_im.manager, or sn_ohs_im.agent

Procedure

  1. On the form, fill in the fields and select Save.
    Note: The Visitors involved field appears only when the Workplace Visitor Management (com.sn_wsd_visitor) application is installed on your instance.
  2. In the Assets tab, manage the assets involved in this observation.
  3. In the People involved tab, manage the people involved in the observation.
  4. In the Root cause analysis tab, perform the root cause analysis for the observation.
  5. In the Actions tab, add actions based on your root cause analysis.
  6. Record your findings that you gathered about the observation.
    • Use the Attachments section to attach findings in the form of image, video, and documentation such as a signed witness statement.
    • Use the Activity section to add findings in the form of free text.
  7. To close the observation, set the state to Closed Complete and select Save.
