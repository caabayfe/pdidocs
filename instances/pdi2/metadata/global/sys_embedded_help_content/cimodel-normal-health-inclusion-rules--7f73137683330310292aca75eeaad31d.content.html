Health Inclusion Rules

Role required: itil for reading, itil_admin (on top of itil) for full access.

Narrow the scope of CIs that are included in health calculations by defining health inclusion rules. Evaluation for the required, orphan, recommended, and staleness health metrics, will apply only to CIs that satisfy health inclusion rules.

  1. Select an existing rule to edit or select New and then fill out the Health Inclusion Rules form.
    FieldDescription
    Applies toClass this rules applies to.
    Active record conditionCriteria that CIs must meet to be included in the evaluation for the specified health metrics.
    Applies to metricMetrics that the rule applies to.
  2. Select Save or Update.
