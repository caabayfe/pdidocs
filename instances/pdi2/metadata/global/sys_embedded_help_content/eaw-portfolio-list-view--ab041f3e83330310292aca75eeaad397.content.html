Portfolio list view

View the details of your portfolio.

In the Lists view, you can see the high-level information for the following records:
  • Business capabilities
    • All
    • Owned by me
  • Business applications
    • All
    • Owned by me
  • Application services
    • All
    • Owned by me
  • Digital integrations
    • All
    • Owned by me
  • Digital Interfaces
    • All
    • Owned by me
  • Information objects
    • All
    • Owned by me
  • Business processes
    • All
    • Owned by me
  • Technology portfolio management
    • All
    • Indicator Scores
    • Logs
  • Technology reference model products
    • All
    • Owned by me
    • Technical Debt
  • Architectural artifacts
    • All
    • Owned by me
    • Diagrams

For most of the list categories, you can see two subsections: All and Owned by me.

  • All: Lists all the available records for the selected category.
  • Owned by me: Lists the records that are assigned to you.

For Technology Portfolio Management, you can view the complete list of hardware models and software products in the application portfolio, whether they have any or no lifecycle. The Logs table shows when the TPM data was refreshed using either the scheduled background job or manually updating the lifecycles for a business application using Update TPM Data option.

You can save, filter and export, or email the list. You can also create your own list and access it from the My Lists page.
