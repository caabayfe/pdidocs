Suggested steps generation: Record clustering

Records are clustered based on similarity.

Select Launch clustering to group records based on similar past closed incidents.

You should have at least 2000 records for successful clustering. If you get an error that there are not enough records to cluster, broaden your criteria when you set the conditions in the Choose inputs screen by setting the rule conditions to include more records or add more data.
