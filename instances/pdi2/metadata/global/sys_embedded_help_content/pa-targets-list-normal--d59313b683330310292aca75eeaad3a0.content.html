Targets

Targets are goals your organization wants to achieve. Targets show the difference between the desired score at a certain date and the actual score of an indicator.

A target can be personal or global. Outside of this table, personal targets are visible only to the owner. As an administrator, you also see them on this table. Personal targets have a value in the Owner field. An indicator can have both personal and global targets, which are listed here separately.

Targets are usually created and changed in either the classic Analytics Hub or in KPI Details on a Workspace Experience. Administrators have direct access to the table here to perform bulk functions or to delete targets.
