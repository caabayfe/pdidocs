Account and contact relationships

Relationships between accounts, partners, and contacts make it easy to manage accounts and assets.

Bi-directional account relationships

This type of relationship exists between:
  • A partner account and a customer account.
  • Two customer accounts.

An account relationship allows partner account contacts with the partner role or the partner administrator role to create and manage cases for their partner account.

View relationship details in the Account Relationships tab on the Account form. This information is available in the account record for either account in the relationship.

Contact relationships

In this type of relationship, a contact from a third account is added to either of the accounts in a bi-directional account relationship.

A contact relationship allows a contact with the customer role or customer administrator role to manage the account for which the contact relationship has been established. These contacts can view information and perform actions on behalf of the accounts, such as creating or updating cases from the customer portal.

View relationship details in the Contact Relationships tab on the Account form. This information includes the contact name, account, and the contact's assigned responsibility.
