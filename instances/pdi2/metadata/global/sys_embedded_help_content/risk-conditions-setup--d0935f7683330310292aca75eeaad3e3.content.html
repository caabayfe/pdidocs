Create, modify, or delete a risk condition

  • To create or modify a risk condition:
    1. Enter or modify the condition Name.
    2. Select the Risk or Impact values or both.
    3. Enter or modify the Description.
    4. Under Condition, add or modify the conditions that must be met to identify the risk.
    5. Click Submit or Update.
  • To delete a risk condition, click Delete.
