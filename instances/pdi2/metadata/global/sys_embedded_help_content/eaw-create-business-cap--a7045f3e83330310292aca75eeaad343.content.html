Create a business capability

Add a business capability according to your business requirement.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_apm.apm_analyst

Procedure

  1. Expand Business Capabilities.
  2. Select All.
  3. Select New.
  4. On the form, fill in the fields.
    For field information, see Create new business capability form.
