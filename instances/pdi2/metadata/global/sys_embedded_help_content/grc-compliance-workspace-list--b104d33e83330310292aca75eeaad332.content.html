Managing policy and compliance tasks

View the list of documents, processes, internal controls, tests, and functions in the form of records to centrally manage standards, policies, and internal control processes and map with external regulatory standards.

Selecting the List icon displays the List view of all compliance-related records, providing the summary of the record in a single view that helps in your analyses to take an informed decision.

  • Compliance library: repository of documents for regulatory compliance.
  • Scoping: helps to catalog and visualize upstream and downstream risks and controls based on the roll up of the related entities.
  • Policy acknowledgements: defines the audience who must provide an acknowledgement that a particular policy is in compliance.
  • Policy exceptions: request exceptions on policies, control objectives, or issues by specifying a reason for the exception.
  • Control attestations: attestations are surveys that gather evidence to prove that a control is implemented.
  • Control testing: tests that run periodically to provide documented evidence whether the associated enforced control is operating correctly.
  • Control monitoring: involves activities related to identifying and creating key risk and control indicators.
  • Evidence requests: used by audit and compliance teams for requesting supporting documents during an audit. The documents requested are used by the audit team to understand the in-scope controls and processes, and to begin fieldwork testing.
  • Issues: Any issue stemming from controls, risks, or compliance. Issues can include operational risk events, regulatory compliance violations, security breaches, or other negative results.
You can view the above categories of records in the Lists section. Apart from the categories of records, such as compliance library, scoping, and others, the List view also provides filtered lists of records within each category, such as All policies, All controls, My policies, and others. You can view a list of records created by all users or view only those that you have created. You can:
  • Click New to create a new record in the specific category.
  • Click a record in the Name column to get an overview and update its details in the Details related list.
