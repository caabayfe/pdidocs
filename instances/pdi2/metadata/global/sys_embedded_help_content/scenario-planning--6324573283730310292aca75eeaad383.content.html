Scenario Planning

Create and evaluate strategic scenarios to explore different planning approaches. Approve the optimal scenario to ensure alignment with organizational goals and strategic priorities.

This application enables you to do these tasks:
  • Create a scenario from a plan, or another scenario, in a simulated environment to compare it with the live plan and other scenarios.
  • Compare scenarios side by side to confirm alignment with primary goals, evaluate comprehensive planning, optimize resource usage, and confirm feasibility.
  • Approve a scenario that aligns with organizational goals and optimizes resource utilization.
