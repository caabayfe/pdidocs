Check out from a kiosk

Check out from a kiosk for a location. A kiosk is a place or desk where the agent or technician provides service to the requester.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_walkup.walkup_technician

About this task

A correct check-in and check-out enables you to effectively manage the workforce for a location.

Procedure

  1. Select the kiosk from which you want to check out.
    The kiosk record is displayed.
  2. Click Check-out.

Result

The technician is checked out from the kiosk and isn’t available for service. The date and time of the check-out is saved and can be viewed and accessed by admin.
