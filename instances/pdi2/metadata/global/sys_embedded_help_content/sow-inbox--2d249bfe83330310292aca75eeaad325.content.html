Work with the inbox

Interact with chat requests using the Service Operations Workspace inbox.

Using the inbox

As an agent, you can set your status to Available or Away to open or close the inbox for chat requests. When a requester initiates a chat conversation from any Service Portal page, the chat interaction appears in the inbox of all operators who are configured as group members for Agent Chat Queue.

When the automatic assignment of chat conversations is enabled, the chat conversation is automatically assigned to you, an interaction record is created in Service Operations Workspace, and you can continue the chat conversation. Based on options selected in the Chat - Most Capacity assignment rule, the interactions can open as non-active tabs in the inbox.

When the automatic assignment of chat conversations is not enabled, you can approve or reject a chat conversation. When you accept a chat conversation, an interaction record is created in Service Operations Workspace, and you can continue the chat conversation.

When the requester in Service Portal or the operator in Service Operations Workspace ends the chat conversation, the interaction closes.
