Configure how end-user actions are delegated

Role required:
  • sn_dlir.admin - Create, edit, and delete.
  • sn_dlir.analyst - View (read-only).

Prevent certain executives in the organization from receiving notifications about the incidents assigned or escalated to them. Consider a scenario where the executive is a user who belongs to a leadership or executive group. The executive shouldn’t receive emails about incident assignment or escalation. A delegated user can then take the necessary action on behalf of the executive.

  1. To create a Delegation Configuration, click New.
  2. On the form, fill in the fields.
  3. Click Submit.
