Incident on hold reasons

There are various reasons to place a change on hold. These reasons include gathering additional information during assessment or approval, agreeing on an appropriate schedule, and so on. The user must provide an explanation for placing the change on hold.

If the change request is placed on hold during assessment or approval and approvals are pending, then these approvals are marked No Longer Required. When the change request is no longer on hold, then the pending approvals are reinstated and are set to Awaiting approval.

A change request that is placed on hold can only be canceled or resumed by clearing the On hold check box.
