Incident form

The Incident form captures information about an incident. This information is used to quickly restore normal service operations. The information is also helpful for a post-resolution analysis to reduce the impact of similar future incidents.

Review the Incident form to determine whether it aligns with your business needs.

To modify the form layout, click the context menu and select Configure > Form Layout.
