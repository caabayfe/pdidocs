Service vendors

Keep track of the vendors you manage and all information related to the vendors such as relationship start date and total cost of contracts from one location.

Service vendor records overview

Analyze the following vendor-related information in the Service Vendors list:
  • Name: A descriptive name for the vendor record.
  • Vendor: Vendor name.
  • Vendor Score: The average overall score of all KPIs configured for the vendor.
  • KPI Group: The vendor KPI group to which the vendor belongs.
  • Vendor manager: The logged in user with the Vendor Manager [sn_vlm.vendor_manager] role that can configure vendor KPI metrics and can also view and manage vendors.
  • Vendor type: Vendors offering similar goods and services that are grouped together. You can analyze and evaluate vendors based on the types.
  • Rank tier: Organizational opinion of the products from the vendor such as Tactical Supplier or Valued Partner.
  • Relationship start date: The date on which your relationship with the vendor began.
  • Total cost of contracts: The cost of all contracts with the vendor.
  • Unique subscribers: The total number of subscribers to the product offered by the vendor.
You can manage the records by refreshing, exporting, or saving the list.
