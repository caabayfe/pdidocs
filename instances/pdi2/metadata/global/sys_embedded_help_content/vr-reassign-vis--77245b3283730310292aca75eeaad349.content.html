Reassign VIs

Remediation owners can update records for reassignment with the Unassign button in Vulnerability Response, Container Vulnerability Response, and Application Vulnerability Response.

Before you begin

If you determine that VIs assigned to you or your groups are not within your scope for remediation, you can clear the Assigned to and Assignment group fields on vulnerable item (VI) and remediation task (VUL) records. This feature is also supported for AVITs in Application Vulnerability Response in the classic environment. For VIs, this feature is available in both the Vulnerability Response workspaces and in the classic environment.

Procedure

  1. In either the classic environment, or in the IT Remediation Owner Workspace, navigate to a VIT record that is assigned to you that is in any state other than Closed or Resolved.
  2. To clear the Assigned to and Assignment group fields, at the top of the record, click Unassign.
    You and your groups are removed from the Assigned to and Assignment group fields, respectively. Unassigned is displayed in the Assignment type field, and the VIT is displayed on the list of the Unassigned module for Vulnerability Response.
  3. Alternatively, navigate to an AVIT in the classic environment that is assigned to you or your groups and click Unassign to clear the assignment fields.
    Unassigned is displayed in the Assignment type field, and the AVIT is displayed in the Unassigned module for Application Vulnerability Response or
  4. Optional: If you want to clear the assignment fields for multiple VIs on a list, select the VIs you want and click Bulk Edit.
    On the dialog that is displayed, select the Unassign check box.
    Notifications that records are unassigned are sent automatically to vulnerability managers or analysts that are added to the Unassign notification group.
