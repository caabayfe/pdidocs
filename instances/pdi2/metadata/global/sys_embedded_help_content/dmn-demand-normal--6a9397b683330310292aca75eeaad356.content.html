Create a demand

As a demand manager, you can promote ideas submitted from the Idea Portal or ideation module to demands, or you can create your own strategic and operational demands.

  1. On the Demand form, fill in the fields.
    Name
    Name of the demand
    Collaborators
    Users who can edit or contribute to the demand. A demand requester can select any user as a collaborator.
    Business case
    Business arguments that support the demand
    Risk of performing
    Risks if the demand is approved and implemented
    Risk of not performing
    Risks if the demand is not approved (for example, risk of loss of opportunity)
    Assumptions
    Assumptions made for the demand. Assumptions help to define scope and risks and fine-tune the estimates for time and cost.
  2. Submit the demand.

    Demand managers can provide further details to a demand by filling in additional fields, adding demand tasks, stakeholders, requirements, risks, decisions, and resource plans.

    Requesters or collaborators can edit their demands if the demand is in the Draft state.
