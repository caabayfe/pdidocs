Demands

The Demands list shows the demands in your instance.

Navigate to My Demands to see a list of the demands opened by you or including you as collaborator.
  • Click the column headers to sort, filter, and edit data.
  • Click the demand number to view and edit the demand details.
  • Select any row(s) and choose an action in the Actions on selected rows drop-down list to update the demand(s).
  • Click New to create a new demand.

Use the Related Links to assess your demands.
