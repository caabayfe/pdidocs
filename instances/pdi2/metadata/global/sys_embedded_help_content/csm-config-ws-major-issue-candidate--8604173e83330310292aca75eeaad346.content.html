Major Issue Management candidate cases

You can create major cases for issues that impact a wider audience. Use candidate cases to flag potential issues and bring them to the attention of a manager. Upon approval, candidate cases become major cases.

Major cases enable you to easily manage the communication and resolution process for issues that impact multiple customers.

You can promote an existing case as a major case candidate by selecting Propose Major Case from the More Actions menu on the Case form. Upon approval:
  • The system creates a major case.
  • The candidate case becomes a child of the major case.

You can also create a candidate case from the Candidates list by clicking New. Upon approval, the candidate case becomes the major case.
