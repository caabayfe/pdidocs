Preventive maintenance management

Perform maintenance planning for workplace services using Preventive maintenance management.

Preventive maintenance management enables you to do the following tasks:
  • Create maintenance plans for workplace models and locations.
  • Create schedules or copy an existing schedule for a maintenance plan.
  • Create meter-based or duration-based schedules.
  • Associate a template to a plan record, maintenance plan, asset, or location.
  • Schedule job checks for an associated template.
  • Track and monitor maintenance plan records.
  • Analyze metrics and trends to show preventive maintenance cases versus corrective maintenance cases.
