Schedule visualization exports

Automate the export of data visualizations to build presentations, share information with external users, or follow data over time.

Before you begin

Role required: You can schedule the export of any visualization that you created or that was shared with you. If you have the admin or viz_admin role, you can schedule the export of any data visualization.

Note: You cannot export calendar reports or indicator scorecards.

Procedure

  1. In the "File to export" section, configure the following information.
  2. Optional: To configure additional exported visualizations to include in this scheduled email, select + Add file.
  3. In the "Email schedule" section, specify when and how often you want to send the exported visualization.
  4. In "Email Details," specify the recipients and the email subject, and you can write a message in the email body.
  5. To send exports as ZIP files, turn on Send as ZIP files.
    Does not affect PNG exports embedded in emails.
  6. Select Save to save the schedule or Save and close to return to the Visualization Designer.
