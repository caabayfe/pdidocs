Scheduled Data Collection

Performance Analytics uses data collection jobs to collect indicator scores and snapshots. Indicator scores are the aggregated values of facts table records. Snapshots are lists of the records that contribute to the indicator score. Both scores and snapshots reflect the state of the data at the time they are collected. As indicator scores accumulate, it becomes possible to identify trends and forecast future indicator scores.

When you create a scheduled collection job, you determine when scores and snapshots are collected for a set of indicators.
