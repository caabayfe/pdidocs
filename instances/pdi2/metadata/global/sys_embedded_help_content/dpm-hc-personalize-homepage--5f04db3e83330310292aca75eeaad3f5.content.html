Personalize your homepage

Personalize your homepage by editing each solution's status conditions and by adding or removing solutions from your view.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_dpm.dpm_manager

About this task

Set the Needs attention attributes and thresholds that you want to track on solutions. After you personalize your homepage, your updates are saved in your user preference record (dpm_home_page_item_list) so you see your personalized homepage every time you log in to DPM.
Important: Your personalizations are unique to your login. Others in your organization don't see your updates, even when you change Needs attention attribute thresholds.

Procedure

  1. Select Personalize.
  2. Select the option to personalize your solution cards.
    • Select Edit status conditions to add more Needs attention attributes and to set different thresholds. Be sure to select the correct application tab to apply your changes to the correct solution type (business applications, services/offerings, or application services). Select Save to save your changes.
    • Select Add items to add solutions types to your homepage. Select the solutions that you want to add, and then select Add.
    • Select Remove items to remove solution types from your homepage. Select the solutions that you want to remove, and then select Remove.
