Viewing learning tasks in Service Operations Workspace

See which learning tasks, which include learning paths or courses, are pending completion and access them from the Service Operations Workspace landing page.

The My active learning section displays up to three learning tasks, which could include learning paths and courses, that are new, due soon, or overdue. The tasks are those that have been assigned to you or the ones you chose to self-enroll. The tasks are sorted by the due date and they're highlighted in red if they're overdue. You can also see the remaining duration to complete a task that you can select and complete.
