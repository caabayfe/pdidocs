Business applications

The Business Applications list shows the business applications in your instance.
  • Click the column headers to sort, filter, and edit data.
  • Click the name to view and edit the application details.
  • Select any row(s) and choose an action in the Actions on selected rows drop-down list to update the record(s).
  • Click New to add a new business application.

The Business Applications list includes information related to the application life cycle, architecture, ownership, contract, and compliance. Some significant columns include:

Business process
Business process for which the application is used
Application type
Custom (homegrown) or commercial (COTS)
Architecture type
Type of application architecture (client server, N-tier, platform application…)
Status
Operational status of the application
Data classification
Security level for the data in the application, which determines the applicable Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) policies
Certified
Indicates whether the application meets requirements or complies with organizational policies
