Create and resolve a safety incident

Create safety incidents that happened to employees (or other people) at your organization.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_ohs_im.operations_manager, sn_ohs_im.manager, or sn_ohs_im.agent

About this task

Note: If your admin has turned off the playbook and you're using a tab-based experience for safety incidents, you can associate multiple injuries and illnesses to a single incident.

Procedure

  1. In the Report an incident playbook tab, fill in the fields in the Enter incident summary activity and select Save.
    Note: If necessary, select Sensitive content to mark the incident as sensitive. Only users with the Sensitive content user [sn_ohs_im.sensitive_content_user] role are able to view this incident detail.
  2. To work on the incident, select your name in the Assigned to field.
  3. Record the information related to the incident using the following playbook activities and tasks.

    Selecting Mark complete marks an activity as complete and makes the next activity or stage available in the playbook. You can also select Skip to move to the next activity. You can go back to previous activities and update them later.

  4. Record your findings that you gathered about the incident in the Enter incident summary activity.
    • Use the Attachments section to attach findings in the form of image, video, and documentation such as a signed witness statement.
    • Use the Activity section to add findings in the form of free text.
  5. To close the incident, select Close Incident and then Resolve.
