Analyze the performance trends for your teams

Analyze trends to monitor your team's performance within a KPI group.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_mgr_workspace.manager

About this task

Analyze the data by drilling down into the data for a team, a specific team member, an indicator, or a case.

Procedure

  1. Select a date range to view your teams' performance for that time period.
    You can set the default date range using the sn_team_perf.default_date_range.
    All teams that you manage are identified with a My team highlight in the group header. You can also see the team that you have visibility into.
  2. Analyze team performance.
    1. Select a team.
    2. In the Performance tab, track and analyze the overall indicator performance for all members in the team.
