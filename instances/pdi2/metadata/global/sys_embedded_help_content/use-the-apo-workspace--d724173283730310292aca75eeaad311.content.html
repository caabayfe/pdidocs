Use the APO Workspace

APO specialists and agents use APO to create cases, route cases to agents, manage invoices, and resolve invoice exceptions.

Learn more about:
  • Invoice case categories and subcategories: An agent and Accounts Payable Specialist can access and work on the invoice cases in the Accounts Payable workspace.
  • Using Invoice Case Management: Agents can use Invoice Case Management to manage and work on invoice inquiry cases raised by employees and suppliers to resolve issues related to invoices.
  • Using Accounts Payable Invoice Processing: Accounts Payable Specialists can use the Accounts Payable Invoice Processing application to manage and work on invoice processing cases to perform end-to-end invoice processing, from invoice ingestion to approval.
  • Composing emails with predefined content from the Source-to-Pay Workspace: Insert predefined content into the message body of emails that you send from the email client in the Accounts Payable Workspace.
