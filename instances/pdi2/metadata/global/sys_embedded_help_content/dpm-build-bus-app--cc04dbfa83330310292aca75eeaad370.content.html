Track the progress of business applications

In the build phase, track the progress of the business application in the context of your solutions, including epics and stories in the current sprint.

Build tab

The build phase of the plan-build-run life cycle enables you to track the progress of work being done for your solutions.
  • Select View Roadmap to track the roadmap dependencies for your solutions.
  • View projects.
    • Select a graph status (color or month) to view the list of project records.
    • Select a card to see a list of the stated records.
    • Select an active project record to view its details, agile information, stories, epics, and more.
  • Select an epic card to view epic records that are in backlog or in progress.
  • Select a story card to view story records that are in backlog, in progress, or blocked.
  • View a list of related current sprints.
