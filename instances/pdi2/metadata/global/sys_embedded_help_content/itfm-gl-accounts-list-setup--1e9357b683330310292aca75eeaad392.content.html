General ledger accounts

If actual general ledger expenses are loaded into the application, a list of genral ledger accounts are generated based on available general ledger data.

By default, all general ledger accounts are generated as Operating (OpEx). To configure a general ledger account as Capital (CapEx), open the general ledger account record and modify the value in the Expense Type field to Capital.

To modify or delete a general ledger account, click the record in the Name column.

To add a general ledger account, click New.
