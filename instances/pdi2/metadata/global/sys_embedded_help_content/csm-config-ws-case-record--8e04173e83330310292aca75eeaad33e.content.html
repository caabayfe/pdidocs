Customer service cases

A customer service case stores detailed information about a customer and their question or issue.

You can use case records to gather information about customers and products and to track case activities, such as the steps taken to reach case resolution.

You can also use case records to:
  • Capture details of any communication between an agent and a customer or an agent and other employees within the organization.
  • Create additional records such as case tasks or work orders.
  • Create and send email to the customer.
  • Include case information in a knowledge base so that it can be used to help resolve other cases.
