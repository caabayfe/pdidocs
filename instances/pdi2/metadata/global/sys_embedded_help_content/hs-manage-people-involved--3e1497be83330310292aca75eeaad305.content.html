Add or modify people involved

People involved are individuals who are directly or indirectly connected to an incident or observation, such as an injured person, collaborators added to the watch list, and witnesses. These individuals can be from within or outside your organization.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_ohs_im.manager or sn_ohs_im.agent

About this task
