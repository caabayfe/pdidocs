Duplicate Relations Scorecard

Role required: itil has read access, itil_admin (on top of itil) has full access.
Configure the threshold for best, at risk, and critical state definitions for duplicate relationships metric.

Duplicate relationships are relationships that have identical parent and child CIs, identical relationship type, and an identical port.

  1. Select configuration item at the top of the Hierarchy list.
  2. If need to, click New to display the scorecard slider.
