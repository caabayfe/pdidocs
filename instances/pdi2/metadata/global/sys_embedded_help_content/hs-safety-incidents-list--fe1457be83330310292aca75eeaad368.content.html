Lists in Health and Safety workspace

Safety team members can quickly access incidents, observations, and actions by using the filtered lists under different categories. The lists that are displayed depend on your role.

Display list records

Select a list to display the corresponding list of records.

Create a safety record

Navigate to the list, for example, Safety Incidents or Safety Observations and select New.

Types of lists

Safety Incidents
A list of safety incidents in various states. Create safety incidents for employees or other people that occurred in your organization. Employees can also use the Employee Center to submit a safety incident.
Safety Observations
A list of safety observations in various states. Create safety observations that you receive from employees in your organization. Employees can also use the Employee Center to submit a safety observation.
Injury / illnesses
A list of all injuries and illnesses that happened in an establishment under your organization.
Actions
A list of corrective and preventive actions to eliminate the root causes of safety incidents and observations.
Establishments
A list of establishments under your organization. The recordable injury and illness records are grouped using this establishment information for reporting such as OSHA forms. You can view this list if you have the safety compliance manager [sn_ohs_im.compliance_manager] role assigned to you.
