Assessments and surveys

The My Assessments and Surveys list shows the assessments and surveys that are assigned to you, displayed as cards that allow you to open and complete them. Completed or expired surveys no longer appear in the list.

Click the survey or assessment card to launch it. If you start to take an assessment but cannot complete it, save your responses, and return to it later. When you have answered all the questions and are satisfied with the responses, submit the survey or assessment.
