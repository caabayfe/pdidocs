Health check

View results of the health scan performed on your Software Asset Management application's configurations in the Health check dashboard.

To install the Health Check application, go to the ServiceNow Store and activate the ITAM Health Check, com.sn_itam_health_chk, plugin.

You can get a detailed view of the health scan results such as health check score, suite checks available, health check issues by priority.

To drill down further, select on the results in a widget to take you to the list view page.

Multiple personas such as implementation partners, SAM managers, and support analysts can use the Health check dashboard.
