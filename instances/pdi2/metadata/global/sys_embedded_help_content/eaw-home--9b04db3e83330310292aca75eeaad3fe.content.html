Enterprise Architecture Workspace

The Enterprise Architecture Workspace empowers enterprise architects with a modern, data-centric user experience to accelerate decision making. As an enterprise architect you can meet your organization's business needs while reducing technology portfolio cost and risk.

Accessing and using the home page

To access the Enterprise Architecture workspace, navigate to Workspaces > Enterprise Architecture Workspace.

Widgets on the Enterprise Architecture Workspace home page show insights for your portfolio, items that need your attention, your portfolio overview and health. You can click each card in the widgets to drill down into and see more details.

Default Data

By default, the workspace displays data in the following sections:
  • Insights
    • Application Portfolio
    • Business Portfolio
    • Information Portfolio
    • Technology Portfolio
  • Needs Attention
    • My requests
    • Certifications
    • Assessments
    • Technology Portfolio Audit
  • Portfolio Overview and Health
