Enterprise asset overview

The Enterprise asset overview enables enterprise asset managers to get insights onto key metrics and make informed decisions about their enterprise assets.

The Enterprise asset overview lets you track and manage your enterprise assets by performing the following tasks:
  • Attend to critical action items that require your immediate attention such as entering missing manufacture data, model number, or asset tag.
  • Quick access to specific dashboards to search for asset requests, transfer orders, stock orders, recall orders, asset refresh requests, disposal orders, and Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) orders.

You can narrow your search results by using the Location, Stockroom, and Model category filters.
