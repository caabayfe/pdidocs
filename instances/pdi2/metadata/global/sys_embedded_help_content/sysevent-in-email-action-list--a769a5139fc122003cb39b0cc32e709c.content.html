Email listeners

You can setup email listeners that process emails sent to ServiceNow from third party monitoring tools.

Your ServiceNow instance can send email notifications for specific system events, such as updates to incidents or change requests. To support notifications, the default email configuration includes a POP3 mailbox on a ServiceNow email server and a ServiceNowSMTP server to process outbound email messages.

To test email notifications, enter a comma-separated list of email addresses in the Send all email to this test email address (non-production testing) field.

To setup email listeners, click New.
