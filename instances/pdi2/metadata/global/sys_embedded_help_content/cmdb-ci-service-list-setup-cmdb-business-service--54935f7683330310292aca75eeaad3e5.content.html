Business services

A business service is a set of interconnected devices, applications, and connections that are configured to offer a service. By defining business services, you can build comprehensive maps of all devices and applications in your organization that are used to provide services.

Review the sample business services in the list and customize them to align with your business needs.

To modify or delete a business service, click the service name in the list.

To add a new service, click New.
