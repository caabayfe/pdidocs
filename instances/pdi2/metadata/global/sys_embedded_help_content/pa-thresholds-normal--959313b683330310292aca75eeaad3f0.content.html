Threshold

Thresholds define a normal range of scores for an indicator and alert users when certain events occur, such as when a score reaches an all-time high or low.

Thresholds are usually created and changed in either the classic Analytics Hub or in KPI Details on a Workspace Experience. As an administrator, you can also add or edit a Threshold record directly here.

All thresholds apply a condition to a specific indicator. You can add thresholds to specific combinations of breakdown element and time series on that indicator.

Thresholds can apply either globally or personally. Personal thresholds have a specific owner. As an administrator, you can change the owner of a personal threshold.

In the Users list, you can control which users receive email notifications when a threshold is hit.
