Assign a remediation task to yourself

From a remediation task record, assign the record to your self in the Vulnerability Response workspace.

Before you begin

This option is only available on remediation task records that are not already assigned to you.

Procedure

  1. Navigate to Lists > Remediation tasks > Assigned to my group.
  2. Click a remediation task (VUL#) to open it.
  3. In the UI action buttons on the right, click Assign to me.

    The remediation task displays on the My Work list under Remediation Tasks on the List view.

  4. You can also assign records to yourself following the same steps from open records.
    The Assign to me button is only available if the record is not already assigned to you.
