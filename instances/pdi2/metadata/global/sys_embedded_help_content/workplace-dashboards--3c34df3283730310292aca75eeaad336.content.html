Workplace dashboards

View all your workplace dashboards at a single place.

The Workplace dashboards displays all the dashboards of various Workplace Core applications. You can select from the following dashboards:
  • Reservation Management Dashboard: View the information related to your workplace reservations. Refer to Reservation Management dashboard.
  • Synchronization Health Dashboard: View the information related to workplace calendar synchronizations. Refer to Synchronization Health Dashboard.
  • Visitor Reception Dashboard: View the information related to your workplace's visitor registrations. Refer to Visitor Reception Dashboard.
  • Workplace Case Dashboard: View the information related to workplace cases. Refer to Workplace Case dashboard.
  • Workplace Manager Dashboard: View the information related to workplace requests and more. Refer to Workplace manager dashboard.
