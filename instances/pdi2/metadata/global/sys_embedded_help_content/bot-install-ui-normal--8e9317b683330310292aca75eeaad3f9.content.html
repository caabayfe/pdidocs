Integrate ServiceNow Virtual Agent with third-party messaging apps

Role required: virtual_agent_admin

Install the third-party messaging apps on your ServiceNow instance to integrate with Virtual Agent.

  1. On the form, fill in the fields.
    Slack
    Installs the Slack workspace on your ServiceNow instance to integrate with Virtual Agent.
    Microsoft Teams
    Installs the Microsoft Teams tenant on your ServiceNow instance to integrate with Virtual Agent.
    Workplace from Facebook
    Installs the Workplace on your ServiceNow instance to integrate with Virtual Agent.
  2. Click Install.
