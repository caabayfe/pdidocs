Sort and group records in a list

After selecting a filtered list in the List view, you can sort and group the records so that you can easily find the record you want.

Select a filtered list to display the corresponding list of records. From this list, you can:
  • Sort the records. For example, sort case records by state.
  • Group the records into expandable lists.

Sort the records in a list

  1. Click a field name at the top of the list.
  2. Click the arrow icon next to the field name to sort in ascending or descending order.

Group the records in a list

  1. Point the mouse to a field name at the top of the list and click the More actions icon.
  2. Click Group by <field name> to group records by the available selections in that field.

    For example, click Group by Channel to group records by communication channel such as Chat or Web. Records in the list are combined into expandable groups.
