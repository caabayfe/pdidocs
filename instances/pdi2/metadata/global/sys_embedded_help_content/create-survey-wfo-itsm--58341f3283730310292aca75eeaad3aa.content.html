Create a survey in Workforce Optimization for ITSM

Create a survey for agents or groups to publish them. You can add other managers as owners of the survey.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_wfo_cfg_ws.manager

Procedure

  1. Select the Assessments tab.
  2. Select Surveys > View Surveys
  3. Add a survey.
  4. Configure the survey based on your needs.
    Note: To share the survey with other managers, you must add managers of those teams as the owners of the survey.
