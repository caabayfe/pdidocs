What you can do on this page

Configure a bot process record and create a robot by selecting the plus icon (). For more information, see Configuring a bot process record in RPA Hub and .

This RPA Hub workspace landing page contains the descriptions about the following basic score cards, lists, and reports that you can use to manage your digital workforce. You can view more details by selecting the sections. For example, select a bar chart on the queues summary to view the details like most pending work items, work items status, and Average Handling Time (AHT).

Bot process summary
Vertical bar charts of the incident records that involve bot processes or a robot. In the Insights section, view a donut report of the robot states. You can also see an overview of the change records that are associated with the robots, bot processes, and business applications that are related to the bot processes.
Robot utilization section
Chart legends of the highest and lowest utilized unattended and attended robots, over a period.
Process jobs summary
Donut report of the process jobs by status in the last 24 hours. For example, abandoned, canceled, failed, running, skipped, or success.
Spline report of the process job trend by status, over a period. Spline reports show how one or more values change over time by connecting a series of known data points with a curve that emphasizes the trend over individual data points.
List of the current running process jobs.
Queues summary
Vertical bar report of the queues that have the most work items pending, work items status, and Average Handling Time (AHT) per work item.
List of the queues with the work item status for each queue. For example, success, pending, in progress, or failed.
Top 5 business applications in the last 7 days
Vertical bar reports of the top 5 business applications used by the Robotic Process Automation (RPA) bot processes in the last 7 days.
License utilization
Semi-donut reports of the license utilization of the unattended and attended robots.

Lists view

A list displays the records in the table. From the list icon (), navigate to the tables on the one side of the list. When you want to see the related list views, go to the other side of the list.

You can create your own filtered lists to monitor your digital workforce by configuring My Lists. These lists are only visible to you. For more information, see Create My Lists in workspace.
