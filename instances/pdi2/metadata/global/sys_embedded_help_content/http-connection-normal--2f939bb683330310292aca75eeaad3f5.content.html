Specify the endpoint for your NLU provider

Role required: admin

Depending on your third-party NLU provider, you may need to enter endpoint information in the HTTP(s) Connection table.

  1. On the form, fill in the fields.
    Host
    Edit the URL to reflect the endpoint in your third-party NLU provider account.
    Base path
    Edit the path to reflect the endpoint in your third-party NLU provider account.
  2. Select Update.
