Create a new enterprise asset audit

Audit your enterprise asset inventory to determine where your assets are located and their current status.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_eam.enterptrise_asset_manager

About this task

You can use the Mobile Agent app to scan assets. If an asset is scanned and its bar code does not exist in our database, then by default that asset is mapped to an unknown model record. The unknown model record appears in the All enterprise models tab in the Model Management view. The asset manager manually associates this asset to the appropriate model. You can't change, update or delete an unknown model.

Procedure

  1. On the Create New Enterprise Asset Audit page, fill in the fields.
  2. Select Save.
    The asset audit inventory record is created and appears with Expected Assets and Scanned Assets tabs. The Audit Results section is activated and shows the details of the audit result when the audit is complete.
