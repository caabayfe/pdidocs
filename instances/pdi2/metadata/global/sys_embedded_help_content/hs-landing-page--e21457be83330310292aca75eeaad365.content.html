Health and Safety Workspace

Get visibility into key safety metrics and outstanding safety tasks using the Health and Safety Workspace landing page.

The workspace landing page enables you to do these tasks:
Get an insight into safety metrics
Components in this section display key safety metrics. Select a metric to display either a filtered list of records or a KPI details page.
Create safety incident or observation
Select New incident and New observation to create safety incidents and observations that are reported in your organization.
Review outstanding tasks
Use My assigned work and My team's work sections to review safety tasks assigned to you or your team. Click a record in this list to open it.

Select the list icon () to view a list of safety incidents, observations, actions, and more. You can access the list icon from any screen.
