Share a visualization

Share a visualization so that other users, groups, or roles can use it. A user can access a shared visualization in any workspace on the instance.

Before you begin

Role required: You can share any data visualizations that you created. When you share a data visualization, you can pass along the rights to share that visualization further. You also decide whether to share with editing rights or only viewing rights. If a data visualization has been shared with you with sharing and editing rights, you can also pass along editing rights. Users with the admin and viz_admin roles can share all data visualizations.

Procedure

  1. Expand the context menu and select Share.
  2. Enter the names of one or more users, groups, or roles you want to share the data visualization with. It is not possible to share data visualizations with inactive users.
    Note: Only users with the admin or viz_admin role can share visualizations with roles.
  3. To allow the people you share the data visualization with also to share that data visualization, select Allow recipients to share.
    When you add a user, group, or role as a viewer, they can only share the dashboard as a viewer. When you add a user, group, or role as an editor, they can share the dashboard as a viewer or as an editor.
  4. Select one of the following options.
  5. Select Confirm.
