Contract details

View details for your enterprise asset contract.

Use the following tabs to view and manage your enterprise asset contract:
  • Details: View details for your enterprise asset contract.
  • Assets Covered: View all enterprise assets that are covered by your contract.
  • Contract History: View the start date, end date, and terms and conditions for your enterprise asset contract.
  • Child Contracts: Create and view child contracts for your enterprise asset contract.
  • Expense Lines: Create and view expense lines that enable you to track costs and record expenses for your enterprise asset contract.
  • Approval History: View the approval history for your enterprise asset contract.
  • Terms and Conditions: Build and view the terms and conditions for your enterprise asset contract.
  • Service Commitment for Contract: Create and view service commitments for your enterprise asset contract.
