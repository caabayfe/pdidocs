Business calendars

Business calendars enable you to schedule events for your users and track their time across applications. You define schedules and calendars of all types, including fiscal calendars, in the Business Calendar menu.

Select New to create a business calendar.

Name
The unique reference name for the business calendar.
Label
The unique singular label for identifying the business calendar when you perform filtering.
Parent
The parent business calendar (if any).
Timezone
The timezone to use for the calendar's starting and end times.
