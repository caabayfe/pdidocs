Business calendar entry names

You can create a name for an individual business calendar entry. You create this record so you can easily identify a specific business calendar entry when you use it in a business calendar.

Select New to create a new name record.

Calendar
Name of the business calendar that is associated the business calendar entry.
Label
Unique label for the business calendar entry when you perform filtering.
Long name
Longer name. For example, January fiscal period.
Short name
Abbreviated name. For example, January.
