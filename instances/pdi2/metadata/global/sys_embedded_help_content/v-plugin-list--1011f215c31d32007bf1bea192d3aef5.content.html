Request the ITOM Metric Management plugin

The ITOM Metric Management plugin (com.snc.sa.metric) requires a separate subscription and must be activated by ServiceNow personnel. This plugin includes demo data and activates related plugins if they are not already active.

Role required: admin

To purchase a subscription, contact your ServiceNow account manager. The account manager can arrange to have the plugin activated on your organization's production and sub-production instances, generally within a few days.

Or, follow the steps below.
  1. Navigate to HI .
  2. Click Service Catalog .
  3. Click Request Plugin Activation , and fill out the form.
    Target InstanceSelect the instance on which to activate the plugin.
    Plugin NameEnter the name of the plugin to activate.
    Date and time you would like the plugin to be enabled

    Leave this field empty to have the plugin activated as soon as possible, or specify a date and time at least 12 hours in the future.

    Note:Plugins are generally activated during business hours in the Pacific time zone, but can be scheduled for a different time with advance notice.
    Reason/CommentsAdd any information that would be helpful for the ServiceNow personnel activating the plugin.
  4. Click Submit .
