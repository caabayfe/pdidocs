Scheduled job for handling OT alerts

This task requires the admin or cmdb_inst_admin role.
Note: Please ensure this step is done so that the alerts against OT devices are handled using OT-specific workflows and show up against the Industrial Workspace.

Before you begin importing the alerts related to OT devices into ServiceNow, it is important to activate this scheduled job. This scheduled job takes care of attesting the alerts that are related to OT devices, and stamps them as OT alerts by adding "sn_ot_device":"true" to the additional information field on the alert.
