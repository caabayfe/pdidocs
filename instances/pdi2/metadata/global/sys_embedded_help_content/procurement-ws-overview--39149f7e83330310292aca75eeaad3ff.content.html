Procurement view

View and manage procurement-related details such as procurement requests, purchase orders, sourcing tasks, and receiving slips.

The Procurement view includes important actions for managing your open requests, pending purchase orders and transfer orders, and escalating requests that require manager approval.

Select any widget or chart to view in detail and take the required actions. For details on the widgets, scroll down to the Procurement overview table.

You can also use Domain, Location, and Stockroom lists to narrow down your results.
Note: The Domain filter is available only when you’ve enabled the Domain Extensions Installer (com.glide.domain.msp_extensions.installer) and Domain Separation (plugin com.snc.pa.domain_support) plugins.
