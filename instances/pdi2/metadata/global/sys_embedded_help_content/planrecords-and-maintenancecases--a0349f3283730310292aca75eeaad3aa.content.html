Plan records and Maintenance cases

View the maintenance plan records and the workplace cases associated with the maintenances.

The Plan records tab displays the individual plan records. The details such as the schedule with which the plan is associated, the location for which it is created are displayed. The view also displayed the next run time, the last completion time and the requested due by.

The Maintenance cases tab displays the workplace cases, that is, the maintenance cases created for every maintenance schedule. View the details such as on what date the maintenance is scheduled on and more.
