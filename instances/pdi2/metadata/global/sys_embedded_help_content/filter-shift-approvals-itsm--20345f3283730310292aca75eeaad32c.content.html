Narrow down the display of agents using filters

Use filters to refine which agents you want to display in the team calendar. You can filter agents based on their location, region, skill, assignment groups, or any field in the agent's record.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_shift_planning.admin

Procedure

  1. In the Team Calendar tab, select the filter () icon.
  2. Click the desired filter.
  3. Move the desired criteria from Available to the Applied column.
  4. Click Apply.
    The agents that meet the criteria display in the team calendar.
