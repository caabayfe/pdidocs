Contacts and consumers

Customer Service Management supports two types of customers: contacts and consumers.

Contacts

A contact is an employee of a customer account. An account can have multiple contacts and one primary contact. This person is typically responsible for communication about the account.

Contacts for an account are listed on the Contacts tab of the Account form.

Consumers

A consumer is an individual customer who is part of a household. A household represents one or more consumers who typically share a common address and use goods or services as a group.

Consumers can have multiple addresses, with one address designated as the primary address. Addresses for a consumer are listed on the Addresses tab of the Consumer form.
