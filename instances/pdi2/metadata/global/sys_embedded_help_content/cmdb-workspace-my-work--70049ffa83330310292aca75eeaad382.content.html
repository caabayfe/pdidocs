My Work view

View and approve any CMDB Data Manager tasks assigned to you or the groups you are a member of. Task status provides an overall count of the tasks by their status and My tasks gives you a list view of Certification, Attestation, and all other tasks that you need to review.

These can include attestation tasks, or life-cycle tasks like archiving or deleting CI records. The list for CMDB admins can also include tasks that aren't assigned to anyone. CMDB Admins are also able to assign those unassigned tasks to another user or group. For managers, tasks of direct reports are included, and if you are a manager of a user group, then tasks of members in that group are also included.

Select Open tasks and Overdue tasks to review and process the tasks.

Task status

Provides overall counts of tasks by their status. Select a status card to show the associated tasks in the Certification tasks, Attestation tasks, and All other tasks tabs in the My tasks section.

My tasks

List view of Certification, Attestation, and All other tasks that are assigned to you.

  • Certification tasks: Review and process these tasks by checking audit results for the associated CIs and attribute values.
  • Attestation tasks: Review and process these attestation tasks by checking the physical existence of IT infrastructure or applications associated with the CIs in the tasks.
  • All other tasks: Review and process all the tasks aside from the Certification and Attestation tasks.
