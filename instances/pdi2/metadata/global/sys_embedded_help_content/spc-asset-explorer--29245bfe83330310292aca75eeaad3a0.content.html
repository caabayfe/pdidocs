Asset search

Set up queries to locate assets that match specific filtering criteria. You can refine your searches until you are satisfied with the results, and you can create policies from your search criteria.

This feature enables you to perform these tasks:
  • Create polices that return results you want. Get results from sample searches with the metadata reported by various tools, modify the search criteria as many times as you want, and confirm the completeness and accuracy of your results before you use the search conditions for a policy.
  • Select a 'base' policy and use its conditions as a start for your search.
  • Exclude results from existing policies on your searches with the Policy exclusion feature. Results that match excluded policies are ignored in your search results.
  • Search results are displayed on a tab on the Home landing page.
  • Save and activate your search criteria as a policy after you are satisfied with the results.
