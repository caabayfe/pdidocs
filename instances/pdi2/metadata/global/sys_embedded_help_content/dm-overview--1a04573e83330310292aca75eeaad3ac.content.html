Data Management overview

View a summary of your data usage and manage the growth of data on your instance.

Key benefits

  • View a summary of primary storage used and monitor data usage over time.
  • Determine which tables are consuming the most amount of storage on your instance.
  • Manage the growth of data on your instance directly from the Data Management Console.
Note: Data usage on the Overview tab is updated daily.

Primary storage used

View a summary of primary storage used on your instance.

  • View the total amount of primary storage used on your instance.
  • Track storage usage on your instance over time by viewing the percentage increase or decrease since a specific date.

Data usage trend

Monitor the top ten tables consuming the most amount of storage over time.

  • Determine if usage has increased or decreased over time.
  • Compare the total amount of primary storage used on your instance to the total amount of storage used by the largest tables on your instance.
  • Compare usage between the top ten largest tables on your instance over time.

Tables over 10 GB with the most growth

View the tables that have grown the most in size over the last 30 days. Only tables larger than 10 GB appear in the list.

  • View the size of tables with the most growth by checking the Table size (GB) column.
  • View the total size of associated attachments, audit records, and journal entries by checking the Associated record size (GB) column.
  • View the tables with the most growth over the last 30 days by checking the Growth (30 days) column.
  • View a table-level summary of data usage by selecting a table in the list.

Largest tables

View a list of the largest tables on your instance.

  • View the largest tables on your instance by checking the Table size (GB) column.
  • View the total size of a table's associated attachments, audit records, and journal entries by checking the Associated record size (GB) column.
  • View a table-level summary of data usage by selecting a table in the list.

Managing the growth of data

After determining the top tables to review, start managing the growth of data on your instance.

  • Manage the growth of audit records from the audit management console by selecting Manage audit config.
  • Delete older or unwanted records by selecting Create a cleanup rule.
  • Move data that's no longer needed to an archive table by selecting Create an archive rule.
