Analyze transcripts and sentiments for the Amazon Connect phone channel

Analyze your agent's call recordings, transcripts, and sentiment reports. With this information, you can assess the call quality and train your agents to handle calls better in the future.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_cti_core.user_manager

About this task

When an agent interacts with an external caller, the call gets recorded and transcribed. The application tags every statement within a transcript with a sentiment score. After a call has been completed, a sentiment report is automatically generated. The transcripts and sentiments are displayed in the Channels application in Workforce Optimization for ITSM.
Note:

To stream the audio recording for your interactions, you must have an active session with the Amazon Connect application.

To view sentiments generated from the Amazon Connect application, you must have at least one utterances for that interaction record.

Procedure

  1. Analyze transcripts or sentiments from an interaction record.
  2. Click Call Analysis.
    The Amazon Connect application generates the interactions and sentiment analysis of those interactions and displays them as a report in Workforce Optimization for ITSM. You can also assess the quality of completed interactions and and assign training to coach the agents.
