Create SNMPv3 credentials

SNMP credentials accept a privacy protocol and an additional privacy key
  1. Complete the fields in the form.
  2. To test your credential before you submit it, click Test credential under Related Links.
  3. Provide the connection specifics in the Test Credential dialog box and click OK.
  4. If the credential connects to the target, click Submit.
