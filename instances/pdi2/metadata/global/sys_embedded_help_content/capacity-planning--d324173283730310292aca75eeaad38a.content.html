Capacity Planning

View resource capacity allocations for your planning items.

This application enables you to:
  • View resource capacity allocations for a defined resource criteria.
  • Re-define resource criteria for planning items.
  • View the Gantt chart representation of the planning items based on their planned dates.
  • View focused resource capacity of a planning item by selecting a Gantt chart from the bottom tray.
  • Toggle the capacity planning view.
