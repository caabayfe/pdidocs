Create a business application

Use the Business Application form to add the applications that your organization uses based on their functions and the business processes they fulfill.

  1. On the Business Application form, fill in the required fields.
    Name
    Name of the business application
    Description
    Unique description of the application
    IT Application Owner

    Person who owns the application from the IT side.

    The business application must have an owner assigned to it.

    If you are designated as the IT Application Owner, you can view all your applications in the My Applications list in Application Portfolio Management (APM).

  2. Save the business application.

    You can provide further details to a business application by filling in the additional fields.

    In APM, navigate to Application 360 to see all the available information for a business application.
