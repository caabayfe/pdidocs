Configure incident consolidation rules to consolidate your DLP incidents

Role required:
  • sn_dlir.admin - Create, edit, and delete.
  • sn_dlir.analyst - View (read-only).

You can use the incident consolidation rules to consolidate multiple incidents of similar nature under one parent incident. The DLP incident consolidation rules enable you to consolidate the DLP incidents based on the configuration provided for Consolidation duration and Consolidation identification.

  1. To create an Incident Consolidation Rule, click New.
  2. On the form, fill in the fields.
  3. Click Submit.
