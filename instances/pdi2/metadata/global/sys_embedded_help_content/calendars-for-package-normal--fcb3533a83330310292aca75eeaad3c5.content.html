Pair a business calendar with a package

Pair a business calendar that you created with a package in the ServiceNow AI Platform. When you associate a business calendar with a package, you can use the calendar in that specific functional area. Until you pair a business calendar definition with at least one package, it remains a definition record only.

  1. On the form, fill in the fields.
    Calendar
    Name of the business calendar that you want to associate with a Now Platform package.
    Package
    Name of the ServiceNow AI Platform package that you want to associate with the selected business calendar.
  2. Select Submit.
