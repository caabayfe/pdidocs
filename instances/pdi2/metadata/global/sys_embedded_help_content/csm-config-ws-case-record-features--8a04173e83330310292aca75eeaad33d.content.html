Case form features

A customer service case form includes a number of features that give you quick access to important information.

Form header

The form header provides a snapshot of case, account, or contact information. Form headers include a primary value and several secondary values. Secondary values can appear below the primary value at the top of the form or in the Contextual side panel.

Form ribbon and components

The form ribbon provides a brief overview of the case details. It includes components that display relevant information such as the case timeline, active SLAs, and the estimated time to resolution. The form ribbon can appear below the form header or in the Contextual side panel.

Tabs and related lists

A number of tabs appear on the Case form below the short description. Case form fields appear in the Details tab. Other tabs store related list information, such as case tasks, emails, and attached knowledge articles. To display additional tabs, click More.

Special handling notes

Special handling notes bring important information about individual records to your attention. If special handling notes are available for a record, these notes appear in a pop-up window when you open the record. For each note, you can see the priority, short description, and message.
