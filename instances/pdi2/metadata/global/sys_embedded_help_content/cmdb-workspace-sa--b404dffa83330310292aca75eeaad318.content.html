CMDB success advisor

CMDB success advisor enables you to review and improve CMDB rules, policies, and settings tailored to specific applications like Hardware Asset Management (HAM).

This application enables you to do these tasks:

  • Identify and address data quality issues specific to Hardware Asset Management (HAM) in the CMDB.
  • Improve HAM data coverage by analyzing existing and suggested data integrations, including Discovery patterns and Service Graph Connectors.
  • Monitor and optimize CMDB settings that support CI-to-asset synchronization, life cycle alignment, and Hardware Asset Management (HAM) data quality.
  • Adjust model category selection to optimize HAM dashboard data coverage in CMDB success advisor for achieving your current HAM goals.
