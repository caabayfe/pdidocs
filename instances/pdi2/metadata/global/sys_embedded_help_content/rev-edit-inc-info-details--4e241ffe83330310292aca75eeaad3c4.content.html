Review and edit information on the Details tab

Review the incident information on the Details tab of the incident record and edit, if required.

Before you begin

Role required: itil

Procedure

  1. Click the Details tab.
    You can summarize an incident from the Details tab.
  2. Review or edit the information.
  3. Click Save.
