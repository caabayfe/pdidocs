Add a business service in Operational Resilience Workspace

Add a business service in Operational Resilience Workspace so that you can configure its related tabs, such as the tabs for change requests, incidents, and tasks. You can then view the service attributes and resilience metrics on the dashboard.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_grc.admin

Procedure

  1. Navigate to All > Operational Resilience Workspace > Services > Business Services.
    A list of the available business services is displayed.
  2. Select New.
    The Create New Service form is displayed.
  3. In the Details tab, fill in the fields.
    The Details tab in the Business Service form is displayed.
  4. To save the details on the service, select Save.
  5. To add the business service to the Operational Resilience reporting, select Add to OpRes reporting.
  6. To view the infrastructure relationship of the service, select Open dependency view.
    It displays the infrastructure relationship of a service with its parent services and child services.
  7. To view the relationship of the service with its related entities, select 360° view.
    The relationship of a service with its related entities, such as the dependencies and red flags in a 360-degree view, is displayed.
  8. To save the details on the form, select Save.
    The Service form with its related tabs is displayed.
