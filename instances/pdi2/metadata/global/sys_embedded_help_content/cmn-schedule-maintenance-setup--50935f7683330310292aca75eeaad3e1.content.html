Create, modify, or delete a maintenance schedule

  • To create or modify a maintenance schedule:
    1. Enter or modify the schedule Name.
    2. Enter or modify the Description.
    3. Select a Time zone for the schedule.
    4. From the Applies to list, select the CI classification to which the conflict detection is filtered.
    5. Under Condition, add or modify the conditions to specify the CIs to which the schedule applies.
      The Condition field is displayed only when a CI classification is selected from the Applies to list.
    6. Right-click the header and click Save.
      The blackout schedule is created or modified.
    7. To associate a new schedule entry to the blackout schedule, click New under Schedule Entries.
    8. To modify or delete a schedule entry associated with the maintenance schedule, click the name of the schedule entry.
  • To delete a maintenance schedule, click Delete.
