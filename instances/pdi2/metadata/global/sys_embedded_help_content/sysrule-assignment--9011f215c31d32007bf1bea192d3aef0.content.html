Create, modify, or delete an assignment rule

  • To create or modify an assignment rule:
    1. Enter or modify the rule Name .
    2. Under Applies To , add or modify the conditions to trigger the assignment rule.
    3. Under Assign To , specify the group or user that you want the matching incidents to be assigned to.
      Either enter a group or a user name, or click the lookup icon to search for and select the group or the user.
    4. Click Submit or Update .
  • To delete an assignment rule, click Delete .
