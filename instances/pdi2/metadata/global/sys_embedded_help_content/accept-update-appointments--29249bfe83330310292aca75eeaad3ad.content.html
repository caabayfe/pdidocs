Accept and update appointments

Accept and update the appointment details.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_walkup.walkup_technician

Procedure

  1. Select the appointment that you want to accept or update.
  2. On the details tab, fill in the fields.
    Note: You can view the interactions that are linked with the appointment by clicking the open record icon () in the Interaction field.
  3. Click Save.
