Recommended Fields

Role required: itil for reading, itil_admin (on top of itil) for full access.
Define a list of CI fields as recommended, noting that it is desirable that they are populated by a data source such as Discovery.
  1. Use the slushbucket to move the fields that you want to designate as recommended, from the Available list to the Selected list.
  2. Click Save.
