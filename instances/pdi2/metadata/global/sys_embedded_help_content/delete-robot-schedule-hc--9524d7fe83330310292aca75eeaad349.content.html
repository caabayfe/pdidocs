Delete a robot schedule

Delete an existing robot schedule of a bot process that you no longer need.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_rpa_fdn.rpa_release_manager, sn_rpa_fdn.rpa_developer, or sn_rpa_fdn.rpa_admin

Procedure

On the Robot Calendar tab, delete a schedule by either right-clicking an event or selecting the event's pop-up window.
If you’re deleting a published bot process, the life-cycle change status is changed to In Maintenance.
