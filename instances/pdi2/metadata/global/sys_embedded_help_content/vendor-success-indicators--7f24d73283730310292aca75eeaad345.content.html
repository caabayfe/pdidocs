Vendor Success Indicators

Gain insights into how your vendors are performing in comparison with other vendors in your organization.

Monitor the attributes that define your vendor performance and compare them with top-performing vendors. The success indicators provide insight into the top five attributes across all vendors that contribute to high performance.

The widget displays the score for the defined attribute and the rank shows the overall vendor ranking for that attribute.
