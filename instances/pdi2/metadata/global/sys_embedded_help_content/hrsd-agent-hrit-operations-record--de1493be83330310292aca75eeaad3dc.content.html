Human Resources Information Technology (HRIT) Operations case record

Case records related to account setup, access, and reports.

Create cases for IT related needs and requests. Here are some areas related to HRIT Operations:
  • Accounts / Account Access Control
    • HR Account Access Request
    • HR Accounts Inquiry
    • Password Reset
    • Setup New Hire HR Profile
  • HR System Support
    • HR Portal Support Request
  • Reporting
    • Report Inquiry
    • Report Request

Sidebar navigation icons

When working with HR case records, select a sidebar icon to help you with the case. The sidebar navigation icons are on the far right side of the workspace page.
Note: Not all side bar icons are available with all records.
  • At a glance: A quick summary of the employee's information and case-related activities.
  • Agent assist: Provides information and links to knowledge articles related to the type of case. You can also provide comments (visible to employee) or work notes (not visible to employee) or view all activities related to the case.
  • Attachments: Documents associated with an HR case that are attachments. Attachments can be moved to Employee Document Management (EDM) for to archive for historic records.
  • Response templates: Shows reusable messages that you can use when responding to an employee that has a question about their case.

    Copy the text from the response template you want to use and paste the text into the Comments or Work notes section of the HR case.

  • Fulfillment instructions: Provides you with focused and targeted information on how to complete an HR case faster and more efficiently.

    Instructions can include detailed instructions, KB articles, or a checklist of what to do on the case.
