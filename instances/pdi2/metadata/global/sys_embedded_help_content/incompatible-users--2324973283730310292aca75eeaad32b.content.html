Fixing role assignments for incompatible users in Subscription Management

If users have roles that require a subscription but have not inherited the roles directly from groups, you need to remove the role assignments from the users and add the users to recommended groups.

For ease of administration, users should inherit their role assignments from their groups. However, some users who have roles that require a subscription don't inherit role assignments this way for one of the following reasons:
  • The role was granted directly to them instead of being inherited from a group
  • The role was removed from the group that originally granted it

To fix these issues, remove the role assignments from the users and add the users to recommended groups.
