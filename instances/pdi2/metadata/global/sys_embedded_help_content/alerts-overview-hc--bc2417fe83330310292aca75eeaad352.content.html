Alerts

Alerts are notifications that you receive when a bot process runs in RPA Hub. The notifications are generated by the alert rules. For example, you can view the alerts that pertain to robots, schedules, process jobs, and so on.

What you can do on this page

You can view the notifications that are generated by the alert rules. Open an alert record to view more details such as the alert type, alert priority, alert message, and so on.
