Add a KB article to an alert

Add your own knowledge base (KB) article to an alert that was generated by Health Log Analytics. For example, you can provide additional information that might help to resolve the underlying issue.

Before you begin

Role required: evt_mgmt_operator or evt_mgmt_admin

Procedure

  1. Select the more actions icon () and then choose Create KB article for this issue.
  2. On the form, fill in the fields.
  3. Select Save.
  4. When the content of the article is final, select Publish.

Result

The KB article is added to the alert. Health Log Analytics also uses your article to enhance similar alerts with the same Event ID property or alert pattern.
