Risk roll up configuration

Role required: admin or sn_vul.manage_risk_score_configuration

Based on the VITs available for the OT devices linked to the equipment model entities, the risk score is computed.

To calculate the Risk score at equipment model entity, the following attributes are considered:
  • Max Risk score of the VITs associated to the equipment model entity.
  • Average Risk score of the VITs associated to the Equipment model entity.
  • Number of vulnerable items per equipment model entity.
