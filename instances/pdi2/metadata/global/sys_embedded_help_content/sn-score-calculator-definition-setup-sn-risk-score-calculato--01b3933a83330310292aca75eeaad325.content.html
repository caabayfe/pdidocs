Risk Labels for Operational Technology Obsolescence Management

Role required: sn_risk_score_calc.admin role or sn_risk_score_calc.user

Perform the following steps to modify or add a new Risk Label to a Risk Definition:

  1. To modify the Risk Label of a Risk Definition, modify the Risk label form fields of the Score range labels related list according to your requirement.
  2. To create new Risk Label, navigate to All > Risk score calculator > Risk calculator definition.
  3. Open the Risk calculator definition where the Risk Labels needs to be added.
  4. Select the Score range labels related list.
  5. Select New.
  6. This opens an empty form to provide the details of Risk label
  7. Provide the following details for Risk label: Score label, Low, and high.
  8. Provide details in the Risk Calculator Definition empty form.
  9. Select Update.
