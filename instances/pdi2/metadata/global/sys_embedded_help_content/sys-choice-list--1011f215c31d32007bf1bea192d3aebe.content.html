Change request categories

Classifying change requests by categories and subcategories improves the clarity and granularity of report data. The categories and subcategories can also be used by the platform to automatically assign a change request to a specific fulfillment group to work on it.

Review the default change request categories and sub-categories to determine whether they align with your business needs.

To modify or delete a default category or sub-category, click the corresponding choice in the list.

To add a new category, click New .
