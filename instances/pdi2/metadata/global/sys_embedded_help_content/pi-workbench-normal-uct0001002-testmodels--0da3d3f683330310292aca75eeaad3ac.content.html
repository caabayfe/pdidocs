Predict Category model testing

Testing your models is a critical step in ensuring that the theoretical likelihood of a correct prediction is met in real outputs. Use real world examples to evaluate the efficacy of your models and help guide your roll out plan.

Consider testing different rows to see if a legacy rule or data-driven rule is triggered. When you understand why a certain prediction was applied, then you can confidently deploy your model.

To assess the quality of classification models, you may also consider other relevant metrics such as recall, F1 score, and more.

Glossary

Single testing:
Testing a single input to get a prediction.
Batch testing:
Testing a table of incident records to get many results at once.
Confidence:
The percentage likelihood that the model output is accurate. Unlike precision which refers to the model, confidence refers to the output itself.

What to do next

The next step in the process is to integrate the best performing model and decide on the retraining schedule. You'll need to understand the different scenarios in which you might trigger and apply the prediction, which may be specific to your implementation.
