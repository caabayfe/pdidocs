Configure the risk rollup definition for OT Obsolesence Management

Roles required: sn_risk_score_calc.admin role or sn_risk_score_calc.user role

A default Risk Rollup Calculator Definition shipped with the Risk score calculator Plugin. For more information on Risk Rollup Calculator Definition, refer to OT Obsolescence documentation.

  1. Modify the form according to your requirement and select Update.
  2. You can also create a new risk rollup definition:
    1. Navigate to All > Risk score calculator > Risk calculator definition.
    2. Select New.
    3. In the new form, you can provide the following details of Risk calculator definition: Name, Description, Score type, Formula and Schedule.
    4. Select Submit.
