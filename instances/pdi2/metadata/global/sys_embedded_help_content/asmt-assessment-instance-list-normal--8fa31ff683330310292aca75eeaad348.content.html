Assessments or Surveys Instances List

A survey or assessment instance represents one occurrence of a questionnaire assigned to one user. You view an instance to verify that instances were created, to check the state of the instance, or to reassign the instance.

To open the assessment or survey instance, click the Number link.

To view the configuration details of the assessment or survey in that instance, click the Metric type link.

To view the user to whom the survey or assessment was assigned, click the Assigned to link.
