Inventory view

View the inventory functions such as asset audits, disposal orders, loaner asset orders, RMA orders, RMA line items, and transfer orders and act accordingly.

Select any widget or chart to view in detail and take the required actions. For details on the widgets, scroll down to the Inventory overview table.

You can also use Domain, Location, Stockroom, and Model category lists to narrow down your results.
Note: The Domain filter is available only when you’ve enabled the Domain Extensions Installer (com.glide.domain.msp_extensions.installer) and Domain Separation (plugin com.snc.pa.domain_support) plugins.
