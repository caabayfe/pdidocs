Assess the quality of completed tasks

Assess the ability of your agents to resolve cases/incidents or the quality of their completed tasks.

Before you begin

Role required: sn_coaching.coach

About this task

Coaching assessments also get created and assigned to the coach based on the trigger conditions that are defined in coaching opportunities.

Procedure

  1. Select the Coaching Assessments icon ().
  2. On the form, fill in the fields to assess the agent.
  3. Select Complete Assessment.
