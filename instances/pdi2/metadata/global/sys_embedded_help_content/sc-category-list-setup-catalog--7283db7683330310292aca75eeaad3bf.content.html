Service catalog categories

Service catalog categories organize service catalog items to help users find what they need.

Review the default categories to determine whether they align with your business needs.

To modify or delete a default category, click the category title in the list.

To activate or deactivate a category, select the check box next to the category title and then click Activate or Deactivate.

To add a new category, click New.
