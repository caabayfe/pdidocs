CMDB Health

Review the health metrics of CIs. Proactively identify and address problematic configurations and practices around your CIs and CMDB data using the visibility that CMDB Health provides.

Review the percentage numbers to navigate to the CMDB Health dashboards.

The percentage number represents the health of all CIs as an aggregation of the following Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for health:
  • Completeness: CIs are tested for required and recommended fields that are not populated.
  • Correctness: CIs are tested against predefined data integrity rules such as identification rules (to detect duplicate CIs), orphan CI rules, and stale CI rules.
  • Compliance: The CMDB data is audited for adherence to predefined certificates.

To drill down into the CMDB Health widget, you need the asset or itil role in addition to any roles required to access CMDB Workspace.

Select factors that affect your score to see the breakdown of the score percentage by the three key KPIs (Completeness, Compliance, and Correctness).
