Now Assist panel settings

Enable or disable the Now Assist Panel on your instance.

The Now Assist panel provides users with generative AI capabilities all within the Next Experience global navigation as a panel on the right side of the screen. If you disable the Now Assist panel, Now Assist skills will still be available on forms and in workspaces.

This panel enables you to do these tasks:
  • Enable or disable the Now Assist panel using the Turn on or Turn off buttons.
  • Learn about the Now Assist panel in the Details section.
  • View helpful resources and answers to common questions using the links on the right edge of the panel.
