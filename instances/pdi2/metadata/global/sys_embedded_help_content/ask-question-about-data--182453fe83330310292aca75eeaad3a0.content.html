Ask a question about your data

You can make natural language queries related to indicators, tables, or columns with Analytics Q&A.

Asking questions

As you type in a query, Analytics Q&A suggests recent searches, indicators, tables, and columns that match what you have typed so far. Only the tables and columns to which you have access are shown.

If Analytics Q&A cannot determine which table you want, it shows you up to three likely tables to choose from.

Results

If your query is successful, Analytics Q&A shows your result as whichever of the following visualizations you specified. If you do not specify a visualization, Analytics Q&A determines which visualization is the most appropriate.

The results are subject to an ACL security check against your roles. Visualizations based on tables follow Reporting security. Visualizations based on Performance Analytics follow PA security.

If you do not want to see the change in scores over time, click Click here to see only the current data. Analytics Q&A returns the current values on the underlying table instead of the indicator scores.

Hold your cursor over the chart to get more information about breakdowns. In a time series trend, you get the values for all the breakdowns on the selected day. In a column chart of the latest scores, you get the score of the breakdown you select. If the result is a visualization for an indicator, click on a value to open the indicator in KPI Details.

New queries

To make a new query or view a list of your KPIs, click Back. Returning to the Analytics Center updates your list of the last five successful queries.

How to make a valid query

When you write a query, use special keywords to denote the type and range of information you are looking for, filters you want to apply, and other parameters. To see a full list of keywords and their uses, click How can I improve my results?.

You can ask your ServiceNow AI Platform administrator to add synonyms and semantic shortcuts for keywords.
