Customize your tabular view.
  • Select the refresh list icon () to refresh the list.

Customize your column view.
  • Move your cursor to any blue item in the view and select the column filter icon () to filter the column.
    • Show Matching displays matching items in the selected column.
    • Filter Out filters out the selected item from the column.
  • Move your cursor to the left of any blue item in the view and select the open preview icon () to open a preview of that item.
