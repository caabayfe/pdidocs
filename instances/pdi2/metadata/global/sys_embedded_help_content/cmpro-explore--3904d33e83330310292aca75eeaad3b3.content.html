Explore Contract Management Pro

Contract Management Pro is a solution that's easy to use and adopt. You can use it to set up contract document templates, clauses, clause variations, and to initiate contract requests. It also supports Now Assist driven contract analysis and metadata extraction, e-signatures, wet signatures, and external storage systems.

Contract Management provides the following benefits:
  • Create self-served or non-self-served contract requests.
  • Revise, approve, and provide a signature for a non-self-served contract request.
  • Get review and feedback from the internal subject matter experts on contract documents.
  • Get an insight on the volume of contract requests that are handled by your team.
  • Work with actionable widgets to categorize, prioritize, and efficiently work on contract requests.
  • Track and manage contract obligations to help ensure compliance and minimize risks.
  • Use Now Assist in Contract Management to analyze contracts for non-standard and missing clauses, and to extract information from signed contracts to add in the contract repository.
  • Create contract templates using Microsoft Word add-in.
