Relationship Scorecard

Role required: itil has read access, itil_admin (on top of itil) has full access.
Configure the threshold for best, at risk, and critical state definitions for the relationship KPI.

The relationship KPI is an aggregation of the orphan relationship, stale relationship, and duplicate relationship metrics, according to the relationship scorecard weight settings.

  1. Select configuration item at the top of the Hierarchy list.
  2. If need to, click New to display the scorecard slider.
