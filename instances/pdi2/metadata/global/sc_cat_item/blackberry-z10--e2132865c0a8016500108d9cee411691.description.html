For those requiring internet access and email on the move.

The BlackBerry Z10 is a 4.2" touch screen cell phone with the following specifications:

  • 16GB internal storage
  • 4G ready
  • 1280 x 768 resolution
  • 8 megapixel camera
  • BlackBerry 10 operating system

 

* requires monthly service plan payments as part of a yearly contract
