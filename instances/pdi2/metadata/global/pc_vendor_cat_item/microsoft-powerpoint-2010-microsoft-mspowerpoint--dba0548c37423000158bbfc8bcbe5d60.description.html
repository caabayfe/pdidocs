PowerPoint gives you more ways to create and share dynamic presentations with your audience than ever before. Exciting new audio and visual capabilities help you tell a crisp, cinematic story that's as easy to create as it is powerful to watch. With new and improved tools for video and photo editing, dramatic new transitions, and realistic animation, you can add polish to presentations that will captivate your audience.

  • Set your presentations apart with many new and improved slide transitions, including high-quality 3D transitions that rival broadcast graphics
  • Use dozens of additional SmartArt layouts to create many types of graphics, such as organization charts, lists, and picture diagrams
  • Transform your images into compelling, vibrant visuals by using new and improved picture-editing features such as color saturation and temperature, brightness and contrast, and an advanced cropping tool, along with artistic filters such as blur, paintbrush, and watercolor
  • View and edit multiple presentations independently, side by side, or even on separate monitors
  • Use the new Backstage view to save, share, print, and publish your presentations with just a few clicks
  • Embed and edit video files directly in your presentation
