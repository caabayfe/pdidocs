Microsoft Word offers the best of all worlds: enhanced features to create professional-quality documents, easier ways to work together with people, and almost-anywhere access to your files. Designed to give you the finest document-formatting tools, Word also helps you easily organize and write your documents more efficiently, and stay within reach so you can capture your best ideas whenever and wherever they occur.

  • Edit papers and share ideas with others at the same time with co-authoring*
  • Create special picture effects with built-in picture editing tools
  • Post your documents online and then access, view, and edit them from almost any computer or your Windows phone**
  • Write, edit, and organize your documents more efficiently with improved document-formatting tools
  • Transform basic, bullet-point text into compelling visuals that better illustrate your ideas
  • Recover draft versions of recently edited files easily, even if you never saved the document
