Features

  • Print and copy at up to 8 ppm in color, and at up to 12 ppm in black & white 
  • Quick First Print provides first print time of approximately 22 seconds in black & white and 28 seconds in color 
  • WiFi Capabilities: Print, Scan and PC Fax at up to 802.11n speed
  • Scan in color up to 9600 dpi quality (interpolated) with up to 600 dpi optical resolution 
  • Scan to USB for documents on the go 
  • 150-sheet cassette plus 1-sheet multipurpose tray
  • Up to 50-sheet ADF (Automatic Document Feeder) 
  • 33.6 Kbps Super G3 fax 
  • Remote device management through embedded web server 
  • USB 2.0 Hi-Speed for optimal performance 
  • Efficient energy-saving mode; approximately 2.1W (wired), 2.5W (wireless) of energy used 
  • User-friendly 5-line LCD for easy navigation 
  • Password-protected Department ID Management helps to control device usage 
  • Single Cartridge System for cost-efficient supplies replenishment
