Model options​

Welcome to the Model Options where customizing and refining your process graph is made simple and efficient. These features are designed to give you control over how you view and analyze your process.​

Activities and Connections Slider​
Adjust the Activities and Connections slider to zoom in on the process graph. This tool allows you to focus on the intricacies of your workflow, down to the finest detail.​

Perspectives​
Utilize the Perspectives feature to assess your process from a selected viewpoint. By filtering out all activities except those that match a specific definition, you can isolate segments of your workflow for targeted perspective analysis.
