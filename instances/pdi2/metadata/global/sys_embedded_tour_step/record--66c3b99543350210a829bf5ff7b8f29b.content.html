Scheduled tasks​

Your central command for tracking and retrieving the outputs of ad-hoc process mining tasks. This interface is crafted to keep you informed about the progress of ad-hoc mining tasks, and allows you to open completed tasks. ​

Instantly view the status of your on-demand tasks. Whether queued, in progress, experiencing errors, or completed, you have a complete, real-time update of each task's current state.​

Once a task like a cluster analysis or an applied filter view is complete, access its results directly from the pane.
