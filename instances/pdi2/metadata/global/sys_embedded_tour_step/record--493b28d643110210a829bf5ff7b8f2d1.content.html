Node and Arc popup and options​

The Inspection section serves as a detailed analytical suite for nodes and arcs.​

Show Records: Reveals the specific records that pass through a selected node or arc, providing a detailed account of the data flow within the process.​

Work notes Analysis: Clusters records based on the similarity of agent work notes, offering insights into the reasons behind specific process transitions.​

Requestor intent (Cluster): Sorts records into clusters according to their descriptive attributes, aiding in the understanding of their underlying requestor intent.​

Key Contributors (RCA): Identifies and highlights the dimensions most associated with certain inefficiencies, shining a light on potential areas for process improvement.​

Filter on transitions: Concentrates on refining the process examination to specific transitions, allowing for a more targeted and narrowed analysis.
