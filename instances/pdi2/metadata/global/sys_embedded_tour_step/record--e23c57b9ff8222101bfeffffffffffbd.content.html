Process comparison​​

​It assist you in evaluating the efficiency of your processes by juxtaposing your current process graph with an ideal or designed process graph. After pinpointing inefficiencies, it enables you to conduct conformance checking to identify discrepancies between the actual process flow and the theoretical model. Through this comparison, you can quickly discern where your process graph and its breakdowns diverge from the envisioned ideal, facilitating targeted improvements and alignment with best practices.​
