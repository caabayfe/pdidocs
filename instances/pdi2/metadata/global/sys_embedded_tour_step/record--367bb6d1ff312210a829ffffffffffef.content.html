Process overview

Dive into the heart of your project’s process with the Project Metrics, a section dedicated to showing your process time efficiency and bottlenecks. ​

Time Series Graph​
Watch the evolution of your process efficiency over time with a trendline graph that displays the average time taken from the first event until the last project record. This visualization helps you identify patterns, trends, and anomalies in your process performance, enabling you to dive deeper into certain time areas.​

Histogram​
Gain a deeper understanding of your process efficiency with a histogram that categorizes records by the time taken to reach the process end. This graph offers a visual representation of how your records are distributed across different time frames, highlighting potential areas for improvement in speeding up process completion.​
