VisualizationDescription
Pie visualizationsPie charts are best when comparing 5-7 segments that total 100%, when no two segments have a value within 10% of each other.
Donut visualizationsDonut charts are best for comparing no more than 5 segments that total 100%, when no two segments have a value within 10% of each other.
Semi-donut visualizationsSemi-donut charts are best for comparing no more than 4 segments that total 100%, when no two segments have a value within 10% of each other.
