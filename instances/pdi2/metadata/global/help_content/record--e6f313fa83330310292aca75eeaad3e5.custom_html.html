FieldDescription
PersonType of the person involved in the event (incident or observation).
  • Employee: An employee of the organization where the event happened.
  • Visitor: A person from outside the organization who came to the site as a visitor.
  • Public: A person from public who was present at the site.
Note: The Visitor option appears only when the Workplace Visitor Management (com.sn_wsd_visitor) application is installed on your instance.
EmployeeName of the employee involved. This field appears only when Employee is selected from Person type.
VisitorEmail id of the visitor involved. This field appears only when Visitor is selected from Person type.
AssociationType of involvement of the person.
  • Witness: A person who was present at the event described.
  • Injured party: A person who got injured. This option appears only for the Employee and Visitor person type.

    You can add only one record with Association set as Injured party.

  • Safety team: A person from safety team handling this event record.
  • Collaborator: A person who's collaborating in any way in the investigation.
  • Involved: A person who's involved in any other way with this event.
