ToDo this
Create a regular work shift
  1. Click Work shift.
  2. Fill in the fields on the form.
  3. Click Save.
Create an on-call shift
  1. Click On-call shift.
  2. Fill in the fields on the form.
    Note: In the Group field, select the group that you want to assign for the shift.
  3. Click Save.
  4. Add a rotation to the shift.
    1. Click Create Rotation.
    2. Fill in the fields on the form.
    3. Click Save.
