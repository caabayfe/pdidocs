OptionDescription
From any Problem list
  1. Navigate to All > Workspace Experience > Workspaces > Service Operations Workspace.
  2. Click the list icon (List icon).
  3. Select any of the following options from a list of problems.
    • Assigned to me
    • Unassigned
    • Open
    • Resolved
    • Risk Accepted
    • Known Errors
    • All
  4. Click New.
From an incident
  1. Open an incident.
  2. From the record page, click Create problem.
From an interaction
  1. Open an interaction.
  2. From the record page, click Create problem.
