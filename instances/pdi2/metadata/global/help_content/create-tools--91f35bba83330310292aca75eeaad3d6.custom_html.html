ToolDescription
Create an Application ServiceOpen the Application Service wizard to create application services. Application services provide the foundation for operation of many ServiceNow AI Platform products such as ServiceNow AI Platform, ITOM Optimization, and ITSM Software Asset Management. When you use application services, you adhere to CSDM standards and standardize the organization, maintenance, and monitoring of services in your organization.
New Business ApplicationUse the Business Application form to add the applications that your organization uses based on their functions and the business process they fulfill.

A business application is software used by business users to perform a business function. In Application Portfolio Management (APM), add any business application that is used to assess and track costs, usage, business value, functional fitment, and risks.

New Technical ServiceTechnical services are associated with service owners and are typically layered under one or more business or application services.

You can use technical services to view and manage the technology that you provide to the business. If you're using Event Management, you can monitor service performance and identify health issues for related infrastructure CIs and application services.

Additional role requirement: service_admin.
