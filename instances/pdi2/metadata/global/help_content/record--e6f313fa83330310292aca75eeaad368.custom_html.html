TabsDescription
Active SessionsActive sessions are the session in which the you are still exploring the ServiceNow instance with different browsers or devices.

The Active Sessions tab in the Identity Center helps you to identity your sessions based on the Browser, IP Address, and Sessions ID. Using this information you can take required actions such as extending or terminating the session.

Login HistoryThe Login History tab in the Identity Center helps you with your login information and the status of your login.
Registered Mobile DevicesThe Registered Mobile Devices tab in the Identity Center displays the details about your registered devices that were used to access the ServiceNow instance.
