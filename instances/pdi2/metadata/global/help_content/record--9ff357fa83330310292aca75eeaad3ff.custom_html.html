To generate a schedule previewDo this
From the team calendar tab
  1. Click the Calendar tab.
  2. Click the Show Schedules icon to display the Schedules panel.
From the Schedule tab
  1. Click the Schedule tab.
  2. Click Generate Preview. The schedule is queued for processing and generates the schedule preview.
  3. Click the Preview tab.
  4. Review the schedule plan and make updates as necessary.
