ActionDescription
+ Create newOpens new tab for creating a new visualization. No specific role required.
DuplicateCopies current visualization into a new tab. Window opens for you to name the duplicate. This duplicate is saved automatically when it is created. No specific role required.
ShareShares the visualization with other users, with different permissions. For details, see the "Share a visualization" topic.
Export

Exports the data visualization as a PDF, PNG, or JPEG file, or embeds a PNG in an email. Choice of sizes and orientation. Can download the export or email to other users on the instance, if your role permits. Not all visualization types can be exported.

ScheduleSchedule a regular email of the exports of data visualizations to multiple recipients. You can schedule several exports to be sent together. You otherwise have the same exporting options as with the Export action.
Bookmark/Remove bookmarkAdd or remove your personal bookmark to/from this visualization. No role required.
DeleteDelete the current visualization from the instance. Deletion cannot be undone. No validation is executed to check if the visualization is used in a dashboard.

You can delete any visualization that you have created. Users with the admin or viz_admin role can delete any visualization.
