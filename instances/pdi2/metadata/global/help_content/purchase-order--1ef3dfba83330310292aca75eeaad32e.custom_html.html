FieldDescription
NumberUnique number identifying the purchase order.
PO dateDate on which the purchase order was created.
Due byDate by which the total cost of the purchase order must be paid.
StatusThe current status of the purchase order.
VendorSupplier to which the purchase order was issued.
Assigned toThe user to whom the purchase order is assigned.
Ship toThe stockroom to which items on the purchase order should be shipped.
Bill toThe location responsible for paying the total cost of the purchase order.
Short descriptionA brief description of the purchase order.
General
ShippingThe delivery method to be used when shipping the items on the purchase order.
Ship rateThe amount that must be paid for the delivery method specified in the Shipping field.
TermsThe purchase order payment terms such as Credit, Net 30 days, or Net 90 days.
Total costThe sum of all item costs on the purchase order and the shipping costs.
DescriptionA full description of the purchase order contents.
Details
Initial requestThe record number of the request that requires the items on the purchase order.
ContractThe record number of the contract with the vendor from which the items on the purchase order are ordered.
Requested byThe user requesting the items on the purchase order.
DepartmentThe department responsible for paying for the purchase order.
RequestedThe date the user in the Requested by field requested the items on the purchase order.
Budget numberThe budget number associated with the purchase order.
OrderedThe date and time the Order button is clicked on the Purchase Order form.
Vendor accountThe vendor account associated with the purchase order.
Expected deliveryThe date the items associated with the purchase order are expected to arrive in the stockroom identified in the Ship to field. This field cannot be edited when the status of the purchase order is Received or Canceled.
ReceivedThe date and time at which the purchase order status changed to Received.
