To analyze call transcripts and sentimentsDo this
From Channels
Note: You can view the call transcripts and sentiments for all interactions that were closed today.
  1. Click the Channels icon Channels icon.
  2. Click Amazon Connect (Phone).
  3. From the All Interaction tab, select an interaction record.
From Teams
  1. Click the Teams icon Teams icon.
  2. Select a team.
  3. Select a user.
  4. Click the Interactions tab.
  5. Select an interaction record.
From Coaching
Note: When you have assessments triggered from calls, you can view call transcripts and sentiments for those interactions.
  1. Click the Coaching icon Coaching icon.
  2. Click the Assessment tab.
  3. Select an interaction record.
