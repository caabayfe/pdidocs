ToolDescription
Dependency ViewA visual infrastructure view for a CI and the application or business services that it is part of and that it supports.

In a Dependency Views map, you can see indicators of the status of CIs on the map and you can access CIs related alerts, incidents, problems, changes, and services. If Service Mapping is activated, Dependency Views maps are enhanced to display dependencies that reflect connections in service maps.

Additional role requirement: dependency_views.

Query BuilderA visual tool that enables you to build complex infrastructure and service queries that span multiple CMDB classes, non-CMDB tables, and that involve many CIs that are connected by different relationships.

Additional role requirement: cmdb_query_builder_read.
